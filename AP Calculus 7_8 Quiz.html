Newton's Law of Cooling gives y(t)=y₀e^(kt), where y=T-A and k<0. If y₀=100 and k=-0.1, what happens to y (and therefore T) as t→∞?

B. As t→∞, e^(kt)→0 for k<0, so y→0, meaning the object's temperature approaches ambient.

Free Response
(a) A bacteria culture grows according to dP/dt=0.06P, P(0)=400. Find P(t), then find P(10).
Sample response: P(t)=400e^(0.06t). P(10)=400e^0.6≈400(1.822)≈728.8.
(b) A radioactive substance decays according to dA/dt=-0.02A, A(0)=150. Find A(t), then find how much remains after 25 years.
Sample response: A(t)=150e^(-0.02t). A(25)=150e^(-0.5)≈150(0.6065)≈91 grams.
(c) MODEL: A hot cup of tea at 200°F is placed in a 68°F room, cooling with k=-0.04. Let y=T-68. Find y(t), then find the tea's actual temperature after 10 minutes.
Sample response: y(0)=200-68=132. y(t)=132e^(-0.04t). y(10)=132e^(-0.4)≈132(0.6703)≈88.5. T(10)=88.5+68≈156.5°F.
