Hard

Historians argue that the printing press was one of the most historically significant technological developments of the Renaissance period because of its cascading effects on religious, intellectual, and political life across Europe. Which statement best explains why the printing press had such far-reaching historical consequences?

B. By dramatically increasing the speed, volume, and affordability of text production, the printing press accelerated the spread of humanist ideas and eventually religious reform movements.

Short Answer Practice

5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Identify ONE effect the printing press had on the production or spread of written texts in Europe.

Sample response: The printing press significantly increased the speed and reduced the cost of producing written texts, greatly accelerating the spread of ideas across Europe.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that the printing press contributed to the growth of vernacular literature.

Sample response: The printing press made it more efficient and profitable to produce texts in vernacular languages, expanding access to literature and written ideas beyond the small population fluent in Latin.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that the printing press challenged the traditional authority of the Catholic Church or universities over knowledge.

Sample response: By reducing the Church's and universities' previous near-monopoly over the production and distribution of written knowledge, the printing press allowed new and sometimes critical ideas to spread more widely and independently of these traditional institutions.
