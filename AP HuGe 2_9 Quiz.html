A country with a rapidly growing percentage of elderly citizens relative to its working-age population is said to have a(n):

B. An aging population refers to a growing share of elderly citizens relative to the working-age population.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

An aging population is most likely to place increased strain on which of the following?

B. A growing elderly population typically increases demand for healthcare and pension systems.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

As a country's elderly dependency ratio rises while its working-age population shrinks, which economic challenge is the country most likely to face?

B. A rising elderly dependency ratio combined with a shrinking workforce means fewer workers must financially support a growing retired population.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Japan has one of the world's oldest population structures, with a shrinking workforce and rising elderly dependency ratio. Which combination of policy responses would most directly address the economic challenges posed by this demographic trend?

B. Addressing a shrinking workforce and aging population typically requires strategies that expand the labor force and/or raise birth rates, such as pro-natalist incentives, a higher retirement age, and increased immigration or labor participation.

Country T's population pyramid has shifted over 40 years from an expansive shape to a constrictive shape, and its elderly dependency ratio has more than doubled.

AExplain what this shift in population pyramid shape suggests about Country T's birth rates over this period.

Sample response: The shift from an expansive to a constrictive shape suggests that Country T's birth rates have declined significantly over this 40-year period.

BExplain one specific economic challenge Country T's government is likely facing due to its rising elderly dependency ratio.

Sample response: Country T likely faces growing strain on its pension system, as a shrinking working-age population must generate enough tax revenue to support a much larger retired population.

CExplain one policy option Country T could pursue to address a shrinking working-age population.

Sample response: Country T could raise its official retirement age or expand immigration policies to attract working-age immigrants, both of which would help offset the shrinking domestic workforce.
