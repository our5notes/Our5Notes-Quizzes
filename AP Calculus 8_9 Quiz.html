For y=x² revolved around the x-axis from 0 to 2, the volume integral is:

B. R(x)=x², so [R(x)]²=x⁴, giving π∫x⁴dx.

A region bounded by x=y², x=0, y=3 is revolved around the y-axis. Which volume formula correctly applies, and why is y (not x) the variable of integration?

A. When revolving around the y-axis, radius (x=y²) and the integration variable must both be in terms of y.

The region under y=√x, from x=0 to x=4, is revolved around the x-axis (shown above).

(a) Set up and evaluate the volume using the disc method.
Sample response: V = π∫(0 to 4)(√x)²dx = π∫(0 to 4) x dx = π(8) = 8π cubic units.
(b) Set up and evaluate the volume of the region bounded by y=x², y=0, x=2, revolved around the x-axis.
Sample response: V = π∫(0 to 2)(x²)²dx = π∫(0 to 2)x⁴dx = π(32/5) = 32π/5 cubic units.
(c) Set up and evaluate the volume of the region bounded by x=y², x=0, y=3, revolved around the y-axis.
Sample response: V = π∫(0 to 3)(y²)²dy = π∫(0 to 3)y⁴dy = π(243/5) = 243π/5 cubic units.
