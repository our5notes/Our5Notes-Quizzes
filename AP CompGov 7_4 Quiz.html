Sociologists note that Nigeria's ethnic, religious, and regional cleavages frequently overlap, since the largely Muslim Hausa-Fulani are concentrated in the north while the largely Christian Igbo are concentrated in the oil-rich south. Which statement best explains the significance of this overlap for understanding Nigerian social divisions?

B. Because these cleavages frequently reinforce rather than cut across one another, they can deepen rather than soften underlying social divisions.

Short Answer

Explain ONE reason why being a rentier state can make a government less accountable to its citizens.

Sample response: Because a rentier state can fund itself through resource exports rather than taxing its population, it has less need to be responsive to citizens' demands in exchange for their tax contributions.

Short Answer

Explain ONE grievance behind oil protests in the Niger Delta, beyond simply foreign ownership.

Sample response: Protesters have also cited the failure of oil wealth to benefit local communities and environmental pollution caused by extraction as major grievances driving unrest in the Niger Delta.

Short Answer

Explain why Boko Haram is described as lacking popular support while still symbolizing broader discontent in Nigeria.

Sample response: Even though most Nigerians reject Boko Haram's violent tactics and extremist ideology, the group's emergence reflects genuine underlying frustrations, such as regional inequality and government neglect in the north, that resonate with broader discontent.
