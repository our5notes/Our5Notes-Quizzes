Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify what was traded from Europe to West Africa.
Sample response: Manufactured goods, shown in the diagram, were traded from Europe to West Africa.
(b) Explain the purpose of the Navigation Acts within the broader system of mercantilism.
Sample response: The Navigation Acts were designed to ensure that the economic benefits of colonial trade flowed primarily to Britain, by requiring that colonial goods be shipped on English vessels and that many goods pass through England before being sold elsewhere; this enforced the mercantilist goal of using colonies to enrich the mother country, rather than allowing colonies to trade freely and independently with other nations.
(c) Explain how the transatlantic trade system connected the economic fortunes of the colonies, Europe, and West Africa.
Sample response: The triangular trade created an interdependent economic system: European manufactured goods were traded for enslaved Africans, whose forced labor in the colonies produced raw materials like sugar and tobacco, which were then shipped back to Europe to be processed and sold, generating profit that funded further trade — each leg of the triangle depended on and reinforced the others, tying the economic fates of all three regions together.
