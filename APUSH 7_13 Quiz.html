Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the June 1944 invasion that opened a second front against Germany.
Sample response: D-Day, shown in the timeline, opened a second front against Germany in June 1944.
(b) Explain the reasoning President Truman used to justify using atomic bombs against Japan.
Sample response: Truman and his advisors argued that a full-scale invasion of Japan's home islands would likely cost hundreds of thousands of American (and Japanese) casualties, based on the extremely fierce Japanese resistance seen in earlier Pacific battles; they believed that using the atomic bomb, while causing devastating civilian casualties, would force a quicker Japanese surrender and ultimately save more lives than a prolonged conventional invasion would have cost, though this decision remains a subject of significant historical debate.
(c) Explain the strategic significance of D-Day for the broader war effort against Germany.
Sample response: D-Day established a crucial Western front against Germany in France, forcing Germany to fight a two-front war against both Western Allied forces advancing from France and Soviet forces advancing from the East; this combined pressure from both directions significantly accelerated Germany's military collapse, contributing directly to Germany's surrender less than a year later in May 1945.
