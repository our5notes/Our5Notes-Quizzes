Historians note that international and regional institutions such as the United Nations, the European Union, and ASEAN developed during this period to help manage increasing global interconnectedness, providing frameworks for diplomatic cooperation, economic integration, and conflict resolution among an increasingly interdependent world of nation-states. Which statement best reflects the broader significance of the development of these institutions?

B. The development of these institutions reflected a broader effort to create structured frameworks for cooperation among nation-states navigating an increasingly interconnected world.
