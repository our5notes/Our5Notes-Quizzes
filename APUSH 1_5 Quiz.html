Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the group positioned at the bottom of the casta system.
Sample response: Native peoples and enslaved Africans, shown at the bottom of the diagram, occupied the lowest position in the casta system.
(b) Explain why the encomienda system was eventually replaced, at least in part, by the repartimiento system.
Sample response: The encomienda system led to widespread abuse and exploitation of Native laborers, contributing to the already severe population decline from disease; as reports of these abuses reached Spain and the shrinking Native population made the system less viable, Spain moved toward the repartimiento system, which nominally limited (though did not eliminate) the amount of forced labor that could be demanded.
(c) Explain how the casta system reflected and reinforced the labor and social structures of Spanish colonial society.
Sample response: The casta system assigned social status and legal privileges based on racial ancestry, placing Spanish-born peninsulares at the top and enslaved Africans and Native peoples at the bottom; this hierarchy justified and reinforced the unequal labor systems of the colonies, since those at the bottom of the caste system were also those most subject to forced labor, linking racial ideology directly to economic exploitation.
