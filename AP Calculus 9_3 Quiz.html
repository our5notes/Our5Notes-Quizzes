(a) Find the arc length of x(t)=t², y(t)=t³ from t=0 to t=2. Show the setup and u-substitution.
Sample response: L=∫(0 to 2)t√(4+9t²)dt. Let u=4+9t², du=18t dt. L=(1/27)(4+9t²)^(3/2) from 0 to 2 = (1/27)[40^(3/2)-8] ≈ 9.07.
(b) Find the arc length of the circle x(t)=5cos(t), y(t)=5sin(t) from t=0 to t=2π.
Sample response: dx/dt=-5sin(t), dy/dt=5cos(t). L=∫(0 to 2π)5 dt = 10π.
(c) Confirm your answer to (b) matches the known circumference formula for a circle of radius 5.
Sample response: Circumference = 2πr = 2π(5) = 10π, which matches the arc length computed. ✓
