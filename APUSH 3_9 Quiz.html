Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the three branches of government established by the Constitution.
Sample response: The legislative, executive, and judicial branches, shown in the diagram, were established by the Constitution.
(b) Explain the purpose of dividing government power into three separate branches.
Sample response: Dividing government power into three separate branches — legislative, executive, and judicial — prevents any single individual or group from accumulating too much power, echoing the framers' fear of tyranny after their experience with British rule; by giving each branch a distinct role and the ability to check the others, the Constitution aims to protect liberty by keeping power balanced and accountable.
(c) Explain how federalism attempts to balance the need for a strong national government with concerns about excessive centralized power.
Sample response: Federalism divides power between the national government (which handles matters like national defense and interstate commerce) and state governments (which retain authority over many local matters), attempting to create a national government strong enough to function effectively — unlike under the Articles of Confederation — while still preserving significant state authority and addressing Anti-Federalist fears of an overly powerful central government.
