Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1794 uprising that tested federal authority.
Sample response: The Whiskey Rebellion, shown in the timeline, tested federal authority in 1794.
(b) Explain the significance of Washington's response to the Whiskey Rebellion.
Sample response: Washington personally led federal troops to suppress the Whiskey Rebellion, demonstrating that, unlike the government under the Articles of Confederation, the new federal government under the Constitution had both the authority and the practical capacity to enforce its laws; this firm response set an important precedent for federal power while also raising concerns among some about the government using military force against its own citizens.
(c) Explain how Hamilton's financial plan contributed to the emergence of the first political parties.
Sample response: Hamilton's financial plan, including federal assumption of state debts and the creation of a national bank, was controversial because it favored commercial and financial interests and stretched the federal government's constitutional powers; supporters of Hamilton's vision coalesced into the Federalist party, while opponents like Jefferson and Madison, who favored a more limited federal government and agrarian interests, formed the opposing Democratic-Republican party.
