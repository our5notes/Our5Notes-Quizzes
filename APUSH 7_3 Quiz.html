Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the treaty that ended the Spanish-American War.
Sample response: The Treaty of Paris, shown in the timeline, ended the Spanish-American War in 1898.
(b) Explain the role of yellow journalism in building support for the Spanish-American War.
Sample response: Newspapers engaged in “yellow journalism” published sensationalized, often exaggerated or fabricated stories about Spanish atrocities in Cuba, stirring American public outrage and sympathy for Cuban independence; this media coverage, combined with the sinking of the USS Maine, helped build enough public pressure that political leaders felt compelled to declare war, even though later investigations raised doubts about Spain's actual responsibility for the Maine's explosion.
(c) Explain the irony of the Philippine-American War following the Spanish-American War.
Sample response: The U.S. had partly justified war with Spain by expressing sympathy for the independence movements of Spanish colonies like Cuba and the Philippines, yet after defeating Spain, the U.S. chose to retain control over the Philippines rather than grant it independence; this led Filipino nationalists, who had fought against Spanish rule expecting independence, to instead fight a new, brutal war against American colonial rule, undermining the anti-colonial rhetoric that had helped justify the original war.
