Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which direction of the cycle shown represents ATP hydrolysis.
Sample response: The direction of the cycle moving from ATP to ADP plus phosphate represents ATP hydrolysis.
(b) Explain why ATP hydrolysis is described as exergonic.
Sample response: ATP hydrolysis is exergonic because breaking the bond to the terminal phosphate group releases more energy than it takes to break that bond, resulting in a net release of energy that can power cellular work.
(c) Explain the concept of energy coupling, using the ATP/ADP cycle as an example.
Sample response: Energy coupling pairs the exergonic hydrolysis of ATP, which releases energy, with an endergonic process that requires energy, such as active transport or biosynthesis, so that the energy released by ATP hydrolysis powers the energy-requiring process, allowing it to proceed.
