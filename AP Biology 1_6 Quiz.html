BRibose has an extra hydroxyl group that makes RNA more reactive
CRNA nucleotides lack a phosphate group present in DNA

Free Response FRQ
(a) List the three components of a nucleotide.
Sample response: A nucleotide consists of a nitrogenous base, a five-carbon (pentose) sugar, and a phosphate group.
(b) Given the DNA strand 5'-GCTACG-3', write its complementary strand in 5' to 3' notation.
Sample response: The complementary strand, written 5' to 3', is CGTAGC.
(c) Explain why RNA is typically single-stranded while DNA is typically double-stranded, connecting your answer to their different biological roles.
Sample response: DNA's double-stranded structure provides a stable, protected way to store genetic information long-term, since each strand can serve as a backup or template for repair; RNA's single-stranded structure makes it more flexible and disposable, well-suited for its typical role as a temporary working copy of genetic instructions used during protein synthesis.
