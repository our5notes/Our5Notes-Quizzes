Question 1 Easy

What modern example of natural selection occurs when bacteria survive antibiotic exposure?

AGenetic drift process
BAntibiotic resistance
CFounder effect process
DBottleneck effect process
Antibiotic resistance is a well-documented modern example of natural selection.
Question 2 Medium

Using the diagram shown, when do resistance-conferring mutations in bacteria typically first arise?

AOnly after antibiotic exposure begins
BBefore antibiotic exposure occurs
COnly during bacterial reproduction alone
DOnly when bacteria are under stress
Resistance mutations arise randomly before antibiotic exposure, not because of it.
Question 3 Medium

What role does an antibiotic play in the evolution of bacterial resistance?

AIt directly causes new resistance mutations to form
BIt acts as a selective pressure favoring resistant bacteria
CIt has no measurable effect on bacterial populations
DIt removes resistance genes from surviving bacteria
The antibiotic acts as a selective pressure, favoring bacteria that already carry resistance.
Question 4 Hard

Using the diagram shown, explain why overuse of antibiotics is a public health concern from an evolutionary perspective.

AAntibiotics permanently damage bacterial DNA in all cases
BFrequent use increases selection favoring resistant bacteria
CAntibiotics eliminate the need for natural selection entirely
DOveruse causes bacteria to stop reproducing altogether
Frequent antibiotic use increases selective pressure favoring resistant bacteria, spreading resistance.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify what happens to non-resistant bacteria when an antibiotic is applied, based on the diagram shown.
Sample response: Non-resistant bacteria are killed when the antibiotic is applied, as shown in the diagram.
(b) Explain why antibiotics do not directly “cause” bacteria to become resistant.
Sample response: Resistance mutations arise randomly through normal mutation processes, independent of antibiotic exposure, and are already present in a small fraction of the population before the antibiotic is introduced; the antibiotic acts as a selective pressure, not a cause of the mutation itself.
(c) Explain why overuse of antibiotics is a public health concern, connecting your answer to natural selection.
Sample response: Every use of antibiotics imposes selective pressure favoring resistant bacteria; frequent or unnecessary use increases opportunities for this selection, accelerating the evolution and spread of resistant bacterial populations.
