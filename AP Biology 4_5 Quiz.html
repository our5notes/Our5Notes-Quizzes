Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which phase of the cell cycle, shown in the diagram, is when the cell physically divides.
Sample response: The M phase, shown in the diagram, is when the cell physically divides.
(b) Explain what occurs during S phase, and why it must happen before mitosis.
Sample response: During S phase, the cell replicates its DNA, producing two identical copies of each chromosome; this must happen before mitosis because mitosis separates these copies into two daughter cells, so without replication first, each daughter cell would not receive a complete set of chromosomes.
(c) Explain why most cells spend the majority of their time in interphase rather than in mitosis.
Sample response: Interphase is when the cell carries out most of its normal metabolic activities, grows, and replicates its DNA, while mitosis is a relatively brief process focused solely on separating already-duplicated chromosomes, so cells spend most of their time in interphase performing ongoing cellular functions.
