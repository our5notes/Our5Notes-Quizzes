Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the battle in 1781 that effectively ended the war.
Sample response: The Battle of Yorktown, shown in the timeline, effectively ended the war in 1781.
(b) Explain why the Battle of Saratoga is considered a turning point in the Revolutionary War.
Sample response: The American victory at Saratoga convinced France that the colonists had a genuine chance of defeating Britain, leading France to formally ally with the American cause and provide crucial military and financial support; this foreign assistance, which followed directly from Saratoga, would prove essential to the eventual American victory.
(c) Explain how the Patriot cause succeeded despite Britain's significant military and financial advantages.
Sample response: Despite Britain's larger, more professional military and greater financial resources, the Patriot cause succeeded through a combination of factors: the resilience and ideological commitment of colonial militias and the Continental Army, George Washington's steady leadership, effective use of guerrilla-style tactics and knowledge of local terrain, and, crucially, financial and military assistance from European allies like France after Saratoga.
