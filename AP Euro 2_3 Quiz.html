Short Answer

Explain ONE way that Calvinism's doctrine of predestination differed from Catholic teachings on salvation.

Sample response: Calvinism's doctrine of predestination held that God had already predetermined who would be saved regardless of individual actions, differing from Catholic teachings that emphasized the role of faith combined with good works and Church sacraments in achieving salvation.

Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that Anabaptists' beliefs about baptism differed from those of most other Christian traditions during this period.

Sample response: Anabaptists believed that only adult believers who could consciously profess their faith should be baptized, rejecting the practice of infant baptism accepted by both the Catholic Church and most other Protestant traditions.
