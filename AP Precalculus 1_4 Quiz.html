(c) A different polynomial has degree 4. What is the maximum number of turning points it could have, and could it have exactly 1 turning point instead? Explain.
Sample response: Maximum of 4−1=3 turning points. Yes, it could have exactly 1 turning point instead, since "at most" sets an upper limit, not a required exact count — a degree-4 polynomial like x⁴ has only 1 turning point.
