Easy

Which contemporary technology has most dramatically increased the speed and reach of cultural diffusion in the twenty-first century?

B. The internet and social media allow cultural traits to spread globally and nearly instantaneously, far faster than earlier technologies.

Medium

A multinational fast-food or clothing brand opening identical stores in dozens of countries around the world is an example of cultural diffusion driven primarily by:

B. Transnational corporations expanding globally are a major contemporary driver of cultural diffusion, spreading brands, products, and practices worldwide.

Medium

International tourism contributes to contemporary cultural diffusion primarily by:

B. Tourism creates direct contact between visitors and host communities, facilitating the exchange of cultural practices in both directions.

Hard

A dance trend originates in one country, is filmed and posted on a global social media platform, and within days is being recreated by users in dozens of countries with no direct connection to the original creator or to each other. Which factor most directly explains why this diffusion occurred so much faster than a similar cultural trend might have spread a century ago?

B. Digital communication technology, such as global social media platforms, allows contagious diffusion to occur at a speed and global scale that was previously impossible.

Free Response
A cooking technique shared by a home cook on a video-sharing app quickly gains popularity worldwide, with people in dozens of countries recreating and adapting the technique within their own local cuisines within just a few weeks.

AIdentify the primary contemporary cause of diffusion illustrated in this scenario.

Sample response: The primary cause is digital communication technology, specifically social media and video-sharing platforms, which allowed the technique to spread rapidly to a global audience.

BExplain how this example of diffusion differs from historical diffusion patterns, such as the spread of ideas along the Silk Road, in terms of speed and reach.

Sample response: While historical diffusion along routes like the Silk Road took months, years, or even centuries to spread ideas across long distances, this cooking technique spread to a global audience within just a few weeks due to instantaneous digital communication.

CExplain one potential effect (positive or negative) this kind of rapid contemporary diffusion could have on local or traditional culinary traditions.

Sample response: Rapid diffusion could positively expose local culinary traditions to a much wider global audience, but it could also risk oversimplifying or commercializing traditional techniques as they are adapted quickly by people unfamiliar with their original cultural context.
