Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the site of Lee's surrender in April 1865.
Sample response: Appomattox Court House, shown in the timeline, is where Lee surrendered in April 1865.
(b) Explain why the Battle of Gettysburg is considered a major turning point in the Civil War.
Sample response: The Union victory at Gettysburg decisively repelled General Lee's second and final attempt to invade the North, inflicting devastating Confederate casualties that the South's smaller population could not easily replace; combined with the Union capture of Vicksburg around the same time (splitting the Confederacy along the Mississippi River), Gettysburg shifted overall momentum decisively toward the Union for the remainder of the war.
(c) Explain how the Union's greater industrial and population resources contributed to its eventual victory.
Sample response: The Union's much larger population and far greater industrial capacity allowed it to sustain a longer war of attrition, continuously replacing soldiers and producing weapons, supplies, and railroads at a scale the Confederacy simply could not match over time; while the Confederacy sometimes won individual early battles through strong leadership and defensive advantages, the Union's material advantages ultimately allowed it to outlast Confederate resistance.
