Free Response FRQ

Refer to the graph shown above.

(a) Identify which growth model shown assumes unlimited resources.
Sample response: The exponential growth model, shown as the J-shaped curve, assumes unlimited resources.
(b) Explain why exponential growth is not sustainable in the long term for most real populations.
Sample response: Exponential growth assumes no limiting factors, but real resources like food and space are always finite, so as a population grows, resources become scarce, eventually slowing growth as the population approaches carrying capacity.
(c) Explain what happens to a population's growth rate as it approaches carrying capacity, and why.
Sample response: As a population approaches carrying capacity, its growth rate slows and approaches zero, because resources become more limited relative to the number of individuals, increasing competition and reducing reproductive success until births and deaths roughly balance.
