Free Response
(a) Identify the center and radius of the circle x²+y²−6x+4y−3=0. (Hint: complete the square for both x and y.)
Sample response: Complete the square: (x²−6x+9)+(y²+4y+4)=3+9+4 → (x−3)²+(y+2)²=16. Center (3,−2), radius 4.
(b) Identify the type of conic section represented by 9x²−4y²=36, and rewrite it in standard form.
Sample response: Hyperbola. Dividing by 36: x²/4−y²/9=1.
(c) For the ellipse x²/16+y²/25=1, identify which axis (x or y) is the major axis, and explain how you know.
Sample response: The y-axis is the major axis, since the denominator under y² (25) is larger than under x² (16) — the larger denominator corresponds to the direction of the major axis.
