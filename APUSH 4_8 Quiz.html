Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1830 law that authorized forced relocation of Native nations.
Sample response: The Indian Removal Act, shown in the timeline, authorized forced relocation of Native nations in 1830.
(b) Explain Jackson's response to the Nullification Crisis and its significance for federal authority.
Sample response: When South Carolina claimed the right to nullify a federal tariff law it opposed, Jackson firmly rejected this claim, asserting that no state had the constitutional authority to unilaterally reject federal law, and he threatened military force to enforce federal authority; this response established an important precedent affirming the supremacy of federal law over individual state objections, even though Jackson personally sympathized with some of South Carolina's tariff concerns.
(c) Explain the contradiction between Jackson's image as a champion of democracy and his policy of Indian Removal.
Sample response: While Jackson expanded political participation and framed himself as a champion of the “common man” and democracy, his Indian Removal policy forcibly displaced tens of thousands of Native people from their ancestral homelands, causing immense suffering and death (as in the Trail of Tears); this reveals that Jacksonian “democracy” was fundamentally limited to white citizens, and did not extend its democratic ideals to Native peoples, whose sovereignty and rights Jackson's policies actively undermined.
