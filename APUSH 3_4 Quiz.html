Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the Enlightenment philosopher whose ideas about natural rights influenced the Declaration.
Sample response: John Locke, shown in the diagram, is the philosopher whose ideas about natural rights influenced the Declaration.
(b) Explain how Locke's concept of natural rights is reflected in the Declaration of Independence.
Sample response: The Declaration of Independence directly echoes Locke by asserting that all people are endowed with unalienable rights, adapting Locke's “life, liberty, and property” into “life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness”; this framing provided the philosophical justification for declaring independence, since the Declaration argues that the British government had failed to protect these natural rights.
(c) Explain the significance of social contract theory for justifying revolution against Britain.
Sample response: Social contract theory held that government exists only with the consent of the governed and for the purpose of protecting their natural rights; by arguing that King George III had repeatedly violated this contract, the Declaration of Independence used social contract theory to justify the colonists' right to dissolve their political ties to Britain and establish a new government.
