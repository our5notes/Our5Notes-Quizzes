A country with deep linguistic and religious divisions between regions, leading to internal political conflict and calls for separation, is experiencing the effects of:

Which of the following best illustrates a centripetal force?

A. A shared national holiday and flag that unite citizens across ethnic lines is a classic example of a centripetal force.

A multiethnic country has historically used a shared national language and a unifying national mythology to promote unity (centripetal forces), but rising economic inequality between regions has recently fueled separatist sentiment in its most resource-rich province. Which statement best explains this situation using the concepts of centripetal and centrifugal forces?

B. Centripetal and centrifugal forces can coexist; a shared national language may continue to unify the country even as an unrelated factor, like economic inequality, introduces new centrifugal pressure.

Free Response
A country has long promoted a shared national language and a unifying national holiday to build a strong sense of common identity among its diverse population, but recently, a region with a distinct ethnic and linguistic minority has begun pushing for greater autonomy, citing unequal treatment and neglect of its local language in schools.

AIdentify one centripetal force present in this scenario and explain how it functions to unify the country.

Sample response: The shared national language and national holiday function as centripetal forces, giving citizens across different regions and backgrounds a common identity and shared experience that unifies the country.

BIdentify one centrifugal force present in this scenario and explain how it threatens national unity.

Sample response: The perceived neglect of the minority region's local language in schools functions as a centrifugal force, fueling resentment and separatist sentiment that threatens to divide the country.

CExplain how the same cultural trait, such as language policy, could function as either a centripetal or centrifugal force depending on how it is implemented.

Sample response: A language policy that respects and includes minority languages alongside the national language could serve as a centripetal force by making minority groups feel included, while a policy that suppresses minority languages in favor of only the national language could instead act as a centrifugal force by alienating those groups.
