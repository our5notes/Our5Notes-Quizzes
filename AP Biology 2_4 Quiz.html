Free Response FRQ

Refer to the permeability chart shown above.

(a) Identify one type of molecule shown that can cross the membrane freely.
Sample response: Small nonpolar molecules, such as oxygen or carbon dioxide, can cross the membrane freely.
(b) Explain why ions like Na+ and K+ cannot cross the membrane without assistance.
Sample response: Ions carry a full electric charge, which is strongly repelled by the hydrophobic interior of the phospholipid bilayer, so they cannot pass directly through the membrane and instead require specific channel or carrier proteins.
(c) Predict what would happen to a cell's ability to take in glucose if all of its glucose transport proteins were blocked, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: If all glucose transport proteins were blocked, the cell would be unable to take in glucose via facilitated diffusion, since glucose cannot cross the hydrophobic membrane interior on its own; this would likely starve the cell of a key energy source unless an alternative pathway existed.
