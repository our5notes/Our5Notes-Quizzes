Free Response
(a) For x(t)=3t−1, y(t)=t²+2, create a table of values for t=−1,0,1,2.
Sample response: t=-1: (-4,3). t=0: (-1,2). t=1: (2,3). t=2: (5,6).
(b) Eliminate the parameter for x(t)=t−2, y(t)=3t+1 to find a rectangular equation.
Sample response: t=x+2. Substitute: y=3(x+2)+1=3x+7.
(c) Explain why eliminating the parameter can sometimes lose information present in the original parametrization.
Sample response: Eliminating the parameter combines the two equations into one relating x and y directly, but this process can lose the DIRECTION of motion (which way the curve is traced as t increases) or any RESTRICTIONS on t that limited the original curve to only part of the resulting rectangular graph.
