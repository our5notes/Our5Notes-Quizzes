Topic 9.3: Technological Advances: Debates About the Environment After 1900

Mark for Review
Easy

The Green Revolution, which introduced high-yield crop varieties and chemical fertilizers to increase agricultural production during the twentieth century, is associated with which environmental concern?

B. The Green Revolution is associated with environmental costs such as soil degradation and water pollution linked to heavy fertilizer and pesticide use.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

Rising global concern about climate change during the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries has focused significantly on:

B. Rising climate change concern has focused on the effects of industrial and technological development on global temperatures, often linked to fossil fuel use.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

Debates over environmental policy during this period have frequently involved tension between:

B. Environmental policy debates have frequently involved tension between continued economic development and the need to address environmental costs.

Hard

Historians note that the same technological advances that enabled significant economic growth and increased agricultural productivity during the twentieth century, such as the Green Revolution's chemical fertilizers and pesticides or continued industrial fossil fuel use, also generated significant environmental costs, including pollution, soil degradation, and climate change concerns. Which statement best captures this complex relationship between technological advancement and environmental impact?

B. The same technologies that drove significant economic and agricultural growth also frequently generated substantial environmental costs, creating ongoing tension between development and environmental protection.
