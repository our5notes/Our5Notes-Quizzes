Free Response FRQ

Refer to the cell diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the organelle labeled as the site of ribosomal RNA synthesis and ribosome assembly.
Sample response: The nucleolus, located inside the nucleus, is the site of ribosomal RNA synthesis and ribosome assembly.
(b) Explain the functional relationship between the rough ER and the Golgi apparatus shown in the diagram.
Sample response: The rough ER synthesizes and initially folds proteins destined for secretion or the membrane, then packages them into transport vesicles that carry the proteins to the Golgi apparatus, where they are further modified, sorted, and packaged for their final destination.
(c) Predict what would happen to a cell's ability to secrete proteins if its Golgi apparatus were nonfunctional, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: If the Golgi apparatus were nonfunctional, proteins produced by the rough ER would not be properly modified, sorted, or packaged into vesicles for secretion, so the cell would likely be unable to correctly secrete these proteins outside the cell, even though the proteins themselves might still be synthesized normally.
