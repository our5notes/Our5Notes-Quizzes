Use the graph of y = k(x) shown above for all parts of this question.

(a) Classify the discontinuity at x=-3 and state the relevant one-sided limits.
Sample response: Removable discontinuity (a hole). Both one-sided limits equal 2 as x→-3, but the function is undefined exactly at x=-3.
(b) Classify the discontinuity at x=0 and state the relevant one-sided limits and k(0).
Sample response: Jump discontinuity. lim(x→0⁻)k(x)=5 and lim(x→0⁺)k(x)=1; k(0)=1, matching the right-hand piece.
(c) Classify the discontinuity at x=3 and explain what feature of the graph indicates this classification.
Sample response: Infinite discontinuity (vertical asymptote). The graph shows k(x) rising without bound as x→3⁺, indicated by the curve shooting upward near the dashed line x=3.
