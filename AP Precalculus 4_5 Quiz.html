Free Response
(a) Using the graph of x²/9+y²/4=1 shown above, verify that the point (0,−2) satisfies the equation.
Sample response: 0/9 + 4/4 = 0+1 = 1 ✓.
(b) Solve x²+y²=36 explicitly for y in terms of x, and state the domain of each resulting piece.
Sample response: y = ±√(36−x²). Each piece (upper: y=√(36−x²); lower: y=−√(36−x²)) has domain [−6,6].
(c) Explain why the full circle x²+y²=36 is not a function, but each of the two pieces from part (b) IS a function.
Sample response: The full circle fails the vertical line test, since most x-values correspond to two y-values (upper and lower semicircle points). Each individual piece passes the vertical line test, since restricting to just the positive (or just the negative) square root gives exactly one y-value per x-value.
