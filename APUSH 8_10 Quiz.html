Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1963 event where MLK delivered his “I Have a Dream” speech.
Sample response: The March on Washington, shown in the timeline, is where MLK delivered his “I Have a Dream” speech in 1963.
(b) Explain how the sit-in movement used nonviolent direct action to achieve its goals.
Sample response: Beginning with four Black college students in Greensboro, North Carolina, the sit-in movement involved activists nonviolently occupying seats at segregated lunch counters and refusing to leave until served, deliberately inviting arrest and media attention to expose the injustice of segregation; this tactic spread rapidly across the South, generating significant national media coverage and public sympathy that built crucial momentum for broader civil rights legislation.
(c) Explain the specific problem the Voting Rights Act of 1965 was designed to address.
Sample response: Despite the 15th Amendment's constitutional guarantee of voting rights regardless of race, many Southern states had long used discriminatory practices like literacy tests, poll taxes, and outright intimidation to prevent Black citizens from actually registering to vote; the Voting Rights Act specifically banned these discriminatory practices and authorized federal oversight of voter registration in areas with a history of discrimination, finally providing meaningful federal enforcement of the constitutional right that had existed on paper for nearly a century.
