Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which mass extinction event shown is considered the largest in Earth's history.
Sample response: The Permian extinction, shown in the diagram, is considered the largest mass extinction in Earth's history.
(b) Explain why mass extinction events are often followed by adaptive radiation among surviving lineages.
Sample response: A mass extinction eliminates many species, freeing up ecological niches that survivors can rapidly diversify to fill with relatively little competition, resulting in a burst of speciation called adaptive radiation.
(c) Explain why the current elevated rate of species extinction is a significant concern from an evolutionary perspective.
Sample response: Since extinction permanently eliminates a species' unique genetic information, a rapid, large-scale loss of species reduces overall biodiversity and the genetic variation available for future evolution, with consequences that could persist for millions of years.
