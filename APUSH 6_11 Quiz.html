Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1883 law that began requiring merit-based federal hiring.
Sample response: The Pendleton Act, shown in the diagram, began requiring merit-based federal hiring in 1883.
(b) Explain the core argument of the Social Gospel movement.
Sample response: The Social Gospel movement argued that living a genuinely Christian life required actively working to address social problems like poverty, unsafe labor conditions, and urban squalor, rather than focusing solely on individual personal salvation; this reframed social reform as a religious duty, motivating many religiously devout Americans, including ministers and lay reformers, to engage directly in efforts to improve conditions for the urban poor and working class.
(c) Explain why the Pendleton Act was passed in response to a specific political event.
Sample response: The Pendleton Act was passed in 1883 partly in direct response to the 1881 assassination of President James Garfield by a disappointed office-seeker who had been denied a political patronage appointment; this shocking event highlighted the dangers and dysfunction of the patronage system, in which government jobs were awarded based on political loyalty rather than qualification, building political support for civil service reform based on merit instead.
