Topic 2.8 Quiz: Mechanisms of Transport

Endocytosis brings material into the cell; exocytosis releases material out of the cell.
Question 1 Easy

What term describes a cell engulfing material by folding its membrane inward to form a vesicle?

AExocytosis process
BEndocytosis process
CFacilitated diffusion
DSimple diffusion process
Endocytosis is the process of engulfing material by folding the membrane inward to form a vesicle.
Question 2 Medium

What term describes a vesicle fusing with the plasma membrane to release its contents outside the cell?

AEndocytosis process
BPhagocytosis process
CExocytosis process
DPinocytosis process
Exocytosis describes a vesicle fusing with the membrane to release its contents outside the cell.
Question 3 Medium

Using the diagrams shown, both endocytosis and exocytosis require which cellular resource?

AA steady supply of ATP energy
BA large surplus of glucose
CA continuous flow of oxygen only
DA high concentration of water
Both processes require ATP to power the significant membrane remodeling involved.
Question 4 Hard

White blood cells use phagocytosis, a type of endocytosis, to engulf and destroy invading bacteria. What most likely happens to the bacterium immediately after it is engulfed?

AIt is immediately released back outside the cell unchanged
BThe resulting vesicle typically fuses with a lysosome
CIt directly enters the nucleus to be broken down there
DIt dissolves spontaneously in the cell's cytoplasm
The vesicle formed during phagocytosis typically fuses with a lysosome, whose enzymes digest the bacterium.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify which diagram shows material being brought into the cell.
Sample response: The diagram showing the membrane folding inward around material represents endocytosis, which brings material into the cell.
(b) Explain why both endocytosis and exocytosis require ATP.
Sample response: Both processes require significant remodeling and movement of the plasma membrane, which depends on motor proteins and cellular machinery that consume ATP, unlike passive transport processes that don't require energy input.
(c) Describe the process of phagocytosis and explain how it helps protect the body from pathogens.
Sample response: During phagocytosis, a cell's membrane extends around and engulfs a large particle, such as a bacterium, enclosing it within a vesicle; this vesicle typically fuses with a lysosome, whose hydrolytic enzymes break down and destroy the engulfed material, helping protect the body from infection.
