Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the type of cell (host or engulfed) that eventually became the mitochondrion.
Sample response: The engulfed cell shown, originally a free-living aerobic bacterium, eventually became the mitochondrion.
(b) Explain how the double membrane surrounding mitochondria supports endosymbiotic theory.
Sample response: If a host cell engulfed a bacterium through a process similar to endocytosis, the resulting structure would end up surrounded by two membranes: the bacterium's own original membrane, plus a second membrane derived from the host cell during engulfment; mitochondria are indeed surrounded by two membranes, matching this predicted outcome.
(c) Describe one additional piece of evidence, beyond membrane structure, that supports endosymbiotic theory.
Sample response: Mitochondria contain their own circular DNA, similar in structure to bacterial DNA, and their own ribosomes, which more closely resemble prokaryotic ribosomes than eukaryotic ones, both supporting the idea that mitochondria descended from independent prokaryotic organisms.
