Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one pattern of interaction between American Indians and Europeans besides warfare.
Sample response: Trade, shown in the diagram, is one pattern of interaction between American Indians and Europeans besides warfare.
(b) Explain why growing English colonial populations increasingly led to violent conflict with Native peoples over time.
Sample response: As English colonial populations grew rapidly through both immigration and natural increase, colonists required more and more land for farming, pushing further into territory that Native nations depended on for their own subsistence and way of life; this escalating competition for land, rather than any single event, was the underlying driver of increasing violent conflict between colonists and Native peoples as the 17th century progressed.
(c) Explain how the consequences of conflicts like King Philip's War extended beyond the immediate loss of life.
Sample response: Beyond the immediate and devastating loss of life on both sides, conflicts like King Philip's War resulted in the further loss of Native land and political sovereignty, as defeated Native nations were often forced onto smaller territories or lost their independence entirely; these conflicts also hardened colonial attitudes toward Native peoples, making future cooperation and peaceful coexistence increasingly difficult.
