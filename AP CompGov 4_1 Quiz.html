Short Answer

Explain how Confucianism contributed to the legitimacy of China's dynastic rulers for centuries.

Sample response: Confucianism reinforced a strong sense of hierarchy, order, and centralized authority, providing a philosophical basis for accepting the emperor's position at the top of a structured, harmonious society, which helped legitimize dynastic rule for centuries.

Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain the purpose of the "mass line" in Maoist philosophy.

Sample response: The mass line was meant to create ongoing communication between party leaders, party members, and the peasantry, ensuring that policy reflected peasant needs and interests while still being guided by Communist Party leadership.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain how Deng Xiaoping's Four Modernizations represented a shift away from Maoist economic policy while still preserving Communist Party political control.

Sample response: The Four Modernizations opened China's economy to trade, private incentives, and market mechanisms in industry, agriculture, science, and the military, marking a sharp departure from Maoist collectivization, but Deng maintained the Communist Party's exclusive hold on political power.
