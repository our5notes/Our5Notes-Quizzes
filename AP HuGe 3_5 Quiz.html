Which historical trade network is a classic example of diffusion spreading goods, religions, and technologies across Asia, the Middle East, and Europe?

Medium

European colonialism historically spread cultural traits such as language, religion, and legal systems to colonized territories primarily through which mechanism?

B. Colonialism spread cultural traits largely through forced political and economic domination rather than voluntary exchange.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

Historical military conquest, such as the spread of the Mongol Empire, often led to which type of cultural diffusion?

B. Large historical empires built through conquest, like the Mongol Empire, often spread administrative systems, trade networks, and cultural practices across the territories they controlled.

Historians note that the historical spread of Buddhism from South Asia into East Asia occurred gradually along trade routes over centuries, carried by merchants, monks, and travelers, rather than through direct military conquest or colonization. Which historical cause of diffusion does this best illustrate?

B. The gradual spread of Buddhism along trade routes over centuries illustrates diffusion driven by historical trade and travel networks, rather than conquest or colonization.

Free Response
Historians studying the spread of a particular textile-weaving technique note that it appears first in a single region, then gradually appears in merchant cities located along a historic overland trade route, and finally appears, centuries later, in a distant colonized territory under the control of an empire that had conquered the original region.

AExplain how trade routes likely contributed to the technique's initial spread to merchant cities.

Sample response: Merchants traveling along the trade route likely carried knowledge of the weaving technique with them as they conducted business in different cities, gradually spreading it along the route.

BExplain how colonialism likely contributed to the technique's later spread to the distant colonized territory.

Sample response: As the conquering empire established political and economic control over the distant territory, it likely imposed or introduced its own cultural and economic practices, including the weaving technique, onto the colonized population.

CExplain one way that historical diffusion (such as through trade or colonialism) differs from contemporary diffusion in the speed at which cultural traits typically spread.

Sample response: Historical diffusion often took decades or centuries to spread across long distances due to slow transportation and communication, while contemporary diffusion, aided by the internet and modern transportation, can spread cultural traits globally within days or weeks.
