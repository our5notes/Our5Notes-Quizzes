Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1831 uprising that heightened Southern fears.
Sample response: Nat Turner's Rebellion, shown in the diagram, heightened Southern fears in 1831.
(b) Explain how the expanding cotton economy affected the experience of enslaved people in this period.
Sample response: As the cotton economy expanded rapidly across the Deep South, demand for enslaved labor grew correspondingly, leading to an internal slave trade that forcibly separated enslaved families as enslaved people were sold from the Upper South to newer cotton-growing regions; this expansion intensified the brutality and scale of slavery even as, elsewhere, some Northern states were abolishing it.
(c) Explain why free Black communities in the North, despite their freedom, still faced significant limitations.
Sample response: Even though free Black communities in Northern cities were legally free from slavery, they still faced widespread discrimination, including restricted voting rights (or none at all in many states), segregation, limited economic opportunities, and the constant threat of kidnapping into slavery; this shows that legal freedom did not translate into full civil equality or safety for free Black Americans in this period.
