A slope field where segments in each vertical strip (fixed x) all have the same slope, regardless of y, suggests a differential equation of the form:

A slope field shows segments forming a clear circular/rotational pattern, perpendicular to the line from the origin to each point. Which differential equation would most likely produce this pattern?

C. dy/dx=-x/y produces circular solution curves, with slope segments perpendicular to radius lines from the origin.

Refer to the three mini slope fields (A, B, C) shown above, corresponding to dy/dx=x, dy/dx=y, and dy/dx=-x/y in some order.

(a) Match Field A to its correct differential equation, and justify.
Sample response: Field A matches dy/dx=x, since the slope depends only on x, creating vertical "columns" of parallel segments.
(b) Match Field B to its correct differential equation, and justify.
Sample response: Field B matches dy/dx=y, since the slope depends only on y, creating horizontal "rows" of parallel segments.
(c) Match Field C to its correct differential equation, and justify.
Sample response: Field C matches dy/dx=-x/y, since the segments form a circular/rotational pattern, perpendicular to lines through the origin, consistent with circular solution curves.
