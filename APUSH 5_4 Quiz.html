Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the principle that let Utah and New Mexico decide the slavery question themselves.
Sample response: Popular sovereignty, shown in the diagram, let Utah and New Mexico decide the slavery question themselves.
(b) Explain why the Fugitive Slave Law was especially controversial in the North.
Sample response: The Fugitive Slave Law required Northern citizens and officials to actively assist in capturing and returning escaped enslaved people, even in states where slavery was illegal, forcing many Northerners who were otherwise not strongly abolitionist to directly participate in enforcing slavery; this direct imposition on Northern communities significantly increased anti-slavery sentiment and outrage in the North, even among those previously indifferent to the issue.
(c) Explain why the Compromise of 1850 ultimately proved to be only a temporary solution to sectional conflict.
Sample response: The Compromise of 1850 addressed the immediate crisis over the Mexican Cession territory but did not resolve the underlying disagreement over slavery's morality and future; provisions like popular sovereignty left the door open for renewed conflict in future territories, and the Fugitive Slave Law's unpopularity in the North actually deepened sectional resentment rather than easing it, setting the stage for further crises like the Kansas-Nebraska Act just a few years later.
