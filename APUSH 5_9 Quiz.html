Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the legal right Lincoln controversially suspended during the war.
Sample response: Habeas corpus, shown in the diagram, was the legal right Lincoln controversially suspended during the war.
(b) Explain how the Emancipation Proclamation changed the political and military purpose of the Civil War.
Sample response: Before the Emancipation Proclamation, the Union's official war goal was preserving the Union rather than ending slavery; by declaring enslaved people in Confederate territory free, Lincoln transformed the war into an explicit fight against slavery itself, which also discouraged European powers (who had generally abolished slavery) from supporting the Confederacy, and allowed formerly enslaved men to enlist in the Union army, providing a crucial new source of manpower.
(c) Explain how the Civil War contributed to a significant expansion of federal government power.
Sample response: The wartime emergency led the federal government to take on powers and responsibilities well beyond its prewar scope, including instituting a military draft, creating a national banking system and income tax, and suspending habeas corpus in some areas; this expansion of federal authority during the crisis set important precedents for a stronger, more active federal government that would continue to shape American governance well beyond the war's end.
