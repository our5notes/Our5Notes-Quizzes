On the interval (0,1), which function is on top for y=x³ vs y=x?

B. Testing x=0.5: x=0.5 > x³=0.125, so y=x is on top on (0,1).

Hard

For y=x³ and y=x on [-1,1], a student sets up a SINGLE integral ∫(-1 to 1)[x³-x]dx without splitting at x=0. Why is this approach flawed?

B. Without splitting, the regions on either side of x=0 partially cancel instead of both contributing positively to total area.

Free Response

Use the graph of y=x³ and y=x shown above.

(a) Find all intersection points.
Sample response: Setting x³=x: x³-x=0 → x(x-1)(x+1)=0 → x=-1, 0, 1.
(b) Set up the correct split integral for the total area enclosed between the curves on [-1,1], being careful with the subtraction order on each piece.
Sample response: A = ∫(-1 to 0)[x³-x]dx + ∫(0 to 1)[x-x³]dx.
(c) Evaluate the total area.
Sample response: First piece: [x⁴/4-x²/2](-1 to 0) = 0-(1/4-1/2) = 1/4. Second piece: [x²/2-x⁴/4](0 to 1) = (1/2-1/4)-0 = 1/4. Total area = 1/2.
