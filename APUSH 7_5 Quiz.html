Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the year the U.S. formally entered World War I.
Sample response: 1917, shown in the timeline, is when the U.S. formally entered World War I.
(b) Explain why the U.S. Senate refused to ratify the Treaty of Versailles and join the League of Nations.
Sample response: Many senators, especially isolationist Republicans, feared that joining the League of Nations would commit the U.S. to costly, open-ended international military obligations that could draw the nation into future European conflicts, undermining traditional American reluctance toward permanent foreign alliances; this reflected a broader postwar desire among many Americans to return to a more isolationist foreign policy after the sacrifices of WWI.
(c) Explain how German submarine warfare specifically influenced American public opinion toward entering the war.
Sample response: Germany's use of unrestricted submarine warfare, which targeted passenger and merchant ships without warning (including the sinking of the British liner Lusitania, which killed American passengers), violated American expectations of neutral rights on the high seas and directly killed American civilians; this made German aggression feel like a direct threat to American lives and interests, shifting public opinion away from strict neutrality and toward supporting U.S. entry into the war on the side of the Allies.
