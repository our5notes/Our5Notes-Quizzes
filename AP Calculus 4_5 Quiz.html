For a draining conical tank (vertex down), radius r and height h are related by similar triangles. Why substitute this r-h relationship into V=(1/3)πr²h BEFORE differentiating, rather than differentiating with both r and h as separate functions of time?

A 10-foot ladder leans against a wall. The base slides away from the wall at 1.5 ft/s.

(a) Set up the Pythagorean relationship between the base distance x and height y of the ladder.
Sample response: x² + y² = 100.
(b) Find how fast the top of the ladder is sliding down when the base is 6 feet from the wall.
Sample response: At x=6, y=8 (6-8-10 triangle). Differentiate: 2x(dx/dt)+2y(dy/dt)=0. Substitute: 2(6)(1.5)+2(8)(dy/dt)=0 → 18+16(dy/dt)=0 → dy/dt = -18/16 = -1.125 ft/s.
(c) MODEL: Air is pumped into a spherical balloon at 80 cm³/min. Find the rate of increase of the radius when r=4 cm.
Sample response: dV/dt = 4πr²(dr/dt). 80 = 4π(16)(dr/dt) → dr/dt = 80/(64π) = 5/(4π) ≈ 0.398 cm/min.
