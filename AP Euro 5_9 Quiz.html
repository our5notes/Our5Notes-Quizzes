Topic 5.9: Continuity and Change in 18th-Century States

AP European History · Unit 5

4 multiple-choice questions (1 easy, 2 medium, 1 hard) + 3 standalone short-answer prompts. Click an answer to check it; click "Show sample response" to check your short-answer responses.

1
Mark for Review
Easy

When historians evaluate what aspects of European political and social life changed versus remained the same between 1648 and 1815, they are applying which historical thinking skill?

B. Evaluating what changed versus remained the same applies the historical thinking skill of continuity and change over time.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

One significant change in European political life between 1648 and 1815 was:

B. A significant change was the emergence of new political ideas, such as popular sovereignty and nationalism, challenging traditional monarchical authority.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

Despite the political upheaval of the French Revolution and Napoleonic era, which aspect of European society demonstrated significant continuity by 1815?

B. Monarchy and traditional social hierarchy demonstrated significant continuity, being restored in much of Europe following the Congress of Vienna.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

A historian evaluating the period from 1648 to 1815 notes that while dramatic changes occurred, such as the French Revolution's challenge to traditional monarchical authority and the spread of nationalist sentiment through Napoleon's conquests, significant continuities also persisted, such as the restoration of monarchy and traditional social hierarchy in much of Europe following the Congress of Vienna. Which statement best reflects a balanced application of the continuity and change over time skill to this period?

C. A balanced application recognizes both significant changes, like the French Revolution's transformation, and important continuities, like the restoration of monarchy and social hierarchy after 1815.

Short Answer

Identify ONE significant political change that occurred in Europe between 1648 and 1815.

Sample response: The French Revolution's challenge to traditional monarchical authority, including the temporary establishment of a republic, was a significant political change during this period.

Short Answer

Explain ONE way that monarchy or traditional social hierarchy demonstrated continuity in Europe by 1815, despite the upheaval of the French Revolution and Napoleonic era.

Sample response: Following the Congress of Vienna, monarchy and traditional social hierarchy were restored in much of Europe, demonstrating significant continuity with the pre-revolutionary political order despite the intervening upheaval.

Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that nationalism represented a significant change in European political thought by 1815.

Sample response: The spread of nationalist sentiment, partly fueled by Napoleon's conquests and the conquered populations' reactions to French rule, represented a significant new development in European political thought, contributing to future political movements throughout the nineteenth century.
