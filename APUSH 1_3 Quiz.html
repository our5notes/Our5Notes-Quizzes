Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify which voyage occurred first: Columbus's 1492 voyage or Magellan's circumnavigation.
Sample response: Columbus's 1492 voyage occurred first, before Magellan's circumnavigation (1519–1522), as shown in the timeline.
(b) Explain why European monarchies were motivated to fund expensive, risky overseas voyages of exploration in this period.
Sample response: European monarchies sought new sources of wealth (particularly gold and valuable trade goods like spices), competed with rival kingdoms for national power and prestige, and were motivated by religious goals of spreading Christianity — a combination of economic, political, and religious incentives that made the financial risk of overseas voyages worthwhile.
(c) Explain how the outcome of Cortés's conquest of the Aztec Empire depended on factors beyond superior European weaponry alone.
Sample response: Cortés's small force could not have defeated the powerful Aztec Empire through weaponry alone; he formed crucial alliances with Native groups who resented Aztec rule (such as the Tlaxcala), and, perhaps most significantly, waves of European disease (like smallpox) devastated the Aztec population and social structure before and during the conquest, weakening their ability to resist.
