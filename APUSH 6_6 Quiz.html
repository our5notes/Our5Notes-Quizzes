Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the strategy Rockefeller pioneered by buying out competitors.
Sample response: Horizontal integration, shown in the diagram, was the strategy Rockefeller pioneered by buying out competitors.
(b) Explain the difference between vertical and horizontal integration.
Sample response: Vertical integration, used by Andrew Carnegie in the steel industry, involves a company controlling every stage of production, from acquiring raw materials to manufacturing and distributing the finished product, reducing dependence on outside suppliers; horizontal integration, used by John D. Rockefeller in oil refining, instead involves buying out or merging with competing companies at the same stage of production, reducing competition and increasing market control over that single stage.
(c) Explain how Social Darwinism served the interests of wealthy industrialists during this period.
Sample response: By framing economic success and failure as a natural, almost biological process of “survival of the fittest,” Social Darwinism suggested that wealthy industrialists had earned their fortunes through natural superiority, while poverty reflected natural weakness rather than exploitation or unequal opportunity; this ideology conveniently justified industrialists' vast wealth and discouraged government intervention (like labor regulations) that might have redistributed some of that wealth or improved conditions for workers.
