Topic 2.5 Quiz: Membrane Transport

Passive transport moves molecules down their gradient with no ATP; active transport uses ATP to move against the gradient.
Question 1 Easy

Which type of transport requires the cell to expend ATP?

APassive transport process
BActive transport process
CSimple diffusion process
DOsmosis transport process
Active transport requires ATP to move substances against their concentration gradient.
Question 2 Medium

Passive transport always moves substances in which direction relative to their concentration gradient?

AFrom low to high concentration
BFrom high to low concentration
COnly sideways, never vertically
DIn a completely random direction
Passive transport moves substances down their gradient, from high to low concentration.
Question 3 Medium

Using the diagram shown, what molecule directly powers the pump protein during active transport?

AA water molecule directly
BA glucose molecule directly
CAn ATP molecule directly
DAn oxygen molecule directly
ATP hydrolysis provides the energy that powers active transport pump proteins.
Question 4 Hard

The sodium-potassium pump moves sodium out of a cell and potassium into a cell, both against their concentration gradients. What does this indicate about the process?

AIt must be passive transport, since ions are involved
BIt must be active transport, since it requires energy input
CIt must be facilitated diffusion, since proteins are involved
DIt must be osmosis, since it involves charged particles
Movement against a concentration gradient always requires energy input, defining it as active transport.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) State the direction (relative to concentration gradient) that passive transport always moves a substance.
Sample response: Passive transport always moves a substance down its concentration gradient, from an area of higher concentration to an area of lower concentration.
(b) Explain why active transport requires ATP while passive transport does not.
Sample response: Active transport moves substances against their concentration gradient, which does not happen spontaneously, so the cell must expend energy from ATP to power this movement; passive transport instead moves substances in the direction they would naturally diffuse, requiring no additional energy input.
(c) Predict what would happen to active transport in a cell if its mitochondria stopped producing ATP, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: If a cell's mitochondria stopped producing ATP, active transport processes like the sodium-potassium pump would eventually stop functioning, since they depend on a continuous supply of ATP to power movement against concentration gradients, potentially disrupting the cell's ion balance.
