Free Response FRQ
(a) Explain the difference between a covalent bond and an ionic bond.
Sample response: A covalent bond forms when two atoms share a pair of electrons, while an ionic bond forms when electrons are completely transferred from one atom to another, creating oppositely charged ions that attract each other.
(b) Nitrogen makes up a much smaller percentage of the human body than carbon, hydrogen, or oxygen. Explain why nitrogen is still essential, giving one specific example.
Sample response: Nitrogen is an essential component of amino acids, which are the building blocks of proteins; without nitrogen, cells could not construct the proteins needed for enzymes, structure, and countless other cellular functions.
(c) Explain why carbon's bonding properties allow for greater molecular diversity than an element that can only form one or two bonds.
Sample response: Because carbon can form up to four stable bonds simultaneously, it can connect to multiple other atoms (including other carbons) at once, creating branched, ringed, and chain structures; an element limited to one or two bonds could only form short chains or simple pairs, drastically limiting molecular complexity.
