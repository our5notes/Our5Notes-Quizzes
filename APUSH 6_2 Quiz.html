Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1862 law that encouraged settlement by granting land.
Sample response: The Homestead Act, shown in the diagram, encouraged settlement by granting land to settlers.
(b) Explain how the transcontinental railroad transformed the economic development of the West.
Sample response: By dramatically reducing the time and cost of transporting goods and people across the country, the transcontinental railroad allowed Western resources like cattle, minerals, and crops to reach profitable Eastern and even international markets; this connectivity attracted massive investment and settlement to the West, transforming what had been a difficult-to-access frontier into an integrated part of the national economy.
(c) Explain why success under the Homestead Act was far from guaranteed for many settlers.
Sample response: Although the Homestead Act granted land, many settlers on the Great Plains faced serious challenges including unpredictable and often harsh weather, lack of adequate water for farming, high costs for necessary equipment and railroad transport, and general unfamiliarity with the region's difficult farming conditions; as a result, many homesteaders struggled financially or abandoned their claims entirely, despite the promise of free land.
