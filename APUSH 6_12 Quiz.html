Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1890 law that aimed to break up harmful monopolies.
Sample response: The Sherman Antitrust Act, shown in the diagram, aimed to break up harmful monopolies in 1890.
(b) Explain why many business leaders favored laissez-faire economic policy in this period.
Sample response: Business leaders generally believed that minimal government interference allowed the most efficient companies to succeed and grow, maximizing overall economic growth and innovation; laissez-faire policy also directly benefited large industrialists by allowing them to build monopolies, set prices, and manage labor relations without government restriction, so it is unsurprising that many wealthy business leaders actively supported this hands-off approach, often reinforced by Social Darwinist ideology.
(c) Explain why the Sherman Antitrust Act had limited practical impact in its early years.
Sample response: Although the Sherman Antitrust Act was intended to break up harmful monopolies and trusts, its vague legal language made it difficult to enforce effectively, and the courts and federal government in the 1890s remained generally sympathetic to business interests; as a result, the law was used more often against labor unions (arguing that strikes illegally restrained trade) than against the very monopolies it was designed to target, showing how weak enforcement can undermine even well-intentioned legislation.
