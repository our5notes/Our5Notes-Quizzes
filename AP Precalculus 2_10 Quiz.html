Using the graph shown of y=2ˣ and y=log₂(x), the point (3,8) on y=2ˣ corresponds to which point on y=log₂(x)?

Given that f(x)=5ˣ and g(x)=log₅(x) are inverses, a student wants to evaluate g(f(12)) without extra calculation. What is the value, and why?

A. Applying a function and its inverse in sequence always returns the original input.

Free Response

Use the graph of y=2ˣ and its inverse y=log₂(x) shown above.

(a) Identify one point on y=2ˣ and its corresponding reflected point on y=log₂(x).
Sample response: (2,4) is on y=2ˣ; the reflected point (4,2) is on y=log₂(x).
(b) State the domain and range of y=log₂(x), based on the domain and range of y=2ˣ.
Sample response: Domain of log₂(x): x>0. Range: all real numbers.
(c) Given that f(x)=4ˣ and g(x)=log₄(x) are inverses, evaluate f(g(9)) without additional calculation, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: f(g(9)) = 9, since applying inverse functions in sequence returns the original input.
