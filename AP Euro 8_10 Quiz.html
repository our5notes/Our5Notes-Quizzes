Explain ONE way that the devastation of World War I contributed to disillusionment among European writers and artists.

Sample response: The unprecedented scale of death and destruction in World War I shattered many Europeans' faith in the traditional values and confident belief in progress that they felt had led society into such a catastrophic conflict, producing deep disillusionment among writers and artists.

Explain ONE core idea of existentialist philosophy as developed by thinkers like Sartre.
