Free Response FRQ
(a) Explain why water is described as a polar molecule.
Sample response: Water is polar because oxygen has greater electronegativity than hydrogen, pulling shared electrons closer and creating a partial negative charge on oxygen and partial positive charges on the hydrogens.
(b) Explain why water's high specific heat capacity helps organisms living in aquatic environments maintain a stable body temperature.
Sample response: Water can absorb or release large amounts of heat energy with only small changes in its own temperature, so aquatic environments tend to have relatively stable temperatures; this helps organisms avoid dramatic temperature swings that would otherwise require additional energy to compensate for.
(c) Explain how cohesion helps water move up a tall tree, from roots to leaves.
Sample response: Because water molecules hydrogen-bond to each other (cohesion), when water evaporates from leaves it pulls the adjoining water molecules upward in a continuous column through the plant's vascular tissue, allowing water to be drawn up from the roots against gravity.
