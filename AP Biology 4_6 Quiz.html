Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the three checkpoints shown in the diagram.
Sample response: The three checkpoints shown are the G1 checkpoint, the G2 checkpoint, and the M (spindle) checkpoint.
(b) Explain the general function of a cell cycle checkpoint.
Sample response: A checkpoint monitors conditions within the cell, such as DNA damage or completeness of replication, and can halt the cell cycle from progressing further if problems are detected, preventing a damaged or unprepared cell from dividing.
(c) Explain how a mutation disabling checkpoint function could contribute to the development of cancer.
Sample response: If a checkpoint is disabled by mutation, a cell with DNA damage (potentially including cancer-causing mutations) could continue dividing unchecked, allowing these mutations to accumulate and be passed on to daughter cells, contributing to the uncontrolled cell growth characteristic of cancer.
