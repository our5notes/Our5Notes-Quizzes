Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1962 crisis that brought the U.S. and USSR near nuclear war.
Sample response: The Cuban Missile Crisis, shown in the timeline, brought the U.S. and USSR near nuclear war in 1962.
(b) Explain how the policy of containment led to U.S. military involvement in Korea and Vietnam.
Sample response: Containment held that the U.S. needed to actively prevent communism from spreading to new countries, since allowing one nation to fall to communism was feared to trigger a chain reaction (the “domino theory”) of neighboring countries following suit; this reasoning directly justified U.S. military intervention in both Korea (to prevent communist North Korea from conquering the South) and later Vietnam (to prevent communist North Vietnam from unifying the country), even though neither conflict directly threatened U.S. territory.
(c) Explain why the Cuban Missile Crisis is considered the most dangerous moment of the Cold War.
Sample response: The discovery of Soviet nuclear missiles being installed in Cuba, just 90 miles from U.S. shores, brought the two superpowers into direct, immediate confrontation, with President Kennedy publicly demanding their removal while U.S. and Soviet forces were placed on high alert; for several tense days, the world faced a genuine, immediate risk of nuclear war, making this crisis the closest the Cold War came to direct military conflict between the two superpowers before a negotiated resolution was reached.
