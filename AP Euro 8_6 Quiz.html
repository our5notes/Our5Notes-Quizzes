Medium

Totalitarian regimes, such as fascist Italy, Nazi Germany, and the Soviet Union under Stalin, relied heavily on which methods to maintain control over their populations?

B. Totalitarian regimes relied heavily on state-controlled propaganda, secret police, and suppression of political opposition.

Hard

Historians note that Nazi Germany built on earlier Italian fascist models while adding a distinctive and virulent ideology of racial nationalism, particularly extreme antisemitism, that would later provide the ideological foundation for the Holocaust. Which statement best explains the significance of this distinction between Italian fascism and Nazi ideology?

B. Nazi Germany's added emphasis on extreme racial ideology and antisemitism represented a distinctive and especially dangerous development beyond earlier Italian fascism.
