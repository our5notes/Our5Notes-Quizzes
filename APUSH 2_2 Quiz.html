Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the colonial power that established New Netherland.
Sample response: The Netherlands (the Dutch), shown in the diagram, established New Netherland along the Hudson River.
(b) Explain why French colonization generally resulted in closer alliances with Native peoples than English colonization did.
Sample response: French colonization was centered on the fur trade, which depended directly on maintaining good trading relationships with Native nations who supplied the furs; because the French colonial population remained relatively small and their economic model required Native cooperation rather than large-scale land displacement, they generally cultivated closer alliances than the English, whose growing agricultural settlements required displacing Native peoples from the land itself.
(c) Explain how the differing motivations behind English colonization (profit vs. religious freedom) shaped different colonies.
Sample response: Colonies founded primarily for profit, such as Virginia (funded by the Virginia Company), prioritized cash crops like tobacco and often struggled with harsh, exploitative labor conditions; colonies founded for religious freedom, such as Massachusetts Bay, prioritized building tight-knit religious communities around shared faith, resulting in very different social structures and priorities even though both were English colonies.
