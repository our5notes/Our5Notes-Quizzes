Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the crop that dominated the South's economy.
Sample response: Cotton, shown in the diagram, dominated the South's economy.
(b) Explain why the North's and South's differing economic systems contributed to growing sectional conflict.
Sample response: The North's free-labor, industrial economy increasingly viewed slavery as economically backward and morally troubling, while the South's plantation economy depended fundamentally on enslaved labor for its profitability and way of life; these fundamentally different economic interests meant that policies favoring one region's economic system (such as restricting slavery's expansion) directly threatened the other's, making economic compromise increasingly difficult to sustain.
(c) Explain why the South's fear of losing political power intensified sectional tension.
Sample response: As Northern population and industrial growth outpaced the South's, the North gained increasing numbers of seats in the House of Representatives, threatening the South's ability to protect its interests (especially slavery) through the political process; this fear made maintaining an equal number of free and slave states in the Senate especially critical to Southern leaders, explaining why every new territory's status became such a high-stakes political battle.
