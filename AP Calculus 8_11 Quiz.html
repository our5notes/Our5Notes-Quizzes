The region between y=√x and y=x/2, from their intersection at x=0 to x=4, is revolved around the x-axis (shown above).

(a) Confirm the intersection points algebraically.
Sample response: √x=x/2 → x=x²/4 → x²-4x=0 → x=0, x=4.
(b) Identify which function gives the outer radius and which gives the inner radius on this interval.
Sample response: √x is farther from the x-axis than x/2 on (0,4), so √x is outer and x/2 is inner.
(c) Set up and evaluate the volume using the washer method.
Sample response: V = π∫(0 to 4)[x-x²/4]dx = π[x²/2-x³/12](0 to 4) = π[8-16/3] = 8π/3.
