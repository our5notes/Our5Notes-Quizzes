Use the graph of the parent function f(x)=x² and the transformed function g(x) shown above.

(a) Describe the transformations applied to f(x)=x² to produce the graph of g(x).
Sample response: Reflection over the x-axis, vertical compression by a factor of 0.5, horizontal shift right 2, vertical shift up 3.

Sample response: g(x) = −0.5(x−2)²+3.
