(a) Verify that y=2e^(-x) is a solution to dy/dx=-y.
Sample response: y' = -2e^(-x) = -y. Both sides match, confirming it is a solution.
(b) Verify that y=x²+Cx (for any constant C) is a solution to xy'-y=x².
Sample response: y'=2x+C. xy'-y = x(2x+C)-(x²+Cx) = 2x²+Cx-x²-Cx = x², matching the right side.
(c) Verify that y=6e^(2x) solves dy/dx=2y AND satisfies the initial condition y(0)=6.
Sample response: y'=12e^(2x)=2(6e^(2x))=2y, confirming it solves the DE. y(0)=6e⁰=6, matching the initial condition.
