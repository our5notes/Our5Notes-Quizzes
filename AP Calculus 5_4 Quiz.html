Free Response

Let f'(x) = (x−1)²(x−4).

(a) Find the critical points of f.
Sample response: Critical points: x=1 and x=4.
(b) Use the First Derivative Test to classify each critical point (consider the squared factor's effect on sign).
Sample response: Testing values: f' is negative on both sides of x=1 (no sign change) — x=1 is NOT a local extremum. f' changes from negative to positive at x=4 — local minimum at x=4.
(c) Explain why x=1 might behave differently from a "typical" critical point, referencing the repeated factor.
Sample response: Because (x-1)² is always non-negative, it never actually changes sign at x=1 — only the linear factor (x-4) determines whether f' changes sign, so x=1 ends up being a critical point where the derivative touches zero without truly changing sign.
