Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1894 strike suppressed by federal troops.
Sample response: The Pullman Strike, shown in the diagram, was suppressed by federal troops in 1894.
(b) Explain why government and business often sided against striking workers in this period.
Sample response: Government officials and business owners often shared a belief in protecting property rights and maintaining order, and many judges and politicians were sympathetic to business interests or worried that strikes could disrupt broader economic activity (like the Pullman Strike's effect on mail delivery); this made government intervention, such as sending in troops or issuing injunctions against strikers, a common and effective tool for suppressing labor unrest, limiting how much workers could achieve through strikes alone.
(c) Explain one key difference between the Knights of Labor and the American Federation of Labor.
Sample response: The Knights of Labor sought to organize a broad coalition of workers, including unskilled laborers, women, and Black workers, around a wide range of social and economic reforms; the American Federation of Labor, by contrast, focused more narrowly on skilled workers and practical, achievable goals like higher wages, shorter hours, and better working conditions, generally avoiding broader political or social reform goals, which contributed to its greater long-term stability and success.
