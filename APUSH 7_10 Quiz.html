Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the program that created a lasting safety net for the elderly.
Sample response: Social Security, shown in the diagram, created a lasting safety net for the elderly and unemployed.
(b) Explain why Social Security is often considered the New Deal's most lasting achievement.
Sample response: Unlike many New Deal relief programs, which were designed as temporary responses to the immediate crisis of the Depression, Social Security created a permanent, ongoing government program providing income security for the elderly and unemployed that has continued, with modifications, to the present day; this made Social Security a fundamental and lasting expansion of the federal government's role in providing for citizens' economic welfare, well beyond the immediate context of the 1930s.
(c) Explain the difference between the New Deal's “recovery” and “reform” goals, using specific examples.
Sample response: Recovery programs, like the National Recovery Administration, aimed to restart economic growth relatively quickly by coordinating business production and prices; reform programs, like the Securities and Exchange Commission, aimed at longer-term structural changes to prevent the kind of unregulated financial speculation and abuse that had contributed to the 1929 crash, seeking to make the financial system more stable and trustworthy for the future, rather than just addressing the immediate economic emergency.
