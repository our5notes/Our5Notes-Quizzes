Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify which type of variation, shown in the diagram, is illustrated by blood type.
Sample response: Blood type, shown in the diagram, illustrates discrete variation.
(b) Explain why continuous variation, like height, typically produces a bell-shaped distribution when graphed.
Sample response: Continuous traits are typically influenced by many different genes, each contributing a small additive effect, plus environmental factors; the combined effect of many small, independent factors naturally tends to produce a bell-shaped distribution.
(c) Explain why studying phenotypic variation is important for understanding how natural selection can act on a population.
Sample response: Natural selection requires variation to act upon; understanding the patterns and sources of phenotypic variation helps predict how a population might respond to selective pressures and whether a trait is likely to be heritable and capable of evolving.
