Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which stage shown separates sister chromatids.
Sample response: Meiosis II, shown in the diagram, is the stage that separates sister chromatids.
(b) Explain why meiosis, rather than mitosis, is necessary to produce gametes.
Sample response: If gametes were produced by mitosis, they would be diploid, and combining two diploid gametes during fertilization would double the chromosome number each generation; meiosis instead produces haploid gametes, so fertilization restores the normal diploid number.
(c) Explain why the chromosome number stays the same between Meiosis I and Meiosis II, even though chromatid count changes.
Sample response: Meiosis I separates homologous chromosomes, reducing the chromosome number from diploid to haploid, but each chromosome still has two sister chromatids at that point; Meiosis II separates these sister chromatids, so the chromosome number doesn't change further, only the chromatid count per chromosome does.
