(a) Find the arc length of y=x^(3/2) from x=0 to x=4. Show the setup and the u-substitution used.
Sample response: f'(x)=(3/2)x^(1/2). L=∫(0 to 4)√(1+(9/4)x)dx. Let u=1+(9/4)x, du=(9/4)dx. L=(8/27)(1+(9/4)x)^(3/2) from 0 to 4 = (8/27)[10^(3/2)-1] ≈ 9.07.
(b) Set up (but do not evaluate by hand) the arc length integral for y=eˣ from x=0 to x=1.
Sample response: L = ∫(0 to 1)√(1+e^(2x))dx.
(c) MODEL: A particle moves with dx/dt=4t and dy/dt=3t, for 0≤t≤3. Find the total distance traveled.
Sample response: Distance = ∫(0 to 3)√[(4t)²+(3t)²]dt = ∫(0 to 3)√(25t²)dt = ∫(0 to 3)5t dt = [5t²/2](0 to 3) = 22.5 units.
