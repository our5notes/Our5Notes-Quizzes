Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the committee that investigated alleged communist influence in Hollywood.
Sample response: HUAC, shown in the diagram, investigated alleged communist influence in Hollywood.
(b) Explain why the Red Scare raised significant civil liberties concerns.
Sample response: Many individuals accused of communist sympathies during the Red Scare faced serious consequences, including job loss and social ostracism, often based on flimsy evidence, guilt by association, or refusal to name other suspected communists; this pattern of punishing people without solid evidence or fair legal process, driven substantially by fear and political opportunism rather than legitimate security concerns, raised serious concerns about the erosion of due process and free speech rights during this period.
(c) Explain why Senator McCarthy's influence eventually declined after several years of prominence.
Sample response: McCarthy's influence began to decline after he made increasingly reckless and poorly substantiated accusations, including against the U.S. Army during televised hearings in 1954, which allowed the public to see his aggressive and often unsubstantiated tactics directly; this public exposure, combined with a formal Senate censure of McCarthy later that year, significantly damaged his credibility and effectively ended his prominent role in national politics.
