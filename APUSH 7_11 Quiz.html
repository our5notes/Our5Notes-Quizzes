Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1941 law that allowed the U.S. to supply Britain with war materials.
Sample response: The Lend-Lease Act, shown in the timeline, allowed the U.S. to supply Britain with war materials in 1941.
(b) Explain why the Lend-Lease Act represented a significant shift away from strict U.S. neutrality.
Sample response: Although the Lend-Lease Act stopped short of formally declaring war, it directly provided Britain and other Allied nations with large quantities of American military equipment and supplies, effectively making the U.S. an active economic and military supporter of one side in the ongoing conflict; this represented a clear departure from the strict neutrality the earlier Neutrality Acts had aimed to maintain, showing how American public and political opinion was shifting well before Pearl Harbor.
(c) Explain the general pattern of U.S. foreign policy shifting from isolationism to involvement between the world wars.
Sample response: After the costly experience of World War I, the U.S. initially retreated into isolationism, avoiding new binding international commitments (like the League of Nations) and passing Neutrality Acts to prevent future entanglement; however, as the aggressive actions of Germany, Italy, and Japan in the 1930s escalated into a new World War, the U.S. gradually and incrementally increased its support for the Allies, through steps like Lend-Lease, until the direct attack on Pearl Harbor made continued neutrality impossible.
