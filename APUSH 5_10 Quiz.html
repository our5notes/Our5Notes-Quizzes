Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the amendment that abolished slavery.
Sample response: The 13th Amendment, shown in the diagram, abolished slavery.
(b) Explain the significance of the 14th Amendment beyond simply granting citizenship.
Sample response: Beyond granting citizenship to all persons born in the United States (including formerly enslaved people), the 14th Amendment's guarantee of “equal protection of the laws” established a constitutional foundation for challenging discriminatory state laws, one that would become critically important far beyond Reconstruction, forming the basis for later civil rights legal victories throughout the 20th century.
(c) Explain why federal enforcement (such as stationing troops in the South) was considered necessary during Reconstruction.
Sample response: Despite the new constitutional amendments granting rights to formerly enslaved people, many Southern state governments and individuals actively resisted these changes through discriminatory laws (Black Codes) and violence; federal troops and oversight were considered necessary to enforce these new constitutional rights against this local resistance, since without active federal enforcement, the rights guaranteed on paper risked being ignored in practice.
