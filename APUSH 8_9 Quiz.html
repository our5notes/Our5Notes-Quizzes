Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the two programs that provide health coverage for the elderly and poor.
Sample response: Medicare and Medicaid, shown in the diagram, provide health coverage for the elderly and poor.
(b) Explain how the Great Society represented an expansion of the federal government's role compared to earlier periods.
Sample response: The Great Society significantly expanded the scope of federal responsibility beyond even the New Deal, creating permanent new programs addressing health care (Medicare, Medicaid), poverty (Head Start, food stamps), and civil rights enforcement; this reflected a belief, common among liberal policymakers of the era, that an active federal government had both the capacity and the responsibility to directly address complex, longstanding social and economic problems, going well beyond the New Deal's primary focus on economic recovery and relief.
(c) Explain the significance of the Immigration Act of 1965 for the demographic makeup of the United States.
Sample response: By eliminating the discriminatory national-origin quota system, which had favored immigration from Northern and Western Europe since the 1920s, the Immigration Act of 1965 opened the door to significantly increased immigration from Asia, Latin America, and Africa; this law dramatically reshaped the demographic composition of the United States over the following decades, contributing to far greater racial and ethnic diversity than existed at the start of this period.
