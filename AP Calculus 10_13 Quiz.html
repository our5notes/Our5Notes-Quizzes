(a) Find the radius of convergence of Σn·xⁿ.
Sample response: |aₙ₊₁/aₙ| = |(n+1)xⁿ⁺¹|/|nxⁿ| = [(n+1)/n]|x| → |x|. Need |x|<1, so R=1.
(b) Find the radius and interval of convergence of Σ(x+2)ⁿ/n² (check both endpoints).
Sample response: |aₙ₊₁/aₙ| → |x+2|. Need |x+2|<1, so R=1, giving -31, converges. At x=-3: Σ(-1)ⁿ/n², converges absolutely. Interval: [-3,-1].
(c) Explain why the Ratio Test alone cannot determine the full interval of convergence, even though it finds the radius.
Sample response: The Ratio Test is inconclusive exactly when the limiting ratio equals 1, which happens precisely at the endpoints of the interval; therefore each endpoint must be checked separately using a different test, since the Ratio Test provides no information there.
