5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain how Russia's Duma electoral system has changed since 2007.

Sample response: Russia moved from a Duma elected entirely through proportional representation (2007-2011) to a mixed system with 225 PR seats and 225 single-member district seats.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain why members of the Federation Council are not considered to hold significant independent political power.

Sample response: Because Federation Council members are appointed rather than elected, one by the regional governor and one by the regional legislature, with the president able to add more, the chamber lacks independent democratic accountability and tends to have limited real political influence.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain why the 2020 constitutional changes to term limits were politically significant for Putin's future in office.

Sample response: The 2020 changes preserved general term limits for future presidents but created a special exception allowing Putin (and Medvedev) to potentially serve additional terms, effectively allowing Putin to remain in power well beyond what the prior term-limit rules would have permitted.
