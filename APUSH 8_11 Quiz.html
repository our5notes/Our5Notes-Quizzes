Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one parallel movement inspired by the Civil Rights Movement.
Sample response: The Chicano movement, shown in the diagram, was one parallel movement inspired by the Civil Rights Movement.
(b) Explain why some civil rights activists shifted toward more militant tactics and separatist goals by the late 1960s.
Sample response: Frustrated by the slow pace of change despite the legislative successes of the mid-1960s, and by continued economic inequality, urban poverty, and police violence that legislation alone had not solved, some activists concluded that nonviolent integrationist strategies were insufficient; groups like the Black Panthers instead emphasized Black self-determination, community self-defense, and direct economic and social programs within Black communities, representing a more assertive and sometimes separatist response to continued inequality.
(c) Explain how the Civil Rights Movement served as a model for other social movements of this era.
Sample response: The Civil Rights Movement demonstrated effective strategies for organizing, including nonviolent direct action, legal challenges, and media-savvy public demonstrations, that other groups facing discrimination adapted to their own circumstances; the Chicano movement, American Indian Movement, and women's rights movement all drew inspiration and practical organizing lessons from civil rights activism, showing how one movement's strategies and successes could provide a template for other groups seeking greater equality and rights.
