Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify one structural similarity shown among the forelimbs of the species in the diagram.
Sample response: All the forelimbs shown share the same basic bone arrangement, despite differing in overall shape.
(b) Explain why homologous structures are considered strong evidence for common ancestry.
Sample response: Although the forelimbs serve different functions in each species, they share the same fundamental bone structure, best explained by all species inheriting this basic pattern from a common ancestor, with natural selection later modifying it for different functions.
(c) Explain why analogous structures, like insect and bird wings, are NOT considered evidence of close common ancestry.
Sample response: Analogous structures arise through convergent evolution, where unrelated lineages independently evolve similar solutions to similar challenges, without inheriting the structure from a shared recent ancestor; since their underlying structures differ despite similar function, they indicate independent evolution rather than close common ancestry.
