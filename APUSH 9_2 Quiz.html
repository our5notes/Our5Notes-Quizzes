Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the term for Reagan's economic policies of tax cuts and deregulation.
Sample response: Reaganomics, shown in the diagram, is the term for Reagan's economic policies of tax cuts and deregulation.
(b) Explain the core economic reasoning behind Reaganomics.
Sample response: Reaganomics was based on supply-side economic theory, which held that cutting taxes, especially for businesses and higher earners, would encourage greater investment and economic activity, ultimately generating enough economic growth to offset the initial loss of tax revenue; combined with deregulation, intended to reduce burdens on business, and reduced social spending, this approach represented a significant shift away from the more government-interventionist economic policies of the mid-20th century.
(c) Explain how the New Right coalition combined economic and social conservatism to support Reagan's political success.
Sample response: The New Right coalition brought together voters motivated by economic conservatism, who supported Reagan's tax cuts and deregulation, with voters motivated by social and religious conservatism, particularly evangelical Christians concerned about issues like abortion and traditional family values; this combination of economic and social conservative voters created a broad and durable political coalition that provided crucial support for Reagan's electoral success and helped establish conservatism as a major force in American politics for decades.
