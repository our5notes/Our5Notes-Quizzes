Short Answer Practice

5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Identify ONE way that proportional representation differs from First Past the Post in how it allocates legislative seats.

Sample response: Proportional representation allocates seats based on each party's overall share of the national vote, while First Past the Post awards each individual district's seat to whichever candidate wins the most votes in that district.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE reason why proportional representation systems are more likely than FPTP to produce coalition governments.

Sample response: Because proportional representation spreads seats across more parties in line with their vote share, it is less likely that any single party wins an outright majority, making coalition governments among multiple parties more likely.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE difference between the pluralist and corporatist models of interest group activity.

Sample response: In the pluralist model, interest groups form and compete freely and independently of the state, while in the corporatist model, the state itself controls which interest groups are allowed to exist and be officially recognized.
