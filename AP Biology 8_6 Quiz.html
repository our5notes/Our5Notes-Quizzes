Question 1 Easy

What term describes a species with a disproportionately large effect on its community relative to its abundance?

AA dominant species
BA keystone species
CAn invasive species
DAn indicator species
A keystone species has a disproportionately large effect on its community relative to its abundance.
Question 2 Medium

Using the diagram shown, what would likely happen to the kelp forest if sea otters were removed?

ASea urchin populations would decrease sharply
BSea urchin populations would increase and overgraze kelp
CThe kelp forest would remain completely unaffected
DSea otters have no real effect on kelp forests
Removing sea otters would allow sea urchin populations to grow unchecked, overgrazing the kelp forest.
Question 3 Medium

What term describes the total number of different species present in a community?

ASpecies evenness measure
BSpecies richness measure
CSpecies diversity index
DSpecies biomass measure
Species richness is the total number of different species present in a community.
Question 4 Hard

Using the diagram shown, explain why a community with high biodiversity tends to be more resilient to environmental disturbances.

AMore species provide backup roles if some are affected
BHigh biodiversity always prevents any disturbances entirely
CFewer species always respond better to sudden changes
DBiodiversity has no real effect on ecosystem resilience
A greater variety of species can fill vacated ecological roles if some species are affected by a disturbance.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the keystone species shown in the diagram.
Sample response: The sea otter, shown in the diagram, is the keystone species.
(b) Explain what would happen to the kelp forest ecosystem if the keystone species shown were removed.
Sample response: If sea otters were removed, sea urchin populations (their prey) would grow unchecked, leading to overgrazing and destruction of the kelp forest, which would remove habitat and food sources for many other species that depend on it.
(c) Explain why a community with high biodiversity tends to be more resilient to environmental disturbances than one with low biodiversity.
Sample response: In a community with high biodiversity, a greater variety of species with different traits and roles means that if a disturbance affects some species, others may be less affected or able to fill vacated ecological roles, helping the community maintain its overall function.
