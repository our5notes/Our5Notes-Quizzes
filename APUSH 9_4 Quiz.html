Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1994 trade agreement between the U.S., Canada, and Mexico.
Sample response: NAFTA, shown in the diagram, was the 1994 trade agreement between the U.S., Canada, and Mexico.
(b) Explain how globalization and deindustrialization were connected in this period.
Sample response: As trade barriers fell through agreements like NAFTA and global transportation and communication improved, many American manufacturing companies found it more profitable to relocate production to countries with lower labor costs, directly contributing to the decline of domestic manufacturing jobs known as deindustrialization; this connection meant that increasing global economic integration and the decline of traditional American manufacturing were closely linked, rather than separate, economic trends.
(c) Explain why the digital revolution and globalization together contributed to widening income inequality.
Sample response: The digital revolution created lucrative new opportunities primarily for highly educated, technically skilled workers in fields like software and finance, while globalization and deindustrialization simultaneously reduced the availability of stable, well-paying manufacturing jobs that had previously supported a broad middle class, including many workers without advanced degrees; this combination meant that economic gains increasingly concentrated among highly skilled workers, while many others faced declining job security and wages, widening the gap between high-skill and low-skill workers.
