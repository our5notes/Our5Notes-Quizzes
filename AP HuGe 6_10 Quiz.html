A city neighborhood experiences a wave of new investment, renovated housing, and rising property values over a decade, and while some see this as a positive revitalization, long-term lower-income residents report being priced out and displaced from the community they grew up in. Which urban change process does this scenario best illustrate, and what is the central controversy associated with it?

B. This scenario illustrates gentrification, and its central controversy is the tension between neighborhood revitalization and the displacement of long-term, lower-income residents.

Free Response
A historically lower-income urban neighborhood experiences significant new investment over a decade, with renovated housing, new businesses, and rising property values; some residents welcome the improved infrastructure and safety, while many long-term residents report being displaced due to rising rents they can no longer afford.

AIdentify the urban change process illustrated in this scenario.

Sample response: This scenario illustrates gentrification.

BExplain one potential benefit of this process for the neighborhood.

Sample response: Gentrification can bring improved infrastructure, increased investment, new businesses, and enhanced safety to a previously underinvested neighborhood.

CExplain one potential negative consequence of this process for long-term residents, and describe one policy a city government could use to address it.

Sample response: Long-term, lower-income residents may be displaced as rising rents and property values make the neighborhood unaffordable; a city government could adopt rent control policies or require a percentage of new housing developments to include affordable housing units to help address this displacement.
