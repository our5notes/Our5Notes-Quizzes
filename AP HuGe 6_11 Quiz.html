Which combination of urban sustainability tensions does this scenario best illustrate?

B. This scenario illustrates a tension between an environmental sustainability goal (reduced emissions through transit) and a social equity concern (displacement of lower-income residents near the new transit line).

Free Response
A city builds a new light rail transit line specifically intended to reduce car dependency and improve air quality in a historically underserved, lower-income neighborhood, but within a few years, rising property values near the new stations begin pricing out many of the original lower-income residents.

AExplain how the light rail line was intended to advance urban environmental sustainability.

Sample response: The light rail line was intended to reduce residents' dependence on personal automobiles, lowering traffic congestion and vehicle emissions, and thereby improving air quality in the neighborhood.

BExplain how this same project created an unintended social equity or environmental justice challenge for the neighborhood.

Sample response: The rising property values triggered by the new transit stations made housing less affordable, ultimately displacing many of the original lower-income residents the project was meant to benefit, creating a social equity challenge.

CExplain one policy a city could adopt to help prevent this kind of displacement while still pursuing sustainable transit investments.

Sample response: The city could require new developments near transit stations to include a set percentage of affordable housing units, helping ensure that original lower-income residents can remain in the neighborhood even as property values rise.
