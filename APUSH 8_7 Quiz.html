Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the U.S. program that provided economic aid to rebuild Western Europe.
Sample response: The Marshall Plan, shown in the diagram, provided economic aid to rebuild Western Europe.
(b) Explain the dual humanitarian and strategic motivations behind the Marshall Plan.
Sample response: The Marshall Plan aimed to help rebuild the devastated economies of Western Europe after the destruction of World War II, addressing genuine humanitarian need; at the same time, U.S. policymakers believed that economic desperation and instability made European nations more vulnerable to communist influence, so rebuilding these economies also served the strategic goal of containing the spread of communism, showing how humanitarian aid and Cold War strategy were closely intertwined in this policy.
(c) Explain the significance of NATO as a departure from traditional U.S. foreign policy.
Sample response: By joining NATO, the U.S. committed for the first time in peacetime to a permanent, binding military alliance, agreeing that an attack on any member nation would be treated as an attack on all; this represented a significant departure from the traditional American reluctance toward permanent foreign alliances (as seen in the earlier rejection of the League of Nations), reflecting the U.S.'s new sense of permanent global responsibility in the Cold War era.
