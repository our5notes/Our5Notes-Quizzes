The exact position of a place on Earth's surface, typically expressed using coordinates such as latitude and longitude, is known as:

B. Absolute location refers to a place's exact, fixed position, usually described with coordinates.

Describing a city as "60 miles northeast of the state capital, along a major river" is an example of:

B. Relative location describes a place's position in relation to other places or features, rather than using fixed coordinates.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

As technology such as high-speed trains and the internet reduces the effective distance between two places, geographers describe this phenomenon as:

B. Time-space compression describes how transportation and communication technology reduce the practical, experienced distance between places.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Two cities are the same physical distance apart, but City A is connected to its neighbor by a high-speed rail line while City B relies only on unpaved roads. A geographer observes that interaction (trade, migration, communication) between City A and its neighbor is far more frequent than between City B and its neighbor. Which concept best explains this difference?

B. Distance decay describes how interaction between places declines as effective (not just physical) distance increases; poor connectivity increases effective distance and interaction decline for City B.

Free Response
A rural town gains a new high-speed highway connection to a major metropolitan area that previously took three hours to reach by old roads and now takes 45 minutes.

AExplain how this change illustrates the concept of time-space compression.

Sample response: The physical distance between the town and the city has not changed, but the new highway dramatically reduces travel time, effectively "shrinking" the distance between them in practical terms.

BExplain how distance decay between the rural town and the city might change as a result of this new highway.

Sample response: Because travel time and effort have decreased, distance decay weakens, meaning interaction (commuting, trade, visits) between the two places is likely to increase.

CDescribe one likely effect this improved connectivity could have on migration or economic patterns between the two places.

Sample response: The town might see increased commuting to city jobs, new economic development, or population growth as it becomes more attractive to people who work in the city but want to live in a smaller town.
