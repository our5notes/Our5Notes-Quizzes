Given dy/dx = x + y, with initial condition y(0)=1.

(a) Use Euler's Method with step size Δx=0.5 to approximate y(1). Show a table of each step.
Sample response: Step 0: x=0, y=1, slope=1, y_new=1.5. Step 1: x=0.5, y=1.5, slope=2, y_new=2.5. Euler approximation: y(1)≈2.5.
(b) If you instead used a smaller step size like Δx=0.1, would you expect your approximation to be more or less accurate? Explain.
Sample response: More accurate, since smaller steps better follow the true curve, reducing the cumulative drift error from the linear approximation.
(c) MODEL: A population's growth rate is dP/dt=0.1P, P(0)=200. Use Euler's Method with Δt=1 to approximate P(2).
Sample response: Step 0: P=200, slope=0.1(200)=20, P(1)≈200+20=220. Step 1: slope=0.1(220)=22, P(2)≈220+22=242.
