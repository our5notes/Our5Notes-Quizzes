Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the crisis that triggered a severe global recession in 2008.
Sample response: The 2008 financial crisis, shown in the diagram, triggered a severe global recession.
(b) Explain how the September 11 attacks transformed U.S. foreign and domestic policy.
Sample response: The September 11 attacks, the deadliest terrorist attacks on U.S. soil, led directly to the launch of the War on Terror, including military interventions in Afghanistan and later Iraq, fundamentally shifting U.S. foreign policy priorities from the post-Cold War focus on globalization toward counterterrorism; domestically, the attacks also led to new security measures, including the creation of the Department of Homeland Security and increased government surveillance powers, representing a significant and lasting expansion of national security policy.
(c) Explain how the challenges of the 21st century reflect ongoing tensions between different American values and priorities.
Sample response: The major challenges of this period often involve genuine tensions between competing values: the War on Terror raised ongoing debates about balancing national security with civil liberties, the 2008 financial crisis raised questions about the proper balance between free markets and government regulation, and climate change policy debates often involve tension between environmental protection and economic growth concerns; these recurring tensions show how 21st-century challenges frequently require difficult trade-offs between deeply held but sometimes conflicting American values.
