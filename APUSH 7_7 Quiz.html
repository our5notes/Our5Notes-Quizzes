Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the manufacturer who pioneered mass production of automobiles.
Sample response: Henry Ford, shown in the diagram, pioneered mass production of automobiles.
(b) Explain how the automobile transformed American society beyond just transportation.
Sample response: The widespread affordability of automobiles, made possible by Henry Ford's mass production techniques, allowed Americans to live farther from their workplaces, fueling the growth of suburbs; it also boosted related industries like oil, steel, and construction (for new roads), and gave individuals and families new freedom and mobility that reshaped social life, dating culture, and vacation and leisure patterns in ways that went far beyond simply changing how people traveled.
(c) Explain how radio contributed to a new sense of shared national culture in the 1920s.
Sample response: Before radio, entertainment and news were often local or regional, but radio broadcasts allowed the same programs, news, music, and advertising to reach millions of Americans simultaneously across the entire country; this created a new, shared national experience and culture, exposing Americans in different regions to the same trends and information, and helping to nationalize American culture and consumer habits in an unprecedented way.
