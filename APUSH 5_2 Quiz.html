Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one group whose land was threatened by westward expansion.
Sample response: Native nations, shown in the diagram, had their land threatened by westward expansion.
(b) Explain how the ideology of Manifest Destiny provided justification for U.S. territorial expansion.
Sample response: By framing westward expansion as a natural, even divinely ordained destiny rather than a political or economic choice, Manifest Destiny provided a powerful moral and ideological justification for American expansion that helped Americans overlook or minimize the displacement and harm this expansion caused to Native nations and Mexico, framing conquest as fulfillment of destiny rather than aggression.
(c) Explain why westward expansion, despite widespread American enthusiasm for it, inevitably produced conflict.
Sample response: Because the land Americans sought to settle was already inhabited by Native nations or claimed by Mexico, westward expansion necessarily meant displacing or subjugating existing populations, regardless of how expansion was ideologically framed; the resulting competition over land and resources made conflict with Native nations and war with Mexico virtually unavoidable outcomes of the pursuit of Manifest Destiny.
