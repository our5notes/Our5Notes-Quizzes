For y=tan(x/2), a student wants to find the new period. What is the period of tan(x/2)?

Free Response

Use the graph of y=tan(x) shown above.

(a) Identify two consecutive vertical asymptotes and confirm the period.
Sample response: x=−π/2 and x=π/2; period = π.
(b) Find all vertical asymptotes of y=tan(3x) on the interval [0,π].
Sample response: tan(3x) has asymptotes where 3x=π/2+kπ → x=π/6+kπ/3. On [0,π]: x=π/6, π/2, 5π/6.
(c) Evaluate tan(5π/6) exactly using the unit circle or reference angle.
Sample response: Reference angle π/6, QII (tangent negative): tan(5π/6) = −tan(π/6) = −√3/3.
