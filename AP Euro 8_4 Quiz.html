5

Short Answer

Identify ONE major provision of the Treaty of Versailles imposed on Germany.

Sample response: The Treaty of Versailles required Germany to accept blame for the war and pay substantial financial reparations to the Allied powers.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that the goals of Clemenceau and Wilson differed at the Paris Peace Conference.

Sample response: Clemenceau sought a harsh settlement designed to permanently weaken Germany and protect French security, while Wilson favored a more moderate peace grounded in his Fourteen Points and focused on establishing lasting international cooperation.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that the terms of the Versailles settlement contributed to later political instability in Germany.

Sample response: The combination of harsh reparations, territorial losses, and the humiliating war guilt clause created severe economic hardship and intense political resentment in Germany, conditions that extremist political movements later exploited to build support.
