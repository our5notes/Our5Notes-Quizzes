Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify what the West generally wanted federal support for.
Sample response: Internal improvements, shown in the diagram, is what the West generally wanted federal support for.
(b) Explain why the South generally opposed protective tariffs that the Northeast favored.
Sample response: Protective tariffs raised the price of imported manufactured goods to protect Northeastern industry, but the South, which had little manufacturing of its own and relied on imports for many manufactured goods, bore the cost of these higher prices without receiving the benefit of protecting domestic industry; the South also worried that tariffs could invite foreign retaliation against its own crucial cotton exports.
(c) Explain how competing regional economic interests complicated national policymaking in this period.
Sample response: Because the Northeast, South, and West had genuinely different economic interests — favoring tariffs, opposing tariffs, and wanting infrastructure spending, respectively — national policies that benefited one region often came at a real or perceived cost to another; this made building broad coalitions difficult and set a pattern of regional negotiation and compromise (such as through figures like Henry Clay) that would characterize much of the era's politics.
