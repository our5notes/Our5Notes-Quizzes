Historians note that despite the significant political changes occurring in seventeenth-century Europe, such as the English Civil War and the growth of absolutist monarchies elsewhere, certain economic trends, including continued enclosure, the expansion of the putting-out system, and gradual population growth, represented ongoing continuities from earlier centuries rather than dramatic new changes. Which statement best reflects a balanced understanding of economic continuity and change during this period?

B. While significant political changes occurred, certain economic trends reflected continuity with earlier patterns, even as population growth and consumer culture gradually expanded.
