Question 1 Easy

What term describes the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane?

AFacilitated diffusion process
BActive transport process

Osmosis specifically refers to the diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.

Question 3 Medium

In an isotonic solution, what happens to the net movement of water into or out of a cell?

AWater moves rapidly into the cell
BWater moves rapidly out of the cell
CThere is no net water movement
DWater moves in a random spiral
In an isotonic solution, solute concentrations are equal, so there is no net water movement.
Question 4 Hard

A marine fish lives in an environment that is hypertonic relative to its body fluids. Based on this, what osmoregulatory challenge would this fish primarily face?

AConstant water loss to the surrounding hypertonic environment
BConstant water gain from the surrounding hypertonic environment
CNo osmoregulatory challenge, since fish don't lose water
DExcess salt loss through its gills into the environment
In a hypertonic environment, water moves out of the fish's body, so it constantly risks water loss.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify which panel shows a cell in a hypertonic solution.
Sample response: The panel showing the shrunken cell represents a cell in a hypertonic solution.
(b) Explain the direction water moves when a cell is placed in a hypotonic solution.
Sample response: In a hypotonic solution, the solute concentration outside the cell is lower than inside, so water moves into the cell by osmosis, from the area of lower solute concentration to the area of higher solute concentration.
(c) A red blood cell is placed in a hypertonic saline solution. Predict what will happen to the cell, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: The red blood cell will shrink (crenate), because the hypertonic solution has a higher solute concentration outside the cell than inside, causing water to move out of the cell by osmosis, reducing the cell's volume.
