Question 4 Hard

AThey share a more recent common ancestor on the tree
BThey currently live in the same geographic location

Species sharing a more recent common ancestor are considered more closely related.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what a branch point (node) represents in the diagram shown.
Sample response: A branch point (node) in the diagram represents a common ancestor from which descendant lineages diverged.
(b) Explain what it means for two species to be “more closely related” based on a phylogenetic tree's structure.
Sample response: Two species are more closely related if they share a more recent common ancestor, meaning their branches diverge from a node closer to the tips of the tree rather than closer to the root.
(c) Explain why shared derived characteristics, rather than any shared characteristic, are used to construct phylogenetic trees.
Sample response: Shared derived characteristics evolved more recently in a specific common ancestor and are shared only by its descendants, allowing scientists to identify precise, recent relationships, unlike ancestral traits shared broadly across a large, less closely related group.
