Free Response

Use the graph of f(x)=sin(x) and the transformed g(x) shown above.

(a) Describe all transformations applied to produce g(x) from f(x).
Sample response: Reflection over the midline, vertical stretch by a factor of 2, vertical shift up 1.
(b) Write the equation of g(x).
Sample response: g(x) = −2sin(x) + 1.
(c) A function h(x) is formed by taking cos(x), stretching vertically by factor 5, compressing horizontally by factor 1/3 (period becomes 2π/3), and shifting down 4, with no reflection. Write h(x).
Sample response: h(x) = 5cos(3x) − 4.
