Easy

The systematic mass killing of Armenians by the Ottoman government during World War I is known as:

B. The systematic mass killing of Armenians by the Ottoman government during World War I is known as the Armenian Genocide.

Mark for Review
Medium

The Holocaust refers to the systematic, state-organized genocide carried out by Nazi Germany, primarily targeting:

B. The Holocaust primarily targeted European Jews, along with other groups including Roma people, people with disabilities, and political dissidents.

Mark for Review
Medium

Mass atrocities during the twentieth century, such as the Armenian Genocide and the Holocaust, were often carried out using:

B. These mass atrocities were often carried out using organized, state-directed policies and the administrative and technological capacities of a modern government.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Historians note that many mass atrocities of the twentieth century, including the Armenian Genocide and the Holocaust, were carried out through organized, state-directed policies that made use of the administrative, bureaucratic, and technological capacities of a modern government, distinguishing them from more spontaneous forms of historical violence. Which statement best explains the significance of this observation?

B. The organized, bureaucratic, and technological capacities of modern states significantly enabled the scale and systematic nature of twentieth-century mass atrocities.

Short Answer

Explain ONE way that a twentieth-century government used organized, state-directed policies to carry out a mass atrocity.

Short Answer

Explain ONE reason why understanding the organized, bureaucratic nature of twentieth-century mass atrocities is important for historical analysis.

Sample response: Recognizing that modern states used their administrative and technological capacities to organize and carry out mass atrocities helps historians understand how the specific conditions of the modern era enabled violence on an unprecedented, systematic scale.
