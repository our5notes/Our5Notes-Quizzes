Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1968 offensive that shocked American public opinion.
Sample response: The Tet Offensive, shown in the timeline, shocked American public opinion in 1968.
(b) Explain why the Tet Offensive, despite being a military setback for North Vietnam, is considered a turning point in American public opinion.
Sample response: Although U.S. and South Vietnamese forces ultimately repelled the Tet Offensive and inflicted heavy losses on North Vietnamese and Viet Cong forces, the sheer scale and coordination of the attack contradicted repeated official U.S. claims that the war was nearly won; this stark gap between government reassurance and the reality Americans saw on television significantly damaged public trust in the war effort, even though Tet was, in narrow military terms, an American victory.
(c) Explain how the Vietnam War contributed to broader distrust of government in American society.
Sample response: As the gap between official government statements about the war's progress and the difficult reality on the ground became increasingly apparent (especially after events like the Tet Offensive and the later release of the Pentagon Papers), many Americans concluded that government officials had knowingly misled the public about the war's true state and prospects; this experience contributed to a broader decline in public trust in government institutions and leadership that would continue through the Watergate scandal in the following years.
