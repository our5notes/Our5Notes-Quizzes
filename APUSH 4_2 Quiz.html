Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the two parties of the First Party System.
Sample response: The Federalist Party and the Democratic-Republican Party, shown in the diagram, made up the First Party System.
(b) Explain the core philosophical difference between the Federalists and Democratic-Republicans.
Sample response: Federalists generally favored a strong, active central government capable of supporting commerce, manufacturing, and national financial institutions, believing this would best promote national strength and stability; Democratic-Republicans favored a more limited federal government that left more power to the states and individuals, believing this better protected liberty and reflected the interests of the nation's farmers and rural communities.
(c) Explain how the emergence of political parties, which the framers had not anticipated or wanted, shaped early American governance.
Sample response: Although the framers of the Constitution hoped to avoid organized political factions, believing them dangerous to national unity, the deep disagreements over Hamilton's financial policies quickly produced exactly the kind of organized parties they feared; rather than destroying the government, however, this two-party competition became a lasting and stabilizing feature of American politics, channeling political disagreement into organized, peaceful electoral competition.
