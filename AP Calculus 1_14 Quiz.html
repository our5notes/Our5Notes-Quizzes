Free Response

Use the graph of y = n(x) = 1/(x−2) shown above, and the additional functions below.

(a) For f(x) = (2x²+3)/(x²−4), find all vertical asymptotes algebraically. Justify using the numerator and denominator.
Sample response: Denominator is zero at x=±2. Numerator at x=±2: 2(4)+3=11≠0, so both x=2 and x=-2 are true vertical asymptotes.
(b) For g(x) = (x²−4)/(x²−x−6), factor and determine whether x=−2 is a vertical asymptote or a removable discontinuity. Justify.
Sample response: Factor: (x-2)(x+2)/[(x-3)(x+2)]. The (x+2) factor cancels, so x=-2 is a removable discontinuity (a hole), not a vertical asymptote.
(c) Explain the general procedure for distinguishing a vertical asymptote from a removable discontinuity where a rational function's denominator is zero.
Sample response: If, after factoring, the problematic factor cancels between numerator and denominator, that location is a removable discontinuity; if the factor remains only in the denominator without cancelling, that location is a true vertical asymptote.
