"The rate of change of y is proportional to y" translates to:

B. "Proportional to y" means the rate equals a constant times y: dy/dx=ky.

Medium

Newton's Law of Cooling states that the rate of change of an object's temperature is proportional to:

C. Newton's Law of Cooling: dT/dt = k(T-A), proportional to the temperature difference.

Mark for Review
Hard

A population's growth rate is "proportional to both the current population and the amount of unused resources, where total resources are fixed at M." Which differential equation correctly models this?

C. This is the logistic model: rate proportional to the product of P and the remaining capacity (M-P).

Free Response
(a) Translate into a differential equation: "A rumor spreads at a rate proportional to the product of the number who have heard it, y, and the number who have not, (500-y)."
Sample response: dy/dt = ky(500-y).
(b) Translate into a differential equation: "A radioactive substance decays at a rate proportional to the amount present."
Sample response: dy/dt = ky (with k < 0, since the substance is decaying).
(c) MODEL: A tank is filled at a constant rate of 8 gal/min while draining at a rate proportional to the square root of the current volume V. Write a differential equation for dV/dt.
Sample response: dV/dt = 8 - k√V.
