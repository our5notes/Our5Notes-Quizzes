Free Response
(a) Without graphing, describe the end behavior of f(x)=5x⁴−3x²+1.
Sample response: Even degree (4), positive leading coefficient: as x→±∞, f(x)→+∞.
(b) Without graphing, describe the end behavior of g(x)=−4x⁵+2x−7.
Sample response: Odd degree (5), negative leading coefficient: as x→−∞, g(x)→+∞; as x→+∞, g(x)→−∞.
(c) A polynomial has the end behavior: as x→−∞, y→−∞, and as x→+∞, y→+∞. What can you conclude about its degree (odd/even) and the sign of its leading coefficient?
Sample response: Since the two ends point in different directions, the degree must be ODD; since the graph rises to the right, the leading coefficient must be POSITIVE.
