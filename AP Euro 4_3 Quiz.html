Enlightenment thinkers, often referred to as philosophes, generally emphasized the use of:

Baron de Montesquieu's proposal that government power should be divided among separate legislative, executive, and judicial branches is known as:

B. Montesquieu proposed separation of powers, dividing government power among separate legislative, executive, and judicial branches.

Mark for Review
Medium

Adam Smith's economic theory, articulated in The Wealth of Nations, advocated for:

B. Adam Smith advocated for laissez-faire economics, in which government intervention in the economy is minimized.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Historians note that Enlightenment ideas spread not only through philosophical treatises but also through social institutions such as salons, where philosophes and educated elites gathered to discuss new ideas, and through major publishing projects such as Diderot's Encyclopedia, which aimed to compile and disseminate Enlightenment knowledge to a broader public. Which statement best explains the significance of these social and publishing mechanisms for the broader spread of Enlightenment thought?

B. Social institutions like salons and publishing projects like Diderot's Encyclopedia played a significant role in spreading Enlightenment ideas to a broader public.
