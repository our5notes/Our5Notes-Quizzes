Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the document produced at the Seneca Falls Convention.
Sample response: The Declaration of Sentiments, shown in the diagram, was produced at the Seneca Falls Convention.
(b) Explain why the Declaration of Sentiments was deliberately modeled on the Declaration of Independence.
Sample response: By deliberately echoing the language and structure of the Declaration of Independence (“We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men and women are created equal”), the authors of the Declaration of Sentiments powerfully highlighted the gap between America's founding ideals of equality and the actual unequal treatment of women, using the nation's own revered founding document to argue for women's rights.
(c) Explain the connection between the abolitionist and women's rights movements in this period.
Sample response: Many early women's rights activists, including organizers of the Seneca Falls Convention, first became politically active through the abolitionist movement, where they gained organizing experience and encountered frustration at being excluded from full participation (such as being denied speaking roles at some abolitionist conventions) because of their gender; this experience directly inspired many of these women to apply the same arguments about natural rights and equality to their own situation as women.
