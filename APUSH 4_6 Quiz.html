Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the type of labor that increasingly replaced household production.
Sample response: Wage labor, shown in the diagram, increasingly replaced household production during the Market Revolution.
(b) Explain the relationship between the Market Revolution and the emergence of “separate spheres” ideology.
Sample response: As paid work increasingly moved outside the home into factories and offices, a new ideology emerged distinguishing a public, economic “male sphere” from a domestic “female sphere” focused on home and family; this ideology reflected genuine economic changes but also idealized a division of labor that did not match the reality for many working-class families, where women's wage labor remained economically necessary.
(c) Explain how the Market Revolution contributed to growing class divisions in American cities.
Sample response: As factory-based production expanded, a small class of wealthy factory owners and merchants accumulated significant capital, while a growing class of urban wage laborers, often working long hours for low pay in difficult conditions, occupied a very different economic position; this widening gap between industrial wealth and wage labor created new class tensions that had been less visible in the more agrarian society of the earlier period.
