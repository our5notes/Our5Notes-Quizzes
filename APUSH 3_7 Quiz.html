Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the rebellion that exposed the weakness of the national government.
Sample response: Shays' Rebellion, shown in the diagram, exposed the weakness of the national government.
(b) Explain why the framers of the Articles of Confederation deliberately created a weak central government.
Sample response: Having just fought a revolution against what they saw as an overreaching, tyrannical monarchy, the framers of the Articles of Confederation deeply feared concentrating too much power in a new central government; they deliberately limited the national government's authority (denying it the power to tax or directly regulate commerce) to prevent it from becoming a new source of tyranny, prioritizing state sovereignty over national strength.
(c) Explain how Shays' Rebellion changed political leaders' views on the Articles of Confederation.
Sample response: Shays' Rebellion revealed that the national government under the Articles lacked the practical power (and often the funds) to respond effectively to internal unrest, since it could not raise an army or tax to fund one; this alarmed many political leaders, including George Washington, who came to believe that a stronger central government with real enforcement power was necessary to maintain order and stability, directly contributing to calls for the Constitutional Convention.
