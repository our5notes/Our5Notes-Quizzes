Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the organelle where a secreted protein is first synthesized.
Sample response: A secreted protein is first synthesized by ribosomes on the rough endoplasmic reticulum.
(b) Explain the role of transport vesicles in moving material through the endomembrane system.
Sample response: Transport vesicles bud off from one organelle, carrying material such as proteins, and travel to and fuse with the membrane of the next organelle in the pathway, allowing material to move between compartments without being exposed to the general cytoplasm.
(c) Explain one advantage that compartmentalization provides to a eukaryotic cell.
Sample response: Compartmentalization allows a cell to keep incompatible processes separated, such as isolating powerful digestive enzymes within lysosomes, away from the rest of the cytoplasm, preventing those enzymes from damaging the cell's own essential structures.
