Question 4 Hard

Using the diagram shown, explain why the Himalayan rabbit's pigment-producing enzyme is active in its ears but not in its warmer body core.

AThe enzyme is only present in cells located in the ears
BThe enzyme is temperature-sensitive and only active when cool
CThe ears contain a completely different genotype than the core
DThe enzyme requires sunlight exposure to become active
The enzyme is temperature-sensitive, functioning only in the cooler extremities, not the warmer body core.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the three components shown in the diagram's causal chain.
Sample response: The diagram shows genotype, environmental factors, and the resulting phenotype.
(b) Explain how the same genotype can produce different phenotypes in different parts of the Himalayan rabbit's body.
Sample response: The enzyme responsible for dark pigment is temperature-sensitive, functioning only at cooler temperatures; since the rabbit's extremities are cooler than its body core, the enzyme is active there and produces dark fur, while in the warmer core, the same enzyme is inactive, so no pigment is produced, even though every cell has the same genotype.
(c) Explain why understanding environmental effects on phenotype matters when studying the heritability of a trait.
Sample response: If a trait's phenotype can vary due to environmental factors, observing phenotype alone is not enough to determine the underlying genotype, since environment could account for some or all of the variation, so researchers must account for environmental influence to accurately determine a trait's genetic contribution.
