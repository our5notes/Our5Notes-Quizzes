Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what caused the shift shown in the population's trait distribution.
Sample response: Selective pressure from the environment, favoring individuals with a particular trait value, caused the shift shown in the distribution.
(b) Explain how natural selection changes a population's allele frequencies over generations.
Sample response: Individuals with advantageous traits survive and reproduce more successfully than others, passing on the alleles for those traits at a higher rate; over many generations, this shifts the population's allele frequencies toward the advantageous alleles.
(c) Explain why a trait must be heritable, not just advantageous, for natural selection to act on it.
Sample response: If a trait is not genetically based, it cannot be passed on to offspring even if it increases an individual's survival; natural selection can only produce lasting evolutionary change when the advantageous trait is linked to heritable alleles.
