Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one reform movement fueled by the Second Great Awakening.
Sample response: Abolitionism, shown in the diagram, was one reform movement fueled by the Second Great Awakening.
(b) Explain the connection between the Second Great Awakening's religious beliefs and the era's reform movements.
Sample response: The Second Great Awakening's belief that individuals could achieve moral perfection through their own choices and effort translated naturally into a broader belief that society itself could be morally improved or perfected through organized reform; this optimistic, activist religious spirit directly inspired many reformers to organize movements against social “sins” like slavery and alcohol consumption, seeing reform as a religious duty.
(c) Explain why the Second Great Awakening spread especially rapidly on the American frontier.
Sample response: Frontier communities often lacked established churches and formal religious institutions, creating both a spiritual need and an opportunity for the emotional, accessible revival meetings of the Second Great Awakening, which did not require extensive formal religious education to participate in or lead; the dramatic, large-scale camp meetings also provided important social and communal experiences for relatively isolated frontier communities.
