Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the executive order that authorized Japanese American internment.
Sample response: Executive Order 9066, shown in the diagram, authorized Japanese American internment.
(b) Explain the reasoning the U.S. government used to justify Japanese American internment, and why historians now view it as unjust.
Sample response: The government justified internment by citing wartime national security concerns, claiming (without credible evidence) that Japanese Americans might pose an espionage or sabotage risk following the attack on Pearl Harbor; historians and the U.S. government have since recognized this policy as a grave injustice rooted primarily in racial prejudice and wartime hysteria rather than any genuine security threat, especially since German and Italian Americans were not subjected to similar mass internment despite the U.S. also being at war with Germany and Italy.
(c) Explain how WWII mobilization created new, though often temporary, economic opportunities for women and minorities.
Sample response: As millions of men left for military service, the urgent wartime need for factory labor to produce military supplies created significant new job opportunities for women and racial minorities in industries that had often previously excluded them; while many of these gains proved temporary, as many women and minority workers were pushed out of these jobs after the war ended, this wartime experience provided important precedents and expectations that would fuel later civil rights and women's rights movements.
