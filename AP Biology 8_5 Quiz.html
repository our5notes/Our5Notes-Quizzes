Free Response FRQ

Refer to the chart shown above.

(a) Identify which interaction type shown produces a +/+ outcome for both species.
Sample response: Mutualism, shown in the chart, produces a +/+ outcome, benefiting both species.
(b) Explain the difference between mutualism and commensalism, using an example of each.
Sample response: In mutualism, both species benefit, such as bees collecting nectar while pollinating flowers; in commensalism, only one species benefits while the other is largely unaffected, such as birds nesting in a tree without harming it.
(c) Explain why competition for a shared limited resource can lead to one species being competitively excluded.
Sample response: If two species require the same limited resource and one is even slightly more efficient at acquiring it, it will tend to outcompete the other over time, potentially reducing the less efficient species' growth, survival, or reproduction enough to exclude it from the habitat.
