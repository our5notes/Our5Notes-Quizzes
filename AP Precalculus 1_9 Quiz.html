For g(x)=(x²−1)/(x²−3x+2), a student factors to get (x−1)(x+1)/[(x−1)(x−2)]. What are the TRUE vertical asymptote(s), after accounting for any cancellation?

B. After canceling the common (x−1) factor, only x=2 remains as a true vertical asymptote; x=1 becomes a hole.

Free Response

Use the graph of f(x) = 3/(x − 2) shown above.

(a) State the vertical asymptote shown, and describe the function's behavior as x approaches it from each side.
Sample response: x=2. As x→2⁻, f→−∞; as x→2⁺, f→+∞.
(b) Find all vertical asymptotes of g(x)=(x+2)/(x²−16).
Sample response: Denominator zero at x=±4; numerator nonzero there, so both x=4 and x=−4 are vertical asymptotes.
(c) Find all vertical asymptotes of h(x)=(x²−25)/(x²−2x−15), being careful to check for cancellation.
Sample response: Factor: (x−5)(x+5)/[(x−5)(x+3)]. The (x−5) factor cancels (hole at x=5, not an asymptote). The remaining vertical asymptote is at x=−3.
