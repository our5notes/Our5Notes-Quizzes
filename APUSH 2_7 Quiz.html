Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one development that helped connect colonists across distant regions.
Sample response: Growing print culture, shown in the diagram, helped connect colonists across distant regions.
(b) Explain how the Great Awakening challenged traditional sources of authority in colonial society.
Sample response: The Great Awakening emphasized a personal, emotional relationship with God that did not require the mediation of formally educated ministers or established churches, encouraging ordinary colonists to question religious authority and think independently about their own faith; this pattern of questioning established authority would later carry over into colonists' willingness to question political authority as well.
(c) Explain how colonial assemblies contributed to colonists' political development, even while the colonies remained under British rule.
Sample response: Colonial assemblies gave many free colonists direct, practical experience with self-government, including debating laws, raising taxes, and representing local interests; this experience built colonists' confidence in their own capacity for self-rule and created expectations about political representation that would later fuel resistance when Britain attempted to tax and govern the colonies without their consent.
