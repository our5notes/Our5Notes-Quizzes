Easy

Which of the following best describes a Geographic Information System (GIS)?

B. GIS is a computer-based system that captures, stores, and analyzes spatial (geographic) data, often by layering different data sets on top of one another.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

Remote sensing is most useful for which of the following tasks?

B. Remote sensing uses satellites or aircraft to collect data about Earth's surface from a distance, making it well suited for monitoring large-scale physical changes like deforestation.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

A geographer studying farmland loss over the last 20 years overlays satellite images, soil quality data, and property boundary data within a single digital mapping program to identify patterns. Which technology is being used?

C. Layering multiple data sets together to analyze spatial patterns is a defining feature of GIS.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

A researcher wants to determine both the precise, satellite-measured extent of urban sprawl over the last decade and gather qualitative resident perspectives on how sprawl has changed their community. Which combination of data collection methods would best achieve both goals?

B. Remote sensing can precisely measure physical land-use change over time, while fieldwork (interviews) is needed to capture qualitative human perspectives that satellite data cannot provide.

Free Response
A local government wants to decide where to build a new hospital using several types of geographic data.

AIdentify one type of geographic data collection method (GIS, remote sensing, GPS, or fieldwork) the government could use and explain what information it would provide.

Sample response: GIS could be used to layer population density, existing hospital locations, and road networks to identify underserved areas.

BExplain one advantage of using quantitative geospatial data (such as GIS layers) over qualitative fieldwork data for this decision.

Sample response: Quantitative geospatial data can be analyzed objectively across a large area quickly, allowing planners to compare many possible sites efficiently, whereas fieldwork is slower and covers less ground.

CExplain one limitation the government should consider when relying primarily on remote sensing or satellite data for this decision.

Sample response: Remote sensing can show physical features like land use and road access, but it cannot capture community needs, resident preferences, or social factors that only fieldwork or surveys could reveal.
