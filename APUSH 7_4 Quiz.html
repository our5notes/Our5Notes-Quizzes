Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the amendment that established direct election of senators.
Sample response: The 17th Amendment, shown in the diagram, established direct election of senators.
(b) Explain how Progressive reforms attempted to address problems created by industrialization and urbanization.
Sample response: Progressive reformers targeted specific problems created by rapid, largely unregulated industrialization: trust-busting addressed the danger of monopolies stifling competition and exploiting consumers, consumer protection laws like the Pure Food and Drug Act addressed public health dangers from unregulated food and medicine production, and political reforms like the direct election of senators addressed corruption in a political system often controlled by wealthy business interests.
(c) Explain the significance of the 19th Amendment as a culmination of decades of activism.
Sample response: The 19th Amendment's ratification in 1920 represented the culmination of a women's suffrage movement stretching back to the Seneca Falls Convention of 1848; the Progressive Era's broader spirit of reform and expanded political participation provided crucial additional momentum for this long-standing movement, finally achieving a constitutional guarantee of women's voting rights after more than seven decades of organized activism.
