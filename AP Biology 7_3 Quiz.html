Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the ancestral species shown that gave rise to broccoli, cabbage, and kale.
Sample response: Wild mustard (Brassica oleracea), shown in the diagram, is the ancestral species of broccoli, cabbage, and kale.
(b) Explain the key similarity and key difference between artificial selection and natural selection.
Sample response: Both processes select for particular heritable traits over generations, causing a population to change; the key difference is that natural selection is driven by the environment, while artificial selection is driven by human preference.
(c) Explain why artificial selection can produce dramatic changes in relatively few generations.
Sample response: Humans can apply very strong, consistent selective pressure, deliberately breeding only individuals with the most extreme desired traits generation after generation, producing much faster change than the more gradual, variable pressures of natural selection.
