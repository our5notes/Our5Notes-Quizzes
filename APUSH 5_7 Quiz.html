Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the fort whose attack in April 1861 began the Civil War.
Sample response: Fort Sumter, shown in the timeline, was attacked in April 1861, beginning the Civil War.
(b) Explain why Lincoln's election, despite his moderate public position on slavery, triggered Southern secession.
Sample response: Although Lincoln did not campaign on immediately abolishing slavery where it already existed, his firm opposition to slavery's expansion into new territories, combined with his Republican Party's Northern, anti-slavery-expansion base, convinced many Southern leaders that the political balance protecting slavery's future was permanently lost; rather than accept what they saw as an existential long-term threat, several Southern states chose immediate secession.
(c) Explain the significance of the Democratic Party's sectional split in the 1860 election.
Sample response: The Democratic Party, previously the one major party with a genuinely national coalition spanning North and South, split into Northern and Southern factions over the question of slavery's expansion, each nominating its own presidential candidate; this fatal division split the anti-Republican vote, directly enabling Lincoln to win the presidency with a plurality despite very limited Southern support, which Southern leaders then used to justify secession.
