Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what event is occurring in the middle panel of the diagram shown.
Sample response: The middle panel shows crossing over, where non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes exchange segments of DNA.
(b) Explain how crossing over creates allele combinations that did not exist in either original chromosome.
Sample response: Crossing over exchanges segments of DNA between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes, which may carry different alleles for the same genes; combining these segments onto a single new chromosome creates a recombinant chromosome with an allele combination that didn't exist on either original parental chromosome.
(c) Explain how independent assortment, separate from crossing over, contributes to genetic diversity.
Sample response: Independent assortment means that for each homologous pair, either the maternal or paternal chromosome can end up in a given gamete, independently of how other pairs are distributed; since there are many chromosome pairs, this independent distribution creates a huge number of possible chromosome combinations in gametes, separate from any effect of crossing over.
