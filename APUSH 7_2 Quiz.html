Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one argument imperialists made for overseas expansion.
Sample response: The need for new markets and resources, shown in the diagram, is one argument imperialists made for overseas expansion.
(b) Explain the central argument anti-imperialists made against U.S. overseas expansion.
Sample response: Anti-imperialists argued that ruling over other peoples without their consent directly contradicted the core American democratic principle of government by the consent of the governed, the same principle the nation itself had been founded on; they argued that becoming a colonial power made the U.S. hypocritical, since it would be denying to others the very self-government it valued for itself.
(c) Explain how economic motivations shaped the imperialist argument for overseas expansion.
Sample response: Imperialists argued that as U.S. industrial production grew, the nation needed new overseas markets to sell its manufactured goods and new sources of raw materials to fuel continued industrial growth; acquiring overseas territories and increasing international trade influence was seen as essential to sustaining American economic growth and competing with other major imperial powers of the era, like Britain and Germany.
