A tide's height is periodic, with the water level repeating its pattern every 12.4 hours.

(a) If high tide occurs at t=2 hours, at what other time (within the first 24.8 hours) will high tide occur again?
Sample response: t = 2+12.4 = 14.4 hours.
(b) Table shows f(x) values: x=0,1,2,3,4,5,6; f(x)=2,5,2,-1,2,5,2. What is the period of this function?
Sample response: The period is 4 (the pattern 2, 5, 2, -1 repeats every 4 units).
(c) Explain why "periodic" does not necessarily mean "always increasing" or "always decreasing."
Sample response: A periodic function repeats its VALUES over regular intervals, but within each period it can increase, decrease, or do both — periodicity only requires the pattern to repeat, not that the function moves in one consistent direction.
