Free Response FRQ

Refer to the pyramid shown above.

(a) Identify which trophic level shown has the highest concentration of toxins.
Sample response: Top predators, shown at the peak of the pyramid, have the highest concentration of toxins.
(b) Explain why top predators are typically the most severely affected by biomagnification.
Sample response: Toxins accumulate in an organism's tissues faster than they are eliminated; as a predator consumes many prey organisms, the toxin becomes increasingly concentrated with each trophic transfer, so top predators, several levels removed from the source, end up with the highest concentrations.
(c) Explain how eutrophication caused by excess nutrient runoff can disrupt an aquatic ecosystem.
Sample response: Excess nutrients can cause explosive algal growth; when the algae die and decompose, decomposing bacteria consume large amounts of dissolved oxygen, creating a low-oxygen environment that can kill fish and other aquatic organisms.
