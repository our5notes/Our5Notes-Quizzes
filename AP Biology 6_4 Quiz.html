AOnly the single nearest amino acid would be affected

Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the molecule shown that delivers an amino acid to the ribosome.
Sample response: Transfer RNA (tRNA), shown in the diagram, delivers a specific amino acid to the ribosome.
(b) Explain how the specific pairing between a codon and an anticodon ensures the correct amino acid is added to the polypeptide.
Sample response: Each tRNA carries an amino acid that corresponds to the anticodon it carries; when the tRNA's anticodon base-pairs with the complementary mRNA codon, only a tRNA carrying the matching amino acid will fit, ensuring the polypeptide is built in the order specified by the mRNA sequence.
(c) Explain why a frameshift mutation typically has a more severe effect on a protein than a single substitution mutation.
Sample response: A frameshift mutation shifts the reading frame for every codon downstream of the mutation, altering essentially the entire amino acid sequence from that point onward, while a substitution typically affects only a single amino acid, making frameshifts far more disruptive to protein structure.
