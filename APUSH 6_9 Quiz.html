Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the settlement house Jane Addams founded in Chicago.
Sample response: Hull House, shown in the diagram, was the settlement house Jane Addams founded in Chicago.
(b) Explain why the Chinese Exclusion Act specifically targeted Chinese immigrants rather than immigrants more broadly.
Sample response: Chinese immigrants faced especially intense racial hostility and economic scapegoating, particularly on the West Coast, where nativists blamed Chinese laborers for depressing wages and taking jobs from white workers; this specific racial targeting, combined with existing racial prejudice, led Congress to single out Chinese immigrants for exclusion specifically, making it the first major U.S. law to restrict immigration based explicitly on nationality and race.
(c) Explain the goals and methods of settlement houses like Hull House.
Sample response: Settlement houses like Hull House aimed to improve the lives of immigrant and working-class urban communities by providing practical services such as English classes, child care, and job training, while reformers often lived directly within these communities to better understand and address their needs; this approach reflected a belief that direct engagement and support, rather than exclusion or hostility, could help immigrants successfully adapt to American life.
