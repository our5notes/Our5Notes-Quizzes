Free Response
(a) Simplify: cos(x)·tan(x).
Sample response: cos(x)·tan(x) = cos(x)·(sin(x)/cos(x)) = sin(x).
(b) Verify the identity sec²(x)−1=tan²(x) by starting from the Pythagorean identity sin²(x)+cos²(x)=1 and dividing appropriately.
Sample response: Dividing sin²(x)+cos²(x)=1 by cos²(x): tan²(x)+1=sec²(x), so sec²(x)−1=tan²(x).
(c) Simplify: (sin(x)+cos(x))² − (sin(x)−cos(x))². (Hint: expand both squares first.)
Sample response: Expanding: (sin²+2sincos+cos²)−(sin²−2sincos+cos²) = 4sin(x)cos(x), since the sin²+cos² terms cancel.
