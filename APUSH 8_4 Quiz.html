Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the law that provided veterans with low-cost home loans.
Sample response: The GI Bill, shown in the diagram, provided veterans with low-cost home loans.
(b) Explain how the GI Bill contributed to the growth of suburbs after World War II.
Sample response: The GI Bill provided millions of returning veterans with low-cost home loans that made purchasing a home affordable for many working- and middle-class families for the first time; this surge in demand for affordable housing fueled rapid construction of new suburban communities on the outskirts of cities, fundamentally reshaping where and how many Americans lived in the following decades.
(c) Explain how postwar economic growth and suburbanization were mutually reinforcing.
Sample response: Rising wages and economic growth gave more American families the financial means to purchase homes and consumer goods, fueling demand for new suburban housing; in turn, this suburban growth stimulated further economic activity, from construction and highway building to appliance and automobile manufacturing, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of economic growth and suburban expansion throughout the postwar boom.
