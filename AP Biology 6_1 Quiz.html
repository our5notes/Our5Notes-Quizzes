Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify one structural difference between DNA and RNA shown in the diagram.
Sample response: One structural difference shown is that DNA is double-stranded while RNA is single-stranded.
(b) Explain the central dogma of molecular biology, describing the general flow of genetic information.
Sample response: The central dogma describes how genetic information generally flows from DNA to RNA through transcription, and then from RNA to protein through translation.
(c) Explain why DNA's double-stranded structure makes it well-suited for long-term genetic storage, compared to RNA.
Sample response: DNA's double-stranded structure allows each strand to serve as a template for repairing damage or replicating the molecule, providing a stable, protected way to store genetic information, while RNA's single-stranded structure makes it more chemically reactive and better suited for temporary, working copies of genetic instructions.
