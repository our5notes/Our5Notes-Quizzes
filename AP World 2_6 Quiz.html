Easy

The bubonic plague (Black Death), which devastated large portions of Afro-Eurasia during the fourteenth century, spread primarily along:

B. The bubonic plague spread primarily along established trade routes, including the Silk Roads, that connected merchants and travelers.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

Which of the following best explains how increased trade connectivity during this period contributed to the spread of disease?

B. The movement of merchants, animals, and goods along trade routes also inadvertently facilitated the movement of disease-carrying organisms.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

The environmental consequences of increased Afro-Eurasian connectivity during this period included:

B. Environmental consequences included the spread of certain crops, animals, and diseases to new regions as a result of expanded trade and travel.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Historians estimate that the Black Death killed a significant percentage of the population in many regions of Afro-Eurasia during the fourteenth century. Which statement best explains the connection between this environmental and demographic catastrophe and the broader trade networks discussed in this unit?

B. The same trade routes and networks that facilitated beneficial economic and cultural exchange also inadvertently facilitated the rapid spread of the plague across Afro-Eurasia.

Short Answer

Identify ONE disease that spread across Afro-Eurasia as a result of increased trade connectivity during this period.

Sample response: The bubonic plague (Black Death) spread across Afro-Eurasia as a result of increased trade connectivity during this period.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that trade routes such as the Silk Roads contributed to the spread of the Black Death during the fourteenth century.

Sample response: Merchants, travelers, and their animals moving along trade routes like the Silk Roads inadvertently carried disease-infected fleas and rodents with them, spreading the plague rapidly across the vast distances connected by these trade networks.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE demographic or economic consequence of the Black Death for a region affected by the disease.

Sample response: The Black Death caused massive population loss in many affected regions, which in some cases led to labor shortages that increased the bargaining power and wages of surviving peasant and working populations.
