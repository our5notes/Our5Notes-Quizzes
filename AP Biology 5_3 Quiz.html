Question 3 Medium

Mendel's law of segregation states that the two alleles for a gene do what during gamete formation?

AThey combine into a single allele
BThey separate into different gametes
CThey duplicate before separating
DThey mutate randomly each time
The law of segregation states that the two alleles separate into different gametes.

Free Response FRQ

Refer to the Punnett square shown above.

(a) Using the Punnett square shown, identify the expected genotypic ratio from an Aa × Aa cross.
Sample response: The Punnett square shown predicts a genotypic ratio of 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa.
(b) Explain Mendel's law of independent assortment.
Sample response: Mendel's law of independent assortment states that alleles for different genes, located on different chromosomes, are distributed to gametes independently of one another, meaning the inheritance of one trait does not affect the inheritance of another.
(c) Explain how Mendel's law of segregation relates to what physically happens to homologous chromosomes during meiosis.
Sample response: Mendel's law of segregation states that the two alleles for a gene separate during gamete formation, which corresponds directly to what happens during meiosis I, when homologous chromosomes, each carrying one of the two alleles for a gene, are physically separated into different daughter cells.
