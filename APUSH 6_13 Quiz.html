Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the political party struggling farmers formed in 1892.
Sample response: The Populist Party, shown in the timeline, was formed by struggling farmers in 1892.
(b) Explain what drove farmers to organize into the Populist Party in 1892.
Sample response: Farmers faced serious economic hardship in this period from falling crop prices (partly due to overproduction and global competition), high and often unfair railroad shipping rates, and mounting debt exacerbated by a currency system based on the gold standard, which they believed kept currency too scarce; frustrated with major parties' unresponsiveness to these concerns, farmers organized the Populist Party to directly advocate for policies like free silver coinage (increasing the money supply) and railroad regulation.
(c) Explain the significance of the assassination of President Garfield for civil service reform.
Sample response: President Garfield's assassination in 1881 by Charles Guiteau, a mentally disturbed office-seeker angry at being denied a political appointment, dramatically illustrated the dangers of the patronage system in a way ordinary political debate had not; the public shock and outrage following this assassination built crucial political momentum for passing the Pendleton Act just two years later, showing how a single dramatic event can accelerate policy change that had previously stalled.
