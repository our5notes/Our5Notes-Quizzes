In many subsistence farming societies around the world, which of the following best describes women's traditional role in agriculture?

B. Women have historically performed a significant share of agricultural labor in many subsistence farming societies around the world.

In many developing countries, women who perform significant agricultural labor often face which specific disparity compared to men?

B. Women in many developing countries face limited legal rights to land ownership and access to credit and agricultural resources, despite performing significant agricultural labor.

Medium

As agriculture in some regions has shifted from subsistence farming toward commercial, mechanized production, which outcome has research often shown for women's roles in agriculture?

B. Research has often shown that as agriculture becomes more commercialized, women can lose decision-making influence and access to land as ownership becomes more male-dominated.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

A rural region shifts from subsistence agriculture, where women managed most day-to-day farming decisions on small family plots, to large-scale commercial agriculture aimed at export markets, controlled by male landowners with access to bank loans that women in the region are legally unable to obtain. Which outcome does this scenario best illustrate regarding women's role in agricultural change?

B. This scenario illustrates how the shift to commercial agriculture, combined with unequal legal access to land and credit, can reduce women's agricultural influence and economic autonomy.

Free Response
In a rural region transitioning from subsistence to commercial agriculture, women who previously managed most day-to-day decisions on small family farms find themselves increasingly excluded from decision-making as larger, export-oriented commercial farms controlled by male landowners with access to bank credit come to dominate the local economy.

AExplain women's traditional agricultural role in this region prior to the shift toward commercial farming.

Sample response: Prior to the shift, women managed most of the day-to-day agricultural decisions on small family subsistence farms, playing a central role in food production for their households.

BExplain how unequal access to land ownership and credit has contributed to women's declining influence in this scenario.

Sample response: Because women lack legal access to bank credit and land ownership needed to participate in the new commercial farming system, control over agriculture has shifted toward male landowners who can access these resources, reducing women's decision-making influence.

CExplain one policy change that could help address the gender disparities illustrated in this scenario.

Sample response: The government could reform land ownership and credit access laws to guarantee women equal legal rights to own land and obtain agricultural loans, helping them maintain influence and participate more equally in the region's changing agricultural economy.
