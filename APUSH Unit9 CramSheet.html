  • U.S. becomes the world's sole superpower.

Causation in Period 9

⚠

Commonly Missed on the Exam

  • The Cold War's end resulted from both Reagan's military buildup AND his diplomacy with Gorbachev — not either alone.
  • Deindustrialization and globalization are closely linked, not separate trends — companies relocated production overseas as trade barriers fell.
  • The 1965 Immigration Act's effects (from Unit 8) continued shaping immigration patterns well into the 1990s–2000s.
  • Distinguish long-term causes (Cold War's end) from short-term causes (9/11) when building a causation argument for this period.
