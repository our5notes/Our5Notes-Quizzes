Topic 1.5 Quiz: Lipids

Question 1 Easy

What property do all lipids share, due to their nonpolar hydrocarbon structure?

AThey are hydrophilic overall
BThey are hydrophobic overall
CThey are highly charged overall
DThey dissolve easily in water
The nonpolar hydrocarbon chains found in lipids make them hydrophobic (water-fearing).
Question 2 Medium

A triglyceride consists of a glycerol molecule bonded to how many fatty acid chains?

AOne fatty acid chain
BTwo fatty acid chains
CThree fatty acid chains
DFour fatty acid chains
A triglyceride has exactly three fatty acid chains attached to a glycerol backbone.
Question 3 Medium

What structural feature distinguishes an unsaturated fatty acid from a saturated one?

AThe presence of at least one double bond
BThe total number of carbon atoms present
CThe presence of a phosphate group
DThe number of hydroxyl groups present
Unsaturated fatty acids contain at least one carbon-carbon double bond; saturated ones contain none.
Question 4 Hard

Phospholipids spontaneously arrange into a bilayer when placed in water. What property explains this behavior?

AThey contain only hydrophobic regions, which repel each other
BThey are amphipathic, having both hydrophilic and hydrophobic regions
CThey carry a strong negative charge that attracts water
DThey form rigid crystalline structures that resist water entirely
Phospholipids are amphipathic, so their heads face water while their tails cluster together, forming a bilayer.
Free Response FRQ
(a) Explain why triglycerides are considered efficient molecules for long-term energy storage.
Sample response: Triglycerides consist mostly of energy-rich carbon-hydrogen bonds, and their hydrophobic nature allows them to be stored compactly without attracting water molecules, making them a dense and efficient way to store energy compared to carbohydrates.
(b) Describe the structural difference between a saturated and an unsaturated fat.
Sample response: A saturated fat has no double bonds between the carbons in its fatty acid chains, allowing the chains to pack tightly together, while an unsaturated fat has at least one double bond, which creates a kink that prevents tight packing.
(c) Explain why the hydrophilic heads of phospholipids face outward toward water on both sides of a cell membrane.
Sample response: Since the phospholipid heads are polar (hydrophilic) and the surrounding fluid on both sides of the membrane is water-based, the heads orient outward to interact favorably with water, while the nonpolar tails cluster together in the interior, away from water, forming the stable hydrophobic core of the membrane.
