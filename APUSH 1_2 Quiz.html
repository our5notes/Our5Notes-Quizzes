Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify one society shown in the diagram and describe one of its key characteristics.
Sample response: The Iroquois Confederacy, shown in the diagram, united multiple Native nations in the Northeast under a shared political system.
(b) Explain why Native American societies developed such diverse political and economic structures across different regions of North America.
Sample response: Native societies adapted their political, economic, and social structures to the specific environments and resources available to them — desert-dwelling peoples like the Pueblo developed irrigation agriculture suited to arid conditions, while Mississippian peoples took advantage of fertile river valleys to support large population centers, showing that environment strongly shaped social organization.
(c) Explain how the Native American concept of land use commonly differed from the European concept that would later arrive.
Sample response: Many Native societies viewed land as something to be used reciprocally and shared communally, rather than owned individually as private property; this fundamental difference in worldview would become a major source of conflict once Europeans, who generally viewed land as a commodity to be owned and bought or sold, arrived in the Americas.
