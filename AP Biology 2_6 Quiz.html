Question 4 Hard

AGlucose transport rate would decrease, despite a favorable gradient
BGlucose transport rate would increase to compensate automatically

Facilitated diffusion depends on available transport proteins, so fewer GLUT proteins limits the transport rate.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify the type of transport protein shown that forms a pore through the membrane.
Sample response: The protein shown forming a pore through the membrane is a channel protein.
(b) Explain why facilitated diffusion is still classified as passive transport, despite requiring a protein.
Sample response: Facilitated diffusion is classified as passive transport because the substance still moves down its concentration gradient and the cell does not expend ATP to power the movement; the protein simply provides a pathway, without driving the transport itself.
(c) Compare how a channel protein and a carrier protein differ in how they move a molecule across the membrane.
Sample response: A channel protein forms a continuous open pore that specific molecules or ions pass directly through, while a carrier protein binds to a specific molecule and physically changes shape to shuttle that molecule from one side of the membrane to the other.
