Use the graph of f on [-1,7] shown above.

(a) Identify the x-coordinates of all critical points and classify each (smooth extremum vs. corner).
Sample response: x=1 is a smooth turning point (local max). x=3 is a corner, where the derivative is undefined (local min).
(b) State the global maximum and global minimum values of f on [-1,7].
Sample response: Global maximum: (7, 8.7), at the right endpoint. Global minimum: (-1, 0), at the left endpoint.
(c) Explain, in general, why every local extremum must occur at a critical point, but not every critical point is a local extremum.
Sample response: A local extremum requires the derivative to change sign, which can only happen where the derivative is zero or undefined — i.e., at a critical point. But a critical point doesn't guarantee a sign change; the derivative could touch zero (or be undefined) without the function actually switching from increasing to decreasing.
