Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which panel in the diagram represents paracrine signaling.
Sample response: The panel showing a cell releasing molecules that diffuse to nearby cells represents paracrine signaling.
(b) Explain the difference between paracrine and endocrine signaling in terms of signal distance.
Sample response: Paracrine signaling involves a signal traveling only a short distance through the extracellular fluid to nearby cells, while endocrine signaling involves a hormone traveling a long distance through the bloodstream to reach distant target cells.
(c) Explain why a cell without the appropriate receptor cannot respond to a given hormone, even if the hormone reaches that cell.
Sample response: A cell can only detect and respond to a signal if it has the specific receptor protein that binds that signal molecule; without the matching receptor, the cell has no way to recognize the hormone is present, so it cannot initiate a response even if the hormone physically reaches it.
