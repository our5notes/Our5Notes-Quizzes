Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one group that remained excluded from suffrage despite its expansion.
Sample response: Women, shown in the diagram, remained excluded from suffrage despite its expansion in this period.
(b) Explain why the expansion of suffrage in this period is often described as “universal white male suffrage” rather than simply “universal suffrage.”
Sample response: Although property requirements for voting were eliminated for white men, allowing nearly all white men to vote regardless of wealth, this expansion of democracy explicitly excluded women entirely and, in most states, excluded free Black men as well; the term “universal white male suffrage” accurately reflects that this was a real but limited democratic expansion, not a truly universal one.
(c) Explain how the elimination of property requirements changed the nature of American political campaigning.
Sample response: As suffrage expanded to include men without significant property, political candidates increasingly needed to appeal directly to a much larger and more diverse electorate rather than a small elite of property owners; this contributed to a new style of mass political campaigning, including rallies, parades, and popular appeals, that would become a defining feature of the Jacksonian era's political culture.
