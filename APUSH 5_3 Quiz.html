Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1848 treaty that ended the Mexican-American War.
Sample response: The Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, shown in the timeline, ended the Mexican-American War in 1848.
(b) Explain why the annexation of Texas contributed to tensions leading to the Mexican-American War.
Sample response: The U.S. annexation of Texas in 1845 angered Mexico, which had never formally recognized Texan independence, and a subsequent dispute over the exact location of the Texas-Mexico border (the Rio Grande vs. the Nueces River) provided the immediate spark for military conflict; underlying this dispute was the broader American desire for further territorial expansion, which many Mexican leaders rightly saw as a serious threat.
(c) Explain how the territorial gains from the Mexican-American War reignited national conflict over slavery.
Sample response: The vast new territory gained through the Mexican Cession immediately raised the same unresolved question that had caused conflict before: would these new territories permit slavery? Since this new land was so large, the stakes of this question were especially high, and proposals like the Wilmot Proviso (which would have banned slavery in the new territory) sparked intense sectional conflict, showing how territorial expansion and the slavery debate remained inseparably connected.
