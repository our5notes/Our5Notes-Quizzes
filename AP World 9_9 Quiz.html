Topic 9.9: Continuity and Change in a Globalized World

AP World History: Modern · Unit 9

4 multiple-choice questions (1 easy, 2 medium, 1 hard) + 3 standalone short-answer prompts. Click an answer to check it; click "Show sample response" to check your short-answer responses.

1
Mark for Review
Easy

When historians evaluate what aspects of global society, politics, and economics have changed versus remained the same from 1900 to the present, they are applying which historical thinking skill?

B. Evaluating what changed versus remained the same applies the historical thinking skill of continuity and change over time.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

One significant change in the global economy from 1900 to the present has been:

B. A significant change has been the development of significantly greater global economic interdependence and integration through trade, institutions, and multinational corporations.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

One significant continuity that has persisted from 1900 to the present, despite considerable global change, is:

B. A significant continuity has been the persistence of significant economic inequality between and within different countries and regions.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

A historian evaluating the period from 1900 to the present notes that while dramatic changes have occurred, such as decolonization, the end of the Cold War, and unprecedented technological and economic global integration, significant continuities have also persisted, such as ongoing economic inequality, nationalism, and social and environmental challenges. Which statement best reflects a balanced application of the continuity and change over time skill to this period?

C. A balanced application recognizes both significant changes, like decolonization and economic integration, and important continuities, like persistent economic inequality and nationalism.

Short Answer Practice

5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Identify ONE significant change in global society, politics, or economics that occurred between 1900 and the present.

Sample response: The development of significantly greater global economic interdependence and integration through trade, international institutions, and multinational corporations was a significant change during this period.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that economic inequality has demonstrated continuity from 1900 to the present, despite considerable global change.

Sample response: Despite significant technological and economic changes, substantial economic inequality has persisted both between and within different countries and regions throughout this period, continuing a pattern of unequal global economic development.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that nationalism has demonstrated continuity as a significant political force from 1900 to the present.

Sample response: Nationalism has continued to shape political developments throughout this period, from its role in decolonization movements to its continued influence in contemporary political movements resisting global integration, demonstrating its persistence as a significant political force.
