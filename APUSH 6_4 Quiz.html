Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one reality that fell short of the “New South” promise.
Sample response: The persistence of an agricultural, cotton-based economy, shown in the diagram, fell short of the “New South” promise.
(b) Explain the gap between the promise of the “New South” and its economic reality.
Sample response: Southern boosters promised a diversified, industrialized economy that would rival the North's, but in reality the South remained heavily dependent on cotton agriculture, and systems like sharecropping and tenant farming kept many Black and poor white farmers trapped in ongoing cycles of debt rather than achieving genuine economic independence or prosperity; whatever industrial growth did occur remained limited compared to the North's continued economic dominance.
(c) Explain how Jim Crow laws and disenfranchisement tactics undermined the constitutional gains of Reconstruction.
Sample response: Although the 14th and 15th Amendments had constitutionally guaranteed citizenship, equal protection, and voting rights, Southern states used Jim Crow laws to enforce rigid racial segregation in nearly all public spaces, while tactics like poll taxes, literacy tests, and grandfather clauses effectively disenfranchised most Black voters despite their technical constitutional right to vote; these state-level actions systematically undermined Reconstruction's legal gains for generations.
