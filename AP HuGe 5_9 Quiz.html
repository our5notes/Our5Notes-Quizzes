Free Response
A small developing country earns the majority of its export revenue from a single cash crop sold through a global commodity chain to wealthy importing countries, with much of the processing and marketing controlled by large multinational agribusiness corporations.

AExplain why this country's economy is vulnerable due to high export commodity dependence.

Sample response: Because the country relies so heavily on a single cash crop for export revenue, any drop in the global price of that crop could severely damage the country's overall economy, with no other major export to offset the loss.

BDescribe the role multinational agribusiness corporations play in this country's commodity chain.

Sample response: Multinational agribusiness corporations control much of the processing and marketing of the crop, meaning they capture a significant share of the value added along the commodity chain, often leaving the producing country with a smaller share of the overall profit.

CExplain one strategy this country's government could pursue to reduce its economic vulnerability to fluctuations in the global price of this single crop.

Sample response: The government could encourage agricultural diversification, supporting farmers in growing additional crops or developing other industries, to reduce the country's economic dependence on a single export commodity.
