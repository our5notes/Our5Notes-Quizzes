(a) Given r(t)=, find r'(t).
Sample response: r'(t) = <3t², 4>.
(b) Using your answer to (a), evaluate r'(1) and describe what this vector represents.
Sample response: r'(1) = <3,4>. This represents the particle's velocity vector at t=1, pointing in the direction of motion at that instant.
(c) MODEL: A hiker's position is r(t)=<3t,4t> (t in hours). Find the hiker's speed, |r'(t)|, and explain why it is constant.
Sample response: r'(t)=<3,4> for all t. |r'(t)|=√(9+16)=5. The speed is constant because the hiker's velocity components don't change with time (straight-line motion at constant rate).
