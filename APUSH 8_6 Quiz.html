Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1954 case that ruled segregated schools unconstitutional.
Sample response: Brown v. Board of Education, shown in the timeline, ruled segregated schools unconstitutional in 1954.
(b) Explain the significance of the Brown v. Board decision for the broader Civil Rights Movement.
Sample response: By overturning the “separate but equal” doctrine established in Plessy v. Ferguson, Brown v. Board provided crucial legal momentum for the broader Civil Rights Movement, establishing that legally enforced racial segregation was unconstitutional; while the decision alone did not immediately end segregation in practice, it gave civil rights activists a powerful legal precedent to challenge segregation in other areas of American life beyond just public schools.
(c) Explain why the Montgomery Bus Boycott is considered an important early success of organized nonviolent protest.
Sample response: The Montgomery Bus Boycott demonstrated that a sustained, organized, nonviolent protest — in this case, a yearlong boycott of the city's segregated bus system — could successfully pressure segregationist institutions to change, since the boycott caused significant financial harm to the bus system and ultimately led to a Supreme Court ruling declaring bus segregation unconstitutional; the boycott's success also brought Martin Luther King Jr. to national prominence as a leader, shaping the strategy and leadership of the broader movement going forward.
