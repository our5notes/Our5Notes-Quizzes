Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one Northern development inspired by revolutionary ideals of equality.
Sample response: The gradual abolition of slavery in several Northern states, shown in the diagram, was inspired by revolutionary ideals of equality.
(b) Explain the limits of revolutionary ideals of liberty and equality in practice.
Sample response: Despite the Declaration's assertion that “all men are created equal,” revolutionary ideals of equality did not extend to enslaved people, particularly in the South, where slavery not only continued but expanded; women's political rights remained extremely limited, and most Native Americans were excluded from and often actively harmed by the new nation's expansion, showing a significant gap between revolutionary rhetoric and reality.
(c) Explain how the concept of Republican Motherhood both expanded and limited women's roles after the Revolution.
Sample response: Republican Motherhood expanded women's perceived importance by framing their domestic role as essential to the nation's civic health, since virtuous mothers were seen as responsible for raising the next generation of informed citizens; however, it simultaneously limited women's roles by confining this new importance to the domestic sphere, rather than extending formal political rights like voting or holding office.
