Free Response FRQ

Refer to the graph shown above.

(a) Identify which curve shown represents an ectotherm's body temperature.
Sample response: The curve that closely follows the environmental temperature line represents an ectotherm's body temperature.
(b) Explain the energetic trade-off between being an endotherm versus an ectotherm.
Sample response: Endotherms expend significant metabolic energy maintaining a constant body temperature, requiring more food, but can remain active across a wide range of temperatures; ectotherms expend far less energy on temperature regulation, surviving on less food, but their activity depends heavily on external temperature.
(c) Explain why behavioral and physiological responses to the environment are shaped by natural selection.
Sample response: Organisms that respond effectively to environmental changes are more likely to survive and reproduce successfully; since these responses are often heritable, natural selection favors individuals with response mechanisms that improve fitness, shaping a population's typical responses over generations.
