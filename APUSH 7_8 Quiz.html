Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the cultural movement that celebrated Black art, literature, and music.
Sample response: The Harlem Renaissance, shown in the diagram, celebrated Black art, literature, and music in the 1920s.
(b) Explain what the Scopes Trial reveals about broader cultural tensions in the 1920s.
Sample response: The Scopes Trial, which tested a Tennessee law banning the teaching of evolution in public schools, became a highly publicized symbol of the broader clash between religious traditionalists, who saw the teaching of evolution as a threat to religious faith and traditional values, and modernists, who championed scientific inquiry and the theory of evolution; the intense national attention the trial received reflected genuine, deep cultural anxiety about how rapidly American society and values were changing.
(c) Explain why Prohibition ultimately proved difficult to enforce and increasingly controversial.
Sample response: Despite Prohibition's goal of eliminating alcohol consumption to address social problems associated with drinking, demand for alcohol remained high, leading to widespread illegal production and sale (bootlegging) and the rise of organized crime syndicates that profited enormously from this illegal trade; the widespread, open violation of Prohibition laws, along with the violence associated with organized crime, gradually eroded public support and enforcement effectiveness, contributing to the amendment's eventual repeal in 1933.
