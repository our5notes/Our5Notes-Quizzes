Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the enzyme shown responsible for synthesizing pre-mRNA from a DNA template.
Sample response: RNA polymerase, shown in the diagram, synthesizes pre-mRNA from the DNA template.
(b) Explain the purpose of adding a 5' cap and poly-A tail to a pre-mRNA transcript.
Sample response: The 5' cap and poly-A tail both help protect the mRNA transcript from degradation, extending its lifespan; the 5' cap also helps the ribosome recognize the mRNA to begin translation, while the poly-A tail assists with nuclear export.
(c) Explain what happens to introns during RNA processing, and why this step is necessary.
Sample response: Introns, the non-coding regions of the pre-mRNA, are removed during splicing, leaving only the coding exons joined together; this step is necessary because introns do not code for protein and must be removed to produce a functional, translatable mature mRNA.
