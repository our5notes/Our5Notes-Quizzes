Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) List the four stages of cellular respiration shown in the diagram, in order.
Sample response: The four stages shown are glycolysis, pyruvate oxidation, the Krebs cycle, and the electron transport chain.
(b) Explain why the electron transport chain produces far more ATP than glycolysis alone.
Sample response: The electron transport chain uses the many electron carriers (NADH and FADH2) generated by earlier stages to create a proton gradient that powers ATP synthase, generating far more ATP than the small, direct amount produced during glycolysis's substrate-level phosphorylation.
(c) Explain why the Krebs cycle and electron transport chain are described as closely interconnected.
Sample response: The Krebs cycle generates electron carriers by removing high-energy electrons from breakdown products of glucose; these carriers then deliver their electrons directly to the electron transport chain, which uses them to generate the proton gradient that powers ATP synthase, so without the Krebs cycle's electron carriers, the chain would have no electron source to drive ATP production.
