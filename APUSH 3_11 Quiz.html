Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify what helped standardize a shared American language.
Sample response: Noah Webster's dictionary, shown in the diagram, helped standardize a shared American language.
(b) Explain why the emergence of political parties in the 1790s complicates the idea of a single, unified American identity in this period.
Sample response: The sharp disagreements between Federalists (who favored a strong central government and commercial interests) and Democratic-Republicans (who favored limited government and agrarian interests) show that Americans in this period held deeply different visions for the nation's future; this suggests that a unified “American identity” was still very much in formation and contested, rather than a settled, shared understanding.
(c) Explain how cultural developments like Webster's dictionary contributed to a sense of national identity.
Sample response: By standardizing distinctly American spellings and usage, Webster's dictionary helped create a shared linguistic identity that distinguished Americans from their former British rulers; this kind of cultural development, alongside efforts to create distinctly American art and literature, helped build a sense of shared national culture that could unite a geographically and politically diverse population.
