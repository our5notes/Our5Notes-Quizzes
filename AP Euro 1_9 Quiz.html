The forced transportation of enslaved Africans across the Atlantic Ocean to work primarily on American plantations is known as:

B. The Middle Passage refers to the extremely dangerous and often fatal ocean voyage transporting enslaved Africans to the Americas.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

The demand for enslaved African labor during this period was driven significantly by:

B. The demand for enslaved African labor was driven significantly by the growth of labor-intensive plantation economies producing cash crops such as sugar.
4
Mark for Review
Hard

Historians note that the growth of the transatlantic slave trade during this period was closely connected to the broader development of European colonial plantation economies in the Americas, particularly the labor-intensive cultivation of cash crops such as sugar, which colonizers increasingly filled with forced African labor following the demographic collapse of Indigenous populations from disease. Which statement best explains this connection between the Columbian Exchange, colonial economies, and the growth of the slave trade?

B. Severe Indigenous population decline, combined with growing demand for plantation cash crops, created labor demand that colonizers increasingly filled through the forced enslavement of Africans.
