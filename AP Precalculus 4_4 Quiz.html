(a) Write parametric equations for a circle of radius 7 centered at the origin.
Sample response: x(t) = 7cos(t), y(t) = 7sin(t).
(b) Write parametric equations for a circle of radius 3 centered at (−1,4).
Sample response: x(t) = −1+3cos(t), y(t) = 4+3sin(t).
(c) Write parametric equations for the line passing through (0,5) with direction numbers (2,−3), and find the point on the line at t=4.
Sample response: x(t)=2t, y(t)=5−3t. At t=4: x=8, y=5−12=−7. Point (8, −7).
