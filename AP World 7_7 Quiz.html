Historians note that World War II was conducted on an even larger and more destructive scale than World War I, involving new military technologies and strategies such as blitzkrieg and atomic weapons, extensive mobilization of civilian populations and economies, and combat across multiple global theaters spanning Europe, North Africa, and the Pacific. Which statement best captures the significance of this expanded scale and destructiveness?

B. World War II's expanded global scope, new destructive technologies, and extensive civilian mobilization reflected an even greater intensification of "total war" compared to World War I.

Explain ONE way that the use of atomic weapons in 1945 affected the conclusion of World War II or postwar international relations.

Sample response: The use of atomic weapons against Japan contributed to Japan's surrender, ending World War II, while also introducing an unprecedented new scale of destructive military technology that would significantly shape postwar international relations and the nuclear arms race.
