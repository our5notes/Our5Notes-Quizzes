A projectile's position is given by x(t)=25t, y(t)=20t−5t², t in seconds.

(a) Find the projectile's position at t=0.5, t=1, and t=1.5.
Sample response: t=0.5: (12.5, 7.5). t=1: (25, 15). t=1.5: (37.5, 15).
(b) Determine when the projectile lands (y=0 for t>0).
Sample response: 20t−5t²=0 → t(20−5t)=0 → t=0 or t=4. It lands at t=4.
(c) Find the projectile's maximum height, and the time at which it occurs.
Sample response: The maximum occurs halfway between t=0 and t=4, i.e., at t=2. y(2)=40−20=20. Maximum height is 20 feet at t=2 seconds.
