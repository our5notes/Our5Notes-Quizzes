Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1877 political deal that ended Reconstruction.
Sample response: The Compromise of 1877, shown in the diagram, ended Reconstruction.
(b) Explain how Black Codes undermined the intent of the 13th Amendment.
Sample response: Although the 13th Amendment formally abolished slavery, Southern states quickly passed Black Codes that restricted formerly enslaved people's freedom of movement, labor contracts, and legal rights in ways that closely resembled conditions under slavery; these laws showed that formal legal freedom did not automatically translate into substantive equality or liberty, revealing the limits of constitutional change without sustained enforcement.
(c) Explain why the Compromise of 1877 is considered the effective end of Reconstruction.
Sample response: The Compromise of 1877 resolved the disputed 1876 presidential election by awarding the presidency to Rutherford B. Hayes in exchange for withdrawing remaining federal troops from the South; without federal troops to enforce Reconstruction-era laws and protect Black political participation, Southern state governments quickly moved to restrict Black rights through segregation and disenfranchisement, effectively ending the Reconstruction-era push for racial equality for generations.
