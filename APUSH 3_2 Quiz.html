Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the treaty that ended the Seven Years' War in 1763.
Sample response: The Treaty of Paris, shown in the diagram, ended the Seven Years' War in 1763.
(b) Explain why Britain's victory in the Seven Years' War ultimately contributed to tension with its own colonies.
Sample response: Although Britain won enormous new territory in the Seven Years' War, the war also left it with a massive debt; Parliament's decision to raise revenue by taxing the colonies directly (rather than relying on colonial self-taxation) violated colonists' expectations of self-government, planting the seeds of resistance that would grow throughout the following decade.
(c) Explain the purpose and colonial reaction to the Proclamation of 1763.
Sample response: The Proclamation of 1763 aimed to reduce conflict with Native nations by forbidding colonial settlement west of the Appalachian Mountains; many colonists deeply resented this restriction, since they felt entitled to settle land won in the war, and the Proclamation became an early source of colonial frustration with British policy.
