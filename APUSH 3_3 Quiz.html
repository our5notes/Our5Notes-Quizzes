Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1773 event that led to the Intolerable Acts.
Sample response: The Boston Tea Party, shown in the timeline, led to the Intolerable Acts.
(b) Explain the colonial argument of “no taxation without representation.”
Sample response: Colonists argued that, as Englishmen, they were entitled to the same rights as those living in Britain, including the principle that taxes could only be levied by a legislature in which they had elected representation; since the colonies had no elected members in Parliament, colonists argued that Parliament's direct taxes on them were illegitimate, regardless of the tax's specific purpose.
(c) Explain why the Intolerable Acts had the opposite of their intended effect on colonial unity.
Sample response: The Intolerable Acts were intended to punish and isolate Massachusetts for the Boston Tea Party, but instead of intimidating the other colonies into compliance, the harshness of the punishment convinced colonists elsewhere that any colony could face similar treatment; this fear helped unite the colonies in opposition to British policy, leading directly to the convening of the First Continental Congress.
