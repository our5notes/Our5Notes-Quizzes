Topic 3.4 Quiz: Photosynthesis

Figure for Questions Below
Photosynthesis has two interconnected stages: the light reactions (thylakoid) and the Calvin cycle (stroma).
Question 1 Easy

In which part of the chloroplast do the light-dependent reactions take place?

AThe stroma region
BThe thylakoid membrane
CThe outer membrane
DThe intermembrane space
The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membrane.
Question 2 Medium

Using the diagram shown, what two molecules does the Calvin cycle receive from the light reactions?

AOxygen and glucose molecules
BATP and NADPH molecules
CWater and carbon dioxide molecules
DCarbon dioxide and oxygen molecules
The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH supplied by the light reactions.
Question 3 Medium

What gas is released as a byproduct when water is split during the light reactions?

ACarbon dioxide gas
BNitrogen gas
COxygen gas
DHydrogen gas
Splitting water during the light reactions releases oxygen gas as a byproduct.
Question 4 Hard

A plant is placed in complete darkness for an extended period. Based on the diagram shown, what would most likely happen to the Calvin cycle?

AIt would speed up to compensate for the lack of light
BIt would eventually stop once ATP and NADPH run out
CIt would continue indefinitely since it needs no light directly
DIt would switch to using oxygen as its main energy source
Without light, the light reactions stop producing ATP and NADPH, so the Calvin cycle eventually halts.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the two products of the light reactions that are used by the Calvin cycle, based on the diagram shown.
Sample response: The light reactions supply the Calvin cycle with ATP and NADPH.
(b) Explain how the Calvin cycle depends on the light reactions, even though it does not directly require light.
Sample response: The Calvin cycle depends on ATP and NADPH, both of which are produced by the light-dependent reactions; without a continuous supply of these energy carriers, the Calvin cycle would run out of the energy and reducing power it needs to fix carbon dioxide into organic molecules.
(c) Explain what happens to the ADP and NADP+ produced by the Calvin cycle, based on the diagram shown.
Sample response: The ADP and NADP+ produced by the Calvin cycle are recycled back to the light reactions, where they are reconverted into ATP and NADPH, allowing the two stages to continue supporting each other in an ongoing cycle.
