Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the political movement that gained strength through the 1970s.
Sample response: The New Right, shown in the diagram, gained strength through the 1970s.
(b) Explain how Watergate and stagflation together undermined confidence in the postwar liberal consensus.
Sample response: Watergate revealed that a sitting president had abused his power and then lied to cover it up, severely damaging public trust in the competence and integrity of the federal government; around the same time, stagflation's combination of high unemployment and high inflation defied the expectations of prevailing liberal economic theory, which held that these two problems generally did not occur together, undermining confidence in the government's ability to successfully manage the economy through active intervention.
(c) Explain how the rising conservative movement of the 1970s positioned itself as an alternative to failing liberal policies.
Sample response: As trust in active federal government and liberal economic management declined due to Watergate and stagflation, the rising New Right conservative movement offered an alternative vision emphasizing reduced government spending and regulation, free-market economic solutions, and a return to traditional social and cultural values; this movement successfully positioned itself as a solution to problems that many Americans felt liberal policies had failed to solve, building the political coalition that would help elect Ronald Reagan in 1980.
