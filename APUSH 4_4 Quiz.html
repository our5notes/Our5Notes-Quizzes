Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the 1823 policy asserting U.S. hemispheric influence.
Sample response: The Monroe Doctrine, shown in the timeline, asserted U.S. hemispheric influence in 1823.
(b) Explain why the Embargo Act, despite its goal of avoiding war, proved deeply unpopular.
Sample response: The Embargo Act aimed to pressure Britain and France by cutting off American trade rather than risking war, but it severely damaged the American economy, particularly hurting merchants, shippers, and farmers who depended on foreign trade; because the embargo caused significant domestic economic pain without achieving its diplomatic goals, it proved deeply unpopular and was eventually repealed.
(c) Explain the significance of the Monroe Doctrine for U.S. foreign policy going forward.
Sample response: The Monroe Doctrine asserted that the United States considered the Western Hemisphere its own sphere of influence and would view further European colonization there as a threat to its security; although the U.S. lacked the military power to fully enforce this doctrine at the time, it established a long-lasting foreign policy principle that later presidents would repeatedly invoke to justify American involvement in Latin American affairs.
