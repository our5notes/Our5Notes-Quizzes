(a) Apply the matrix [[1,0],[0,−1]] to the point (3,5) (as a vector). Describe the transformation.
Sample response: [[1,0],[0,−1]]·<3,5> = <3,−5>. This is a reflection over the x-axis.
(b) Apply the matrix [[0.5,0],[0,0.5]] to the vector <8,−4>. What transformation does this represent?
Sample response: [[0.5,0],[0,0.5]]·<8,−4> = <4,−2>. This represents a dilation (compression) by a factor of 0.5.
(c) Using the figure shown above, explain why the matrix [[2,1],[0,1]] produces a "shear" transformation rather than a simple stretch.
Sample response: The matrix sends (x,y) to (2x+y, y). The bottom edge (y=0) becomes (2x,0), a simple horizontal scaling. But the top edge (y=1) becomes (2x+1,1), shifted further to the right compared to the bottom — this differing horizontal shift between top and bottom edges is what creates the slanted parallelogram shape, the defining feature of a shear.
