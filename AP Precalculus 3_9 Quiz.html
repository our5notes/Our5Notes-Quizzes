(a) Evaluate arctan(1) exactly, expressing your answer as an angle in the correct range.
Sample response: arctan(1) = π/4.
(b) Evaluate cos(arcsin(5/13)) exactly. (Hint: draw a right triangle with the given sine ratio.)
Sample response: If sin(θ)=5/13, draw a right triangle: opposite=5, hypotenuse=13, adjacent=√(169−25)=√144=12. cos(θ) = 12/13.
(c) Explain why arcsin(sin(π)) does NOT equal π, and find the correct value.
Sample response: Since arcsin's range is restricted to [−π/2,π/2], and π is outside this range, arcsin(sin(π)) doesn't return π. Since sin(π)=0, arcsin(0)=0, so the correct value is 0.
