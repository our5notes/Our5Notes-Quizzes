Topic 2.3 Quiz: Plasma Membranes

Figure for Questions Below
The fluid mosaic model: a phospholipid bilayer embedded with integral and peripheral proteins, cholesterol, and a glycoprotein.
Question 1 Easy

What model describes the plasma membrane as a flexible bilayer embedded with a variety of proteins?

ARigid frame model
BFluid mosaic model
CStatic lattice model
DSolid crystal model
The fluid mosaic model describes the membrane as a fluid bilayer with a mosaic of embedded proteins.
Question 2 Medium

Using the diagram shown, what is the primary function of the molecule labeled cholesterol within the bilayer?

AIt transports large polar molecules
BIt regulates membrane fluidity
CIt catalyzes chemical reactions
DIt stores genetic information
Cholesterol helps regulate the fluidity of the membrane, especially at varying temperatures.
Question 3 Medium

A protein that spans the entire width of the membrane is called a(n):

APeripheral protein
BGlycoprotein marker
CIntegral protein
DCytoskeletal protein
Integral proteins span the entire width of the phospholipid bilayer.
Question 4 Hard

A researcher lowers the temperature of a cell membrane significantly. Based on the fluid mosaic model, what change would most likely occur?

AThe membrane becomes more fluid and less structured
BThe membrane becomes more rigid as phospholipids pack tighter
CThe membrane dissolves completely into surrounding fluid
DThe membrane's proteins detach and float away entirely
Lower temperatures cause phospholipids to pack more closely together, making the membrane more rigid.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the membrane diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the type of protein labeled that spans the entire membrane.
Sample response: The labeled protein spanning the entire membrane is an integral protein.
(b) Explain the role of the carbohydrate chain shown attached to the glycoprotein.
Sample response: The carbohydrate chain on the glycoprotein plays a role in cell recognition, allowing cells to identify and communicate with each other, such as during immune responses or tissue formation.
(c) Explain why cholesterol helps prevent the membrane from becoming too rigid at low temperatures.
Sample response: Cholesterol molecules are wedged between the phospholipids and prevent them from packing too closely together at low temperatures, helping to maintain the membrane's fluidity rather than allowing it to become rigid and lose function.
