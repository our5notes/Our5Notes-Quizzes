Short Answer Practice

5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Identify ONE core idea of Darwin's theory of natural selection.

Sample response: Darwin's theory of natural selection argued that species change over time as individuals with advantageous inherited traits are more likely to survive and reproduce.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that Social Darwinism differed from Darwin's own scientific theory.

Sample response: Darwin's theory was a scientific explanation of biological change in species with no claims about human social or racial superiority, while Social Darwinism was a social and political ideology that misapplied similar language to argue that certain human groups were naturally superior to others.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE way that Social Darwinism was used to justify European imperialism during this period.

Sample response: By framing imperial conquest and racial domination as a natural, inevitable result of struggle between "fit" and "unfit" groups, Social Darwinism gave European imperialists a pseudo-scientific justification for colonial expansion and the subjugation of colonized peoples.
