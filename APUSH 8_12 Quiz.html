Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the movement closely tied to youth counterculture that protested the Vietnam War.
Sample response: The antiwar movement, shown in the diagram, was closely tied to youth counterculture and protested the Vietnam War.
(b) Explain the connection between the youth counterculture and the antiwar movement.
Sample response: The youth counterculture's broader rejection of mainstream values, authority, and materialism aligned naturally with growing opposition to the Vietnam War, which many young Americans saw as an unjust conflict imposed by an older generation of political and military leaders they no longer trusted; college campuses became especially important sites of both cultural experimentation and antiwar organizing, making the counterculture and the antiwar movement deeply intertwined rather than entirely separate phenomena.
(c) Explain why the era's youth culture is often described as creating a significant “generation gap.”
Sample response: Many young Americans in this period embraced values, lifestyles, and political positions, including questioning of traditional authority, experimentation with drugs, changing sexual norms, and opposition to the Vietnam War, that directly conflicted with the values of their parents' generation, who had generally come of age during the more conformist atmosphere of the Depression, World War II, and the early Cold War; this stark values divide between generations, more pronounced than in many earlier periods, is what historians describe as the era's “generation gap.”
