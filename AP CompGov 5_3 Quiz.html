Political scientists note that although multiple parties legally compete in Mexican elections, MORENA's overwhelming electoral dominance in recent years has meant that the party's internal primary contests have, in practice, become more politically significant than the general election itself. Which statement best explains the significance of this pattern?

Short Answer Practice

5
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Identify ONE way that PRI maintained political control in Mexico for much of the twentieth century.

Sample response: The PRI maintained political control in part through a corporatist structure that integrated major interest groups, such as labor unions, directly into the party itself.
6
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain ONE difference between the PAN and the PRD in terms of their political and economic positions.

Sample response: The PAN, based largely in northern Mexico, has generally favored market-oriented economic reforms and ties to the Catholic Church, while the PRD has generally favored a more interventionist, socially progressive approach.
7
Mark for Review
Short Answer

Explain why Mexico is often described as a "one-party dominant" system despite allowing multiple parties to legally compete.

Sample response: Even though multiple parties are legally permitted to compete, one party, first the PRI and more recently MORENA, has achieved such overwhelming electoral dominance that meaningful competition for power has often been concentrated within that single dominant party.
