Free Response

Use the graph of x=y² and x=y+2 shown above.

(a) Confirm the intersection points algebraically (solving for y).
Sample response: y²=y+2 → y²-y-2=0 → (y-2)(y+1)=0 → y=-1, y=2.
(b) Set up and evaluate the area between the curves, integrating with respect to y.
Sample response: A = ∫(-1 to 2)[(y+2)-y²]dy = 4.5 (the same value as the x=x²/y=x+2 region, since it's the same curves with x and y roles swapped).
(c) Explain one advantage of integrating with respect to y instead of x for this particular region.
Sample response: Since x=y² cannot be easily solved for a single y(x) function without splitting into upper and lower branches, describing the region as functions of y avoids this complication entirely.
