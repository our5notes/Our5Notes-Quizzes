Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify one new institution that fueled the era's consumer culture.
Sample response: Department stores, shown in the diagram, fueled the era's consumer culture.
(b) Explain the connection between industrial growth and the rise of a new middle class.
Sample response: As large industrial corporations grew, they required an expanding layer of managers, clerks, accountants, and other white-collar professionals to coordinate their increasingly complex operations, creating new job opportunities distinct from the industrial factory labor most associated with this era; these new white-collar workers, along with independent professionals, formed a growing urban middle class with more disposable income and different social aspirations than either wealthy industrialists or factory laborers.
(c) Explain the significance of the middle class's involvement in the era's reform movements.
Sample response: The growing middle class, having benefited from industrial growth without necessarily identifying with the excesses of wealthy industrialists, often had both the education and the economic security to engage in social reform efforts; middle-class reformers, including many women, provided crucial energy, funding, and leadership for movements addressing issues like immigrant welfare, temperance, and political corruption, making the middle class a significant force in shaping the era's reform agenda.
