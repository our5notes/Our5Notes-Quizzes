Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which moth color had a survival advantage after industrial pollution, based on the diagram shown.
Sample response: The dark-colored moth had a survival advantage after industrial pollution darkened the tree bark, shown in the diagram.
(b) Explain why Lamarck's theory of inheritance of acquired characteristics is now known to be incorrect.
Sample response: Modern genetics shows that only changes to an organism's germline DNA can be passed to offspring; traits acquired during an organism's lifetime do not alter the DNA in gametes, so they cannot be inherited.
(c) Explain the key difference between Darwin's theory of natural selection and Lamarck's theory.
Sample response: Darwin's theory proposes that variation already exists within a population, and the environment selects for individuals with advantageous existing traits; Lamarck's theory instead proposed that individuals actively change their own traits during their lifetime and pass on those acquired changes.
