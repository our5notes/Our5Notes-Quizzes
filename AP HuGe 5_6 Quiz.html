Easy

Which factor most directly determines the type of commercial agriculture practiced in a given world region?

B. The type of commercial agriculture practiced in a region is most directly shaped by a combination of climate, soil, and market access.
2
Mark for Review
Medium

Plantation agriculture, which produces cash crops like coffee, sugar, and bananas for export using large-scale labor, is most commonly found in which climate zone?

B. Plantation agriculture producing cash crops like coffee, sugar, and bananas is most common in tropical and subtropical climates.
3
Mark for Review
Medium

Regions with a distinctive climate of hot, dry summers and mild, wet winters, such as parts of California, southern Europe, and Chile, are well suited for which type of specialized agriculture?

A. Mediterranean climate regions, with hot dry summers and mild wet winters, are well suited to Mediterranean agriculture, such as grapes, olives, and citrus.

Hard

A large, relatively flat region with fertile soil and a temperate climate, located far from major coastal population centers, has developed into a major producer of wheat and other grains grown primarily for regional and international commercial sale, using extensive, highly mechanized farming methods. Which type of agricultural production region does this best describe?

B. This scenario describes commercial grain farming, characterized by extensive, mechanized production of grain crops for regional and international sale.

A large, flat region with fertile soil and a temperate climate, located far inland from major cities, has become a major global exporter of wheat, produced using extensive, highly mechanized farming methods on very large landholdings.

AIdentify the type of agricultural production region best represented by this scenario.

Sample response: This scenario describes a commercial grain farming region.

BExplain how the region's climate and soil conditions support this type of agriculture.

Sample response: The region's fertile soil and temperate climate provide favorable growing conditions for large-scale wheat production over extensive areas of land.

CExplain why this type of agriculture typically relies on extensive rather than intensive farming methods.

Sample response: Because land in this inland region is relatively cheap and abundant compared to land near urban markets, farmers can profitably use extensive methods across large landholdings rather than investing heavily in intensive, small-scale production.
