Which of the following best explains why expanding women's participation in the paid workforce tends to lower fertility rates?

B. Workforce participation often leads women to delay childbearing and have fewer children overall, lowering total fertility rates.

Medium

A country that expands women's access to reproductive healthcare and family planning resources would most likely see which demographic change?

B. Greater access to family planning resources typically allows women more control over childbearing, generally lowering total fertility rates.

Hard

Two countries have similar GDP per capita, but Country X has much higher rates of female secondary education and workforce participation than Country Y. Which outcome would a geographer most likely predict based on demographic patterns associated with women's empowerment?

B. Higher female education and workforce participation are strongly associated with lower total fertility rates, even when controlling for similar overall wealth.

Free Response
Over the past 20 years, Country S has significantly expanded access to secondary and higher education for girls and women, alongside expanding women's participation in the formal workforce.

AExplain the likely effect of these changes on Country S's total fertility rate.

Sample response: Country S's total fertility rate is likely to decline as more women pursue education and careers, often delaying marriage and childbearing and choosing to have fewer children.

BExplain one reason increased female workforce participation tends to delay the average age of first childbirth.

Sample response: Women who are building careers often prioritize job stability and income before starting a family, pushing the average age of first childbirth later.

CExplain how these demographic changes might affect Country S's future population pyramid shape.

Sample response: As fertility declines, Country S's population pyramid is likely to shift from an expansive shape toward a more constrictive or stationary shape over time.
