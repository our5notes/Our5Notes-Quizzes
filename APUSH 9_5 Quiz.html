Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the group that became the nation's largest minority group.
Sample response: Hispanic Americans, shown in the diagram, became the nation's largest minority group during this period.
(b) Explain how the 1965 Immigration Act's legacy shaped immigration patterns in the 1990s and 2000s.
Sample response: By eliminating the discriminatory national-origin quota system, the 1965 Immigration Act opened the door to significantly increased immigration from Latin America and Asia; this shift, whose effects grew more pronounced over subsequent decades, meant that by the 1990s and 2000s, immigration to the U.S. increasingly came from these regions rather than Europe, fundamentally reshaping the nation's demographic and cultural composition compared to earlier in the 20th century.
(c) Explain why immigration policy became an increasingly contentious political debate in this period.
Sample response: As both legal and illegal immigration increased significantly, debates intensified over how to balance economic benefits of immigration (such as filling labor needs) against concerns about job competition, cultural change, and the rule of law regarding undocumented immigration; these debates became increasingly politically polarized, with disagreements over border security, pathways to legal status, and the broader question of how immigration was reshaping American identity and culture.
