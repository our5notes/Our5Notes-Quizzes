A region contains one very large capital city that is more than four times larger than the country's second-biggest city, followed by several much smaller towns offering only basic goods and services like gas stations and grocery stores.

AIdentify whether this region's largest city functions as a primate city, and justify your answer using population data from the scenario.

Sample response: Yes, this region's largest city functions as a primate city, since it is more than twice, in this case more than four times, the size of the second-largest city in the country.

BExplain the concept of threshold using the small towns' gas stations and grocery stores as an example.

Sample response: Threshold refers to the minimum population or customer base needed to support a good or service; gas stations and grocery stores have a relatively low threshold, meaning even small towns have enough customers to support them.

CExplain why a large, specialized service, such as a major hospital or international airport, would likely be found only in the capital city rather than in the smaller towns.

Sample response: Specialized services like a major hospital or international airport have a very high threshold, requiring a large population base to remain viable, which only the large capital city, not the smaller towns, can provide.
