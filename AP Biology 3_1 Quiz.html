Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what the diagram illustrates about the relationship between an enzyme and its substrate.
Sample response: The diagram illustrates the induced fit model, showing the enzyme's active site changing shape slightly as it binds its substrate.
(b) Explain why the induced fit model differs from a simple “lock and key” model.
Sample response: In the lock-and-key model, the enzyme's active site is imagined as a rigid, pre-formed shape that fits its substrate exactly; the induced fit model instead shows the active site as flexible, molding around the substrate as it binds, rather than being a fixed match beforehand.
(c) Predict what would happen to an enzyme's function if a mutation altered the shape of its active site, and explain your reasoning.
Sample response: If a mutation altered the shape of the enzyme's active site, the enzyme would likely have reduced or eliminated ability to bind its substrate properly, since binding depends on the active site's shape being complementary to the substrate, decreasing or destroying the enzyme's catalytic function.
