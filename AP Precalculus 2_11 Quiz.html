Use the graph of f(x)=log₃(x) shown above.

(a) State the vertical asymptote, domain, and range.
Sample response: Vertical asymptote: x=0. Domain: x>0. Range: all real numbers.
(b) Describe the transformations applied to y=log(x) to produce h(x)=3log(x+2)−1.
Sample response: Vertical stretch by a factor of 3, horizontal shift left 2, vertical shift down 1.
(c) Without graphing, determine whether k(x)=log₀.₂(x) is increasing or decreasing, and state its vertical asymptote.
Sample response: Since the base 0.2 is between 0 and 1, k is decreasing. Vertical asymptote at x=0.
