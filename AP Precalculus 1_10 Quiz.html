Free Response

Use the graph of f(x) = (x − 1)(x + 3)/(x + 3) shown above.

(a) State the location of the hole shown, as a coordinate point.
Sample response: (−3, −4).
(b) Find the hole (if any) in g(x)=(x²−36)/(x−6), expressed as a coordinate point.
Sample response: Factor: (x−6)(x+6)/(x−6) = x+6 for x≠6. Hole at x=6, y=6+6=12. Hole at (6, 12).
(c) Determine whether h(x)=(x²−4)/(x+2) has a hole or a vertical asymptote at x=−2, and find the relevant coordinate.
Sample response: Factor: (x−2)(x+2)/(x+2) = x−2 for x≠−2. This is a HOLE (the factor cancels), at x=−2, y=−2−2=−4. Hole at (−2, −4).
