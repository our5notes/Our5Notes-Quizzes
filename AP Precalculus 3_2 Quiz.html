(a) Convert 5π/3 radians to degrees, and convert 225° to radians.
Sample response: 5π/3×(180/π) = 300°. 225×(π/180) = 5π/4.
(b) Using the unit circle, find sin(2π/3) and cos(2π/3) exactly.
Sample response: sin(2π/3) = √3/2, cos(2π/3) = −1/2.
(c) Find tan(π/6) exactly, using your knowledge of sin(π/6) and cos(π/6).
