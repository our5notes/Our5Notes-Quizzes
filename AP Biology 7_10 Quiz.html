Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what causes the initial separation of the population shown in the diagram.
Sample response: A geographic barrier, shown in the diagram, causes the initial separation of the population.
(b) Explain the general process of allopatric speciation, using the diagram shown.
Sample response: The geographic barrier divides the population into two groups, preventing gene flow between them; over many generations, each isolated population accumulates different mutations and experiences different selective pressures, causing genetic divergence, and eventually reproductive isolation may evolve, resulting in two separate species.
(c) Explain why reproductive isolation is considered the key criterion for determining that speciation has occurred.
Sample response: Under the biological species concept, a species is defined as a group that can interbreed and produce fertile offspring; physical differences alone don't necessarily prevent interbreeding, so reproductive isolation is used as the key criterion, since it marks the point of true genetic independence.
