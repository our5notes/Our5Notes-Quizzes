Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which row in the diagram shows a substitution (point) mutation.
Sample response: The second row of the diagram, showing one base changed, illustrates a substitution mutation.
(b) Explain why a silent mutation typically has no effect on the resulting protein.
Sample response: A silent mutation changes a nucleotide in a way that still codes for the same amino acid, due to the redundancy of the genetic code, so the protein's amino acid sequence and function remain unaffected.
(c) Explain why an insertion or deletion of one nucleotide causes a frameshift, while an insertion or deletion of three nucleotides typically does not.
Sample response: The ribosome reads mRNA in consecutive groups of three nucleotides (codons); inserting or deleting one nucleotide shifts every subsequent codon's grouping, altering the reading frame, while inserting or deleting three nucleotides adds or removes one whole codon without shifting the grouping of the remaining sequence.
