Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what changed in the population's gene pool between the two generations shown.
Sample response: The relative frequency of the two alleles shown changed between generation 1 and generation 20.
(b) Explain the difference between the bottleneck effect and the founder effect.
Sample response: The bottleneck effect occurs when a population's size is drastically reduced, leaving a small surviving population whose allele frequencies may not reflect the original population; the founder effect occurs when a small group becomes isolated from a larger population and establishes a new population with limited genetic variation.
(c) Explain why genetic drift has a stronger effect on small populations than large ones.
Sample response: In a small population, random chance events have a much larger proportional impact on allele frequencies, since each individual represents a larger fraction of the gene pool; in a large population, random events tend to average out, keeping allele frequencies more stable.
