Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the group of painters that celebrated the American landscape.
Sample response: The Hudson River School, shown in the diagram, celebrated the grandeur of the American landscape.
(b) Explain how Transcendentalism reflected broader trends of this era, such as expanding democracy and individualism.
Sample response: Transcendentalism's emphasis on individual intuition and self-reliance over established authority and social convention reflected the era's broader democratic spirit, which similarly emphasized the value and judgment of the individual over inherited hierarchy; this philosophical movement paralleled political trends like expanding suffrage, both reflecting a growing cultural confidence in the judgment of the individual citizen.
(c) Explain why the development of a distinct American culture mattered for the young nation's identity.
Sample response: Developing distinctly American art, literature, and philosophy helped the United States establish a cultural identity independent from Britain and Europe, reinforcing the sense that America was not merely a former colony but a unique nation with its own values and creative traditions; this cultural development complemented the political independence won decades earlier, helping to build a more complete sense of American national identity.
