Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the labor system that dominated Chesapeake agriculture before the shift to chattel slavery.
Sample response: Indentured servitude, shown in the diagram, dominated Chesapeake agriculture before the shift to chattel slavery.
(b) Explain how Bacon's Rebellion influenced the colonial shift toward chattel slavery.
Sample response: Bacon's Rebellion demonstrated the danger of relying on a large population of poor, discontented former indentured servants who could unite (even across racial lines) against colonial elites; in response, Virginia's planter elite increasingly favored the permanent, hereditary labor of enslaved Africans instead, reasoning that lifetime enslavement (unlike temporary indentured servitude) would not produce a growing class of free, land-hungry, potentially rebellious laborers.
(c) Explain the significance of slave codes in shaping the legal and social status of enslaved people.
Sample response: Slave codes formally established slavery as a permanent, hereditary, race-based institution, meaning enslaved status would automatically pass to a person's children and last for their entire lifetime, unlike indentured servitude; these codes also stripped enslaved people of legal rights and protections, providing a legal foundation that would keep chattel slavery entrenched in colonial society for generations.
