Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1825 canal that connected the Great Lakes to the Hudson River.
Sample response: The Erie Canal, shown in the diagram, connected the Great Lakes to the Hudson River in 1825.
(b) Explain how the transportation revolution contributed to the growth of a national market economy.
Sample response: Improvements like the Erie Canal and early railroads dramatically reduced the time and cost of transporting goods over long distances, making it newly profitable for farmers and manufacturers to sell their products in distant markets rather than only locally; this increased economic interconnection tied previously isolated regional economies together into a single national market, accelerating economic growth and regional specialization.
(c) Explain how the Market Revolution contributed to growing economic specialization between the North and South.
Sample response: As transportation and market connections improved, the North increasingly specialized in manufacturing and commerce, supported by its growing industrial base, while the South increasingly specialized in cotton production for these expanding markets, deepening its reliance on enslaved labor; this growing economic specialization reinforced regional differences that would later contribute to sectional political conflict.
