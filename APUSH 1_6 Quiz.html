Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which pattern of interaction shown in the diagram involves the blending of Native and European religious practices.
Sample response: Syncretism, shown in the diagram, involves the blending of Native and European religious practices.
(b) Explain why some Native groups chose to ally with European conquistadors against other Native peoples.
Sample response: Some Native groups, such as the Tlaxcala, had long-standing grievances and rivalries with dominant powers like the Aztec Empire (which demanded tribute and captives from subordinate groups); these groups saw an alliance with the Spanish as an opportunity to weaken or overthrow their Aztec rivals, not realizing that this would ultimately also lead to their own subjugation under Spanish colonial rule.
(c) Explain how religious syncretism in the Spanish colonies reflects a broader pattern of cultural interaction in this period.
Sample response: Religious syncretism, such as the blending of Catholic saints with existing Native deities or practices, reflects how cultural interaction in this period was rarely a simple one-way imposition of European culture; instead, Native and African peoples actively adapted, reinterpreted, and blended new religious and cultural elements with their own existing traditions, creating genuinely new hybrid cultural forms rather than pure replacement.
