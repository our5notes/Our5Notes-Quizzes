Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the Soviet leader who engaged in reform and diplomacy with Reagan.
Sample response: Mikhail Gorbachev, shown in the timeline, engaged in reform and diplomacy with Reagan.
(b) Explain how the combination of Reagan's military buildup and diplomacy contributed to the end of the Cold War.
Sample response: Reagan's massive military buildup, including proposed programs like the Strategic Defense Initiative, placed enormous economic strain on the already struggling Soviet economy, which could not afford to keep pace with this new arms race; at the same time, Reagan's direct diplomatic engagement with reform-minded Soviet leader Gorbachev opened channels for meaningful arms control agreements and reduced tensions, showing how military pressure and diplomatic engagement worked together to help bring the Cold War to a peaceful conclusion.
(c) Explain the significance of the U.S. becoming the world's “sole superpower” after the Cold War's end.
Sample response: With the collapse of the Soviet Union, the U.S. no longer faced a rival global power of comparable military and ideological strength, fundamentally changing the structure of international relations that had defined the previous four decades; this new position brought both new opportunities for U.S. global leadership and new challenges, as the country had to redefine its foreign policy goals and role in the world without the organizing framework of Cold War competition that had shaped policy since 1945.
