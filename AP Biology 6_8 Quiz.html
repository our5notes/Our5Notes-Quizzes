Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify which lane in the diagram most likely represents the DNA ladder (size standard).
Sample response: The leftmost lane, showing evenly spaced bands, most likely represents the DNA ladder used as a size standard.
(b) Explain why smaller DNA fragments migrate farther through the gel than larger fragments in the same amount of time.
Sample response: The gel material acts like a molecular sieve; smaller DNA fragments move through the gel's pores more easily and quickly, allowing them to travel farther in a given amount of time, while larger fragments are slowed more by the gel matrix.
(c) Explain how gel electrophoresis could be used to compare DNA fragment sizes between different samples.
Sample response: By running multiple DNA samples in separate lanes alongside a DNA ladder of known sizes, researchers can compare the migration distance of each sample's bands to the ladder, allowing them to estimate and compare the sizes of DNA fragments across different samples.
