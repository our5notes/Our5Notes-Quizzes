Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Identify which event shown occurred closest to 3.5 billion years ago.
Sample response: The appearance of the first true cells, shown in the diagram, occurred closest to 3.5 billion years ago.
(b) Explain why the RNA world hypothesis is considered a compelling explanation for the origin of the first genetic material.
Sample response: Unlike DNA, which typically cannot catalyze reactions, and proteins, which typically cannot store heritable information, RNA can both store genetic information and catalyze chemical reactions as a ribozyme, allowing a single molecule to perform both essential functions of early life.
(c) Explain the concept of a protobiont, and why its formation was an important step toward true cells.
Sample response: A protobiont is a simple, cell-like structure, such as a membrane-bound vesicle, that exhibits some properties of life but is not yet a true cell; its formation was important because it allowed self-replicating molecules to be concentrated and protected within a boundary, setting the stage for true cellular life.
