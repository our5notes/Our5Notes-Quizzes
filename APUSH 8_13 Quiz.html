Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the 1970 federal agency created to enforce environmental regulations.
Sample response: The EPA, shown in the diagram, was created in 1970 to enforce environmental regulations.
(b) Explain how Silent Spring contributed to the rise of the modern environmental movement.
Sample response: By presenting detailed scientific evidence of the harmful effects of pesticides like DDT on wildlife and ecosystems, Silent Spring challenged the previously widespread assumption that chemical pesticides were an unambiguously beneficial technological achievement; the book's significant public impact raised broad awareness of environmental harm and helped build the public concern and political pressure that would lead to the first Earth Day and the creation of the EPA less than a decade later.
(c) Explain how the 1970s energy crisis connected environmental and foreign policy concerns.
Sample response: The 1970s energy crisis, triggered partly by an Arab oil embargo in response to U.S. support for Israel, revealed America's heavy dependence on foreign oil imports, causing gas shortages and price spikes that directly affected millions of Americans; this crisis linked environmental and energy policy debates (about conserving resources and developing alternative energy) directly to foreign policy concerns (about reducing dependence on politically volatile oil-producing regions), showing how domestic and international issues were often deeply intertwined in this period.
