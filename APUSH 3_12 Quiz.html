Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify what the Northwest Ordinance banned in the territory it organized.
Sample response: The Northwest Ordinance, shown in the diagram, banned slavery in the territory it organized.
(b) Explain why westward migration in the early republic intensified conflict with Native nations.
Sample response: As growing numbers of American settlers moved into the Northwest Territory seeking land, they increasingly encroached on territory that Native nations depended on and considered their own; this competition for land led to escalating conflict, as Native nations resisted this encroachment while the U.S. government, often backing settler expansion, sought to negotiate or force new land cessions through treaties.
(c) Explain the significance of the Northwest Ordinance's ban on slavery in the territory it organized.
Sample response: The Northwest Ordinance's ban on slavery in the Northwest Territory established an early legal precedent distinguishing free and slave territory, a distinction that would become increasingly significant (and contentious) as the nation continued to expand westward in the decades that followed, foreshadowing later sectional conflicts over the expansion of slavery into new territories.
