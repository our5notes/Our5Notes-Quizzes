For the region between y=x² and y=x+2, a student sets up ∫(-1 to 2)[x²-(x+2)]dx instead of ∫(-1 to 2)[(x+2)-x²]dx. What is the consequence?

B. Reversing top and bottom in the subtraction produces a negative value, since it's now bottom-minus-top.

Use the graph of y=x² and y=x+2 shown above.

(a) Confirm the intersection points algebraically.
Sample response: x²=x+2 → x²-x-2=0 → (x-2)(x+1)=0 → x=-1, x=2.
(b) Set up and evaluate the area between the curves.
Sample response: A = ∫(-1 to 2)[(x+2)-x²]dx = [x²/2+2x-x³/3](-1 to 2) = 4.5.
(c) Find the area between y=4-x² and y=x+2 (find new intersection points first).
Sample response: 4-x²=x+2 → x²+x-2=0 → (x+2)(x-1)=0 → x=-2,1. A = ∫(-2 to 1)[(4-x²)-(x+2)]dx = 4.5.
