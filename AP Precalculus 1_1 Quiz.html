A city's population data shows the population rising every year from 2010–2015, then falling every year from 2015–2020. Over which years is the population increasing?

B. The population rises (increases) specifically during 2010–2015.

A student examines a table showing f(x) values at x=1,2,3,4,5 as 8,5,5,5,9 and claims f is "decreasing then increasing." Is this fully accurate?

B. A careful reading of the table reveals a constant middle interval that a simplified description misses.

The table shows a company's daily profit over one week.

(a) Over which day-interval(s) is profit increasing? Day: 1,2,3,4,5,6,7; Profit ($): 120,180,220,220,190,150,100.
Sample response: Profit is increasing from day 1 to day 3.
(b) Over which day-interval(s) is profit decreasing?
Sample response: Profit is decreasing from day 4 to day 7 (day 3 to day 4 is constant).
(c) Explain what it means, in this context, for profit to "change in tandem" with day number over the interval [1,3].
Sample response: It means that as the day number increases over [1,3], profit also consistently increases — the two quantities move in the same direction together.
