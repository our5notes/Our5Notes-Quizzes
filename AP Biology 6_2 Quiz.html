Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the enzyme shown that unwinds the DNA double helix at the replication fork.
Sample response: Helicase, shown at the replication fork, unwinds the DNA double helix.
(b) Explain why DNA replication produces one leading strand and one lagging strand.
Sample response: Because DNA polymerase can only add nucleotides in the 5' to 3' direction, and the two template strands run antiparallel, one new strand is synthesized continuously in the direction the fork opens (the leading strand), while the other must be synthesized discontinuously in short fragments (the lagging strand).
(c) Explain the experimental significance of the Meselson-Stahl experiment in confirming semiconservative replication.
Sample response: The Meselson-Stahl experiment used labeled nitrogen isotopes to track DNA through replication rounds; the resulting density patterns matched the semiconservative model's predictions but not the conservative or dispersive models, providing strong evidence that each new DNA molecule contains one old and one new strand.
