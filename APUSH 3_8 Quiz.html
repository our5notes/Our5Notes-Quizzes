Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the two competing plans reconciled by the Great Compromise.
Sample response: The Virginia Plan and the New Jersey Plan, shown in the diagram, were reconciled by the Great Compromise.
(b) Explain how the Great Compromise resolved the disagreement between the Virginia Plan and the New Jersey Plan.
Sample response: The Virginia Plan (favoring large states) wanted representation based on population, while the New Jersey Plan (favoring small states) wanted equal representation per state; the Great Compromise resolved this conflict by creating a bicameral Congress with both features — the House of Representatives based on population, satisfying large states, and the Senate with equal representation per state, satisfying small states.
(c) Explain the main concern that led Anti-Federalists to oppose ratifying the Constitution as originally written.
Sample response: Anti-Federalists feared that the new Constitution created a central government with too much power, potentially threatening individual liberties and state sovereignty in the same way British rule once had; their insistence on adding a Bill of Rights to explicitly protect individual freedoms was ultimately successful and became a condition for ratification in several states.
