Use the graph of the cardioid r=2+2cos(θ) shown above.

(a) Find the area enclosed by r=4cos(θ) for θ from -π/2 to π/2, and confirm it matches the known area formula for a circle of radius 2.
Sample response: A = (1/2)∫(-π/2 to π/2)(4cosθ)²dθ = 4π. This matches πr²=π(2²)=4π ✓.
(b) Find the area of one petal of r=3sin(2θ), for θ from 0 to π/2.
Sample response: A = (1/2)∫(0 to π/2)(3sin2θ)²dθ = 2.25∫(1-cos4θ)dθ = 2.25(π/2) = 1.125π ≈ 3.53.
(c) Find the total area enclosed by the cardioid r=2+2cos(θ), for θ from 0 to 2π.
Sample response: A = (1/2)∫(0 to 2π)(2+2cosθ)²dθ = (1/2)[4(2π)+0+4π] = 6π.
