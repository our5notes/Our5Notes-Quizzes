Free Response FRQ

Refer to the timeline shown above.

(a) Using the timeline shown, identify the process that made steel production cheap and abundant.
Sample response: The Bessemer process, shown in the timeline, made steel production cheap and abundant.
(b) Explain how the Bessemer process contributed to broader industrial and urban transformation.
Sample response: By dramatically reducing the cost and increasing the speed of steel production, the Bessemer process made steel abundant and affordable enough to use in large-scale construction; this enabled the building of taller buildings (skyscrapers), stronger bridges, and vastly expanded railroad networks, directly contributing to rapid urban growth and the broader industrial transformation of the American economy in this period.
(c) Explain how the spread of electricity changed both industry and everyday life in this period.
Sample response: The development of electric lighting and expanding electrical power grids allowed factories to operate for longer hours regardless of natural daylight, increasing industrial production; beyond the factory, electric lighting also transformed urban daily life, extending the hours available for commerce, entertainment, and social activity, and setting the foundation for even more transformative electrical technologies in the following decades.
