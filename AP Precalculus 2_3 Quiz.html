(c) MODEL: A bacteria culture starts with 300 cells and doubles every 4 hours. Write an exponential function P(t) for the population after t hours, and find the population after 12 hours.
Sample response: P(t) = 300(2)^(t/4). P(12) = 300(2)³ = 300(8) = 2400.
