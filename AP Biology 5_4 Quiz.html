Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagrams shown above.

(a) Identify which inheritance pattern shown produces a blended phenotype in heterozygotes.
Sample response: Incomplete dominance, shown in the diagram, produces a blended phenotype in heterozygotes.
(b) Explain the key difference between incomplete dominance and codominance.
Sample response: In incomplete dominance, the heterozygous phenotype is a blend of the two homozygous phenotypes; in codominance, the heterozygous phenotype shows both homozygous phenotypes fully and separately, without blending.
(c) Explain why linked genes do not follow Mendel's law of independent assortment.
Sample response: Linked genes are physically located on the same chromosome, so they tend to be inherited together as a unit rather than independently, since the whole chromosome, not individual genes, is what gets distributed during meiosis, unless separated by crossing over.
