Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify one item shown flowing from the Americas to the Old World, and one flowing from the Old World to the Americas.
Sample response: Maize flowed from the Americas to the Old World, while horses flowed from the Old World to the Americas, as shown in the diagram.
(b) Explain why European diseases had such a catastrophic effect on Native American populations, while Native diseases had comparatively little effect on Europeans.
Sample response: Native Americans had been isolated from Afro-Eurasian populations for thousands of years and had no prior exposure (and therefore no immunity) to diseases like smallpox and measles, which had circulated in Afro-Eurasia for centuries; Europeans, by contrast, had developed some immunity to these diseases through generations of exposure, so while the Americas did have some diseases to share, none had comparable devastating effects on European populations.
(c) Explain the connection between the demographic collapse of Native populations and the growth of African slavery in the Spanish colonies.
Sample response: The catastrophic decline of the Native American population (due to disease) created a severe labor shortage for Spanish colonizers, who needed workers for mines and plantations; as Native labor became scarce, Spanish colonizers increasingly turned to the forced labor of enslaved Africans to fill this gap, directly linking demographic collapse to the growth of the transatlantic slave trade.
