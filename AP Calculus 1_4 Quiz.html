A table can be used to _____ the value of a limit, but not necessarily prove it with full certainty.

Free Response

The table below gives values of g(x) near x=5: g(4.9)=11.8, g(4.99)=11.98, g(4.999)=11.998, g(5.001)=12.002, g(5.01)=12.02, g(5.1)=12.2.

(a) Estimate lim(x→5)g(x) based on the table.
Sample response: The table suggests lim(x→5)g(x) ≈ 12.
(b) Explain what feature of the table specifically supports your answer (referencing both sides).
Sample response: Both the left-approaching values (11.8, 11.98, 11.998) and right-approaching values (12.2, 12.02, 12.002) converge toward the same number, 12, from their respective sides.
(c) If instead the right-side values had approached 15 rather than 12, what would you conclude about the limit?
Sample response: The limit would not exist, since the one-sided estimates would disagree — 12 from the left and 15 from the right.
