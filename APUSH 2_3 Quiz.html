Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Using the diagram shown, identify the region located furthest north among the four shown.
Sample response: New England, shown at the top of the diagram, is the region located furthest north.
(b) Explain how New England's geography shaped its economy and society differently than the Chesapeake's geography did.
Sample response: New England's rocky soil and short growing season made large-scale cash-crop agriculture impractical, so colonists relied on fishing, shipbuilding, trade, and small subsistence farms organized around tight-knit towns; the Chesapeake's warmer climate and fertile soil, by contrast, were ideal for growing tobacco on a large scale, leading to a plantation-based economy dependent on a large, coerced labor force rather than New England's more self-sufficient family farms.
(c) Explain why the Lower South developed an enslaved majority population, unlike the other colonial regions.
Sample response: The Lower South's economy depended heavily on labor-intensive cash crops like rice, which required a large workforce to cultivate in harsh, disease-prone conditions; planters imported enslaved Africans (some with specific rice-cultivation expertise) in such large numbers to meet this labor demand that enslaved people eventually outnumbered the free population in some areas, unlike the other regions where free colonists remained the majority.
