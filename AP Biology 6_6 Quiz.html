Cells with identical DNA become specialized by expressing different subsets of their genes.
Question 1 Easy

What term describes different cell types expressing different subsets of the same genome?

ACell differentiation only
BDifferential gene expression
CGenetic recombination only
DAlternative splicing only
Differential gene expression describes different cell types expressing different genes from the same genome.
Question 2 Medium

Using the diagram shown, what remains identical between a neuron and a muscle cell in the same organism?

ATheir overall cell shape
BTheir DNA content overall
CTheir protein composition overall
DTheir specific cell function
Both cell types share the same DNA content, despite differing in shape, function, and protein composition.
Question 3 Medium

What is the process called by which unspecialized cells become specialized cell types?

ACell differentiation process
BCell replication process
CCell transduction process
DCell fermentation process
Cell differentiation is the process by which unspecialized cells become specialized.
Question 4 Hard

Using the diagram shown, explain why a muscle cell and a neuron have very different structures despite containing identical DNA.

AThey contain entirely different sets of genes in their genome
BThey express different subsets of genes from the same genome
CTheir DNA mutates differently as each cell type matures
DOne of the two cell types lacks a nucleus entirely
Differential expression of the same genome, not different genes, produces different cell structures and functions.
Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify what remains constant across the different cell types shown in the diagram.
Sample response: The same DNA (genome) remains constant across all the different cell types shown in the diagram.
(b) Explain why a muscle cell and a neuron have very different structures and functions despite sharing identical DNA.
Sample response: Although both cell types contain the same complete genome, they express different subsets of genes; the muscle cell expresses genes for contractile proteins, while the neuron expresses genes for electrical signaling, and this differential gene expression produces their very different structures and functions.
(c) Explain why understanding differential gene expression is important for stem cell research.
Sample response: Stem cells can differentiate into many cell types by turning on different sets of genes; understanding how this differential gene expression is controlled is essential for directing stem cells to become specific, medically useful cell types.
