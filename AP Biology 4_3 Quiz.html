Free Response FRQ

Refer to the diagram shown above.

(a) Identify the sequence of components shown in the diagram, from ligand binding to cellular response.
Sample response: The sequence shown is: ligand binds GPCR, GPCR activates a G protein, the G protein activates adenylyl cyclase, the enzyme produces cAMP, and cAMP triggers the cellular response.
(b) Explain how a phosphorylation cascade contributes to signal amplification.
Sample response: In a phosphorylation cascade, one activated kinase can phosphorylate multiple copies of the next kinase in the pathway, and each of those can activate multiple copies of the next, so the effect of the original signal multiplies rapidly at each step, producing a large final response.
(c) Explain the role of a second messenger, such as cAMP, in relaying a signal within the cell.
Sample response: A second messenger, like cAMP, is a small molecule produced inside the cell after the receptor is activated, and it relays the signal further by diffusing through the cytoplasm and activating additional downstream proteins, ultimately leading to the cell's response.
