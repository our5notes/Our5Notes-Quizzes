When centrifugal forces within a state significantly outweigh centripetal forces over a sustained period, the most likely long-term political consequence is:

B. When centrifugal (dividing) forces consistently outweigh centripetal (unifying) forces, the likely consequence is political instability or even state breakup.

A government's decision to grant a restless region greater autonomy and recognition of its distinct identity, in response to rising separatist sentiment, is an attempt to:

B. Granting greater autonomy is a common government strategy to address centrifugal pressure and potentially reinforce national unity.

Medium

The breakup of Yugoslavia into several new independent states in the 1990s is best understood as a consequence of:

B. Yugoslavia's breakup resulted from centrifugal forces, including ethnic, religious, and nationalist divisions, overwhelming the centripetal forces that had previously held the country together.

Hard

A country successfully manages decades of ethnic and regional diversity through strong centripetal forces, including a popular national symbol, shared national holidays, and a unifying national language policy, but a sudden economic collapse introduces severe new centrifugal pressure as different regions blame each other for the crisis. Which outcome is most likely if the government fails to respond effectively to this new economic centrifugal pressure?

B. Even strong existing centripetal forces are not guaranteed to remain dominant forever; a severe new centrifugal pressure, like an economic crisis, could shift the balance and increase instability if not effectively managed.

Free Response
A country with a long history of national unity, built on strong centripetal forces like a shared language and popular national celebrations, experiences a sudden economic crisis that triggers new regional resentment, with different regions blaming each other and the national government for the downturn.

AIdentify one centripetal force present in this country prior to the crisis, and explain how it historically helped maintain unity.

Sample response: The shared national language functioned as a centripetal force, giving citizens across different regions a common means of communication and a shared sense of national identity that helped maintain unity.

BExplain how the economic crisis has introduced a new centrifugal force into the country's political situation.

Sample response: The economic crisis has introduced regional blame and resentment as a new centrifugal force, as different regions turn against each other and the national government rather than remaining unified.

CExplain one policy response the national government could use to strengthen centripetal forces and offset this new centrifugal pressure.

Sample response: The national government could implement targeted economic relief programs and public communication emphasizing shared national sacrifice and recovery, aiming to rebuild unity and counteract the new regional resentment.
