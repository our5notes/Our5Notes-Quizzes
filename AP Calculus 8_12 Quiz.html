The region between y=√x and y=x/2 (from Topic 8.11), on [0,4], is revolved around the line y=−2 instead of the x-axis.

(a) Write the new outer radius R(x) and inner radius r(x).
Sample response: R(x) = √x + 2. r(x) = x/2 + 2.
(b) Set up the new volume integral (you do not need to fully evaluate by hand, but show the complete setup).
Sample response: V = π∫(0 to 4)[(√x+2)²-(x/2+2)²]dx.
(c) Explain why both radii increased compared to revolving around the x-axis, but the relationship between which is "outer" and which is "inner" stayed the same.
Sample response: Since both curves lie above y=-2 throughout the interval, shifting the axis downward increases the distance from each curve to the axis by the same fixed amount, which doesn't change which curve was originally farther away.
