Free Response

Use the polar curve r=3+2sin(θ) shown above.

(a) Convert the polar point (4,π/3) to rectangular coordinates.
Sample response: x = 4cos(π/3) = 2. y = 4sin(π/3) = 2√3. Rectangular point: (2, 2√3).
(b) Find dy/dx for r=3+2sin(θ) at θ=π/2.
Sample response: x(θ)=(3+2sinθ)cosθ, y(θ)=(3+2sinθ)sinθ. At θ=π/2: dx/dθ=-5, dy/dθ=0. dy/dx = 0/(-5) = 0 (a horizontal tangent).
(c) For the circle r=5 (constant), show that dy/dx=-cot(θ), and explain why this matches the fact that a tangent to a circle is perpendicular to its radius.
Sample response: x=5cosθ, y=5sinθ. dx/dθ=-5sinθ, dy/dθ=5cosθ. dy/dx=-cosθ/sinθ=-cotθ. The radius has slope tanθ, and -cotθ is its negative reciprocal, confirming the tangent is perpendicular to the radius.
