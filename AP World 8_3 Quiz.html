Hard

Historians note that the Cold War's effects extended well beyond direct US-Soviet relations, including a costly nuclear arms race, numerous proxy conflicts fought in regions such as Korea, Vietnam, and Angola, and the emergence of the Non-Aligned Movement among countries seeking to avoid full alignment with either superpower. Which statement best reflects the global scope of the Cold War's effects?

B. The Cold War significantly shaped global politics and conflict well beyond the two superpowers themselves, through proxy wars, the arms race, and movements like non-alignment.

Short Answer

Explain ONE effect the nuclear arms race had on international relations during the Cold War.

Sample response: The nuclear arms race between the United States and the Soviet Union heightened global tensions and the risk of catastrophic conflict, while also contributing to a strategy of mutual deterrence that helped prevent direct war between the two superpowers.

Explain ONE reason why some countries chose to join the Non-Aligned Movement rather than fully align with either Cold War superpower.

Sample response: Some countries, often newly independent from colonial rule, joined the Non-Aligned Movement to maintain independent foreign policies and avoid becoming entangled in superpower rivalry, preferring to pursue their own national interests.
